Companies hold everything about a client or target employer: contacts, jobs, placements, notes and billing details. Create the company before you start work for a new client, so all activity is tracked against it from day one.

## Create a company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Go to **Companies** and click **Add**. The **Create New Company** page opens.

    To add many companies at once, click the arrow next to **Add**, then click **Import CSV**. See [Bulk import](/candidates/bulk-import).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the basic details">
    **Company Name** is required and must be at least 2 characters. The other fields are optional unless your admin has made them required:

    - **Industries** and **Countries:** choose one or more of each.
    - **Status:** Active, Inactive, Prospect, Prospect - Cold, Prospect - Lead, Prospect - Negotiating, Signed or Signed & Paying.
    - **Website** and **LinkedIn URL**
    - **About:** a short description of the company.
    - **Sourced by:** the teammate who brought in the company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add billing details (optional)">
    Fill these in if you invoice the company:

    - **Billing Entity Name:** the legal entity you invoice.
    - **Tax ID / VAT Number**
    - **Billing Address**
    - **Billing Currencies:** one or more currencies you bill this company in.
    - **Payment Terms (Days):** for example 30, 60 or 90.
    - **Fee Terms:** your fee structure, for example "30% base".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in custom fields">
    If your admin has added custom fields for companies, they appear on the form. Admins manage them in **Settings → Field Configuration**. See [Custom fields](/advanced/custom-fields).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Company**. The company's record opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### If the company already exists

If the name, website or LinkedIn URL matches an existing company, Connekt shows **Duplicate Company Detected** instead of creating a new record. For each field that differs, choose the existing value or the one you just entered. Then click **Update Company** to update the existing company. No new company is created.

## After you create the company

The company record has tabs for **Notes**, **Activity**, **News**, **Invoice Instructions**, **Jobs**, **Contacts**, **Placements**, **Attachments**, **Tasks** and **Meetings**.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Add contacts" icon="user-plus" href="/companies/managing-contacts">
    On the **Contacts** tab, click **Add Contact**. The new contact is linked to this company.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Set a primary contact" icon="star" href="/companies/managing-contacts#set-a-primary-contact">
    Turn on **Primary Contact** on the contact you deal with most. The **Contacts** tab labels them **Primary**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Create jobs" icon="briefcase" href="/jobs/creating-a-job">
    On the **Jobs** tab, click **Add Job** to open a role for this company.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Record invoice instructions" icon="file-lines">
    Use the **Invoice Instructions** tab to note how this client wants to be invoiced.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Change the owner

Click the **⋯** menu in the company header, then click **Change Owner**. You need permission to change company ownership.

## Merge duplicate companies

If a company is in Connekt twice, click the **⋯** menu, then click **Merge Duplicate**. See [Merge duplicates](/candidates/merging-duplicates#merge-two-companies).

## Delete a company

Users with permission to delete companies can remove them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Open the company, click the **⋯** menu, then click **Delete Company**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Type `DELETE` in the box, then confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a company also deletes its contacts, jobs, submissions, placements, notes and attachments. You can't undo this in Connekt. If you only want to stop working with a client, change the company's **Status** to **Inactive** instead.
</Warning>
