Contacts are the people you work with at client companies: hiring managers, HR leads and anyone else involved in hiring. Keeping them up to date gives your whole team context on who to talk to and what's been discussed.

## Create a contact

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Go to **Contacts** and click **Add**. To add a contact for a specific company, open the company, go to the **Contacts** tab, and click **Add Contact** instead. The company is filled in for you.

    To add many contacts at once, click the arrow next to **Add**, then click **Import CSV**. See [Bulk import](/candidates/bulk-import).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the contact's details">
    Fill in the fields you need, such as **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Work Email**, **Personal Email**, **Job Title**, **Contact Stage**, **LinkedIn URL**, **Work Phone** and **Mobile Phone**. Your admin can make fields required in **Settings → Field Configuration**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link a company">
    In **Company Association**, choose the contact's **Company**. You can skip this and add it later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Contact**. The contact's record opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each email address can belong to only one contact. If a contact already uses the email, Connekt shows "Contact with this email already exists".
</Note>

You can also save contacts from LinkedIn with the Chrome extension. Use **Save as Contact** or **Save as Contact & Candidate**. See [Chrome extension](/integrations/chrome-extension).

## Link a contact to a company

To link or change the company later, open the contact, click **Edit**, choose the **Company**, and click **Update Contact**.

## Set a primary contact

Each company can have one primary contact: the main person you deal with there.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact's form">
    Open the contact and click **Edit**. Or turn it on while you create the contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Primary Contact">
    In **Company Association**, turn on **Primary Contact**. Make sure the contact is linked to a company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update Contact**, or **Create Contact** for a new contact.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The contact's record shows a **Primary Contact** badge, and the company's **Contacts** tab labels them **Primary**.

<Note>
  When you make someone the primary contact, Connekt removes the primary flag from the company's previous primary contact. You don't need to turn it off first.
</Note>

## Track contact stages

Use **Contact Stage** to track where each relationship stands:

- Not Available
- Prospect
- Prospect - Cold
- Prospect - Lead
- Prospect - Negotiating
- Signed
- Signed & Paying

Set the stage on the contact's form. On **Contacts**, you can filter the list by stage.

## Change the owner or delete a contact

Open the contact and click the **⋯** menu in the header. From there, you can transfer ownership or delete the contact, if your role allows it.
