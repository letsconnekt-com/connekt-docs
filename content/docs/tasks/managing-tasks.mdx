Tasks help you and your team keep track of follow-ups. You can link a task to candidates, jobs, companies, contacts and teammates, give it a due date and a reminder, and assign it to anyone in your workspace. To open the Tasks page, click **Tasks** in the sidebar, or press **G** then **T**.

## Create a task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Task dialog">
    On the **Tasks** page, click **New Task**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the task">
    Type what needs to be done in the title box. To link a record, type **@** and start typing a name. Pick a result from **Candidates**, **Jobs**, **Companies**, **Contacts** or **Team**. You can link as many records as you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the priority">
    Choose **Low**, **Medium** or **High** under **Priority**. New tasks start as **Medium**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add labels (optional)">
    Under **Labels**, click an existing label to add it. To create a new label, type it in **Add a label** and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a due date and reminder (optional)">
    Click **Pick a date** under **Due date**. To get a reminder, click **Set reminder** under **Remind** and choose a date and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the task">
    Under **Assign to**, choose a teammate. The task is assigned to you by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Task**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Mentioning a teammate with **@** links them to the task, but it doesn't assign the task to them. Use **Assign to** to choose who owns the task.
</Note>

Linked records appear as chips in the **Linked To** column. Click a chip to open that record. The task also appears on the **Tasks** tab of each linked candidate, contact, company and job.

## Task statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | The task hasn't been started. Every new task starts here. |
| **In Progress** | Someone is working on the task. |
| **Completed** | The task is done. |
| **Cancelled** | The task is no longer needed. Reminders aren't sent for cancelled tasks. |

## Find tasks

The Tasks page lists every task in your workspace. Use the filters above the list to narrow it down:

- **Status**: show one status, or **All Statuses**.
- **Assignee**: show tasks for one teammate. Choose **Me** to see your own tasks.
- **Priority**: show one priority, or **All Priorities**.
- **Search tasks...**: search by task title.

Click **Clear all** to reset the filters.

In the **Due Date** column, overdue dates show in red. Dates due within the next 24 hours show in amber.

## Edit, complete or delete a task

Click the **⋯** menu on a task row, then choose an option:

- **Edit** opens the **Edit Task** dialog. You can change the title, links, **Status**, priority, labels, dates and assignee. Click **Save Changes** when you're done.
- **Mark Complete** sets the status to **Completed**.
- **Delete** removes the task.

You can use the same menu on the **Tasks** tab of a candidate, contact, company or job.

<Warning>
  Deleting a task can't be undone.
</Warning>

## Other ways tasks are created

### From meetings

On a meeting's detail page, the **Tasks** card lets you add follow-ups from the call:

- Click **Suggest with AI**. Connekt reads the transcript and suggests follow-up tasks. Click **Add** on a suggestion, or select several and click **Add Selected**.
- Click **Add Task** to create a task yourself.

Tasks added here are linked to the meeting. See [Linking and AI actions](/meetings/linking-and-ai-actions).

### From Outreach sequences

Manual steps in an email sequence create tasks. These steps are **Manual LinkedIn message**, **Manual LinkedIn connection request**, **Manual phone call** and **Manual task**. When one of these steps runs, Connekt creates a task for the sequence owner, links it to the candidate or contact, and notifies the owner straight away. The task includes the message or instructions from the step. See [Email sequences](/outreach/email-sequences).

<Note>
  Connekt never sends anything on LinkedIn for you. Manual LinkedIn steps only create a task so you can send the message yourself.
</Note>

### From the Chrome extension

When you view a saved candidate, contact or company in the Chrome extension, open the **Tasks** tab and click **Add Task**. Enter a **Title**, and optionally a **Description**, **Priority**, **Due Date** and **Assignee**. If you leave **Assignee** empty, the task is assigned to you. See [Chrome extension](/integrations/chrome-extension).

## Task notifications

Connekt notifies you about your tasks in two ways:

- **Task assigned**: when someone assigns a task to you. You aren't notified when you assign a task to yourself, except for tasks created by an Outreach sequence.
- **Task reminders**: at the reminder time you set. The reminder goes to the assignee. If the task has no assignee, it goes to the person who created it.

Notifications appear on the **Notifications** page. To choose how you receive each type, go to **Settings → Notifications**. See [Notification preferences](/integrations/notification-preferences).

## Tasks on mobile

You can view, create and complete tasks in the Connekt mobile app. Go to **More → Tasks**. See [Using the mobile app](/mobile/using-the-mobile-app).
