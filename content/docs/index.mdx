## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your workspace">
    Follow your invite link, choose your plan, and add your company name and industry. See [Setting up your workspace](/getting-started/workspace-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Subscribe from **Settings → Plan** with a seat for each person, then go to **Settings → Manage Users** and invite colleagues with the right role. Your free trial covers one user. See [Inviting your team](/getting-started/inviting-your-team).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first records">
    Create a company, add a job, then add a candidate and submit them to the role. See the [Quick start guide](/getting-started/quick-start).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record your first placement">
    When a candidate accepts an offer, create a placement from the submission to track fees, splits, and invoicing. See [Converting a submission to a placement](/submissions/converting-to-placement).
  </Step>
</Steps>
