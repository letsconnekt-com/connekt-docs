The Connekt app can record in-person meetings, such as a coffee with a candidate or a client visit. When you save a recording, Connekt uploads it, transcribes it and writes an AI summary. You can then chat with the meeting, turn it into tasks, link it to records and share it.

The app records audio from your phone's microphone. It doesn't join online calls. To record Google Meet, Zoom or Teams calls, use the Notetaker on the web. See [Recording meetings](/meetings/recording-meetings).

<Warning>
  Recording laws vary by country and region, and some require every participant's consent. You're responsible for telling everyone present that the meeting is being recorded and getting consent where required.
</Warning>

## Record a meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Meetings">
    Go to **More → Meetings** and tap the microphone icon at the top of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow microphone access">
    The first time you record, tap **Enable Microphone** and allow access when your phone asks. If you declined before, tap **Open Settings** and turn on the microphone for Connekt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start recording">
    Tap **Start**. A timer and a sound-level wave show that the app is recording.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pause or resume if you need to">
    Tap **Pause** to pause the recording, and **Resume** to carry on. The paused time isn't recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the recording">
    When the meeting ends, tap **Save**. The app uploads the audio and starts the transcript.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Keep recording in the background

Once you start, the recording keeps going in the background. You can lock your phone or switch to another app. Come back to Connekt when you're ready to save.

### Discard a recording

Tap **Discard**, then confirm. The audio is deleted from your phone and nothing is saved to Connekt. If you tap the back arrow while recording, the app asks whether you want to discard the recording.

## Upload and transcription

After you tap **Save**, the app shows two stages:

1. **Saving recording…** The audio is uploading. Keep the app open until this finishes.
2. **Generating your transcript…** This can take a minute or two. You can leave this screen while Connekt finishes the meeting.

When processing finishes, you see **Meeting Recorded**. Tap **Done**.

New recordings are titled with the date and time they were made. The recorded time counts toward your workspace's notetaker hours. See [Usage and add-ons](/billing/usage-and-add-ons).

<Tip>
  If an upload fails, you see "Something went wrong finishing this recording. Your audio is safe on this device." Tap **Try Again** once you have a better connection.
</Tip>

## Review a meeting

Tap a meeting in **More → Meetings** to open it. Recordings made in the app show an audio player at the top. Meetings recorded on the web show the video. The meeting has four tabs.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Overview">
    Shows the **AI Summary**. The summary uses a summary template, and **General Notes** is the default.

    - Tap the template name to open **Summary Template** and choose another template. Templates marked **(Private)** are only visible to you.
    - Tap the refresh icon to regenerate the summary.

    You create and edit templates on the web. See [Summary templates](/meetings/summary-templates).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Transcript">
    Shows the full transcript by speaker. Tap **Copy** to copy it. Transcripts of in-app recordings don't show timestamps.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Chat">
    Ask questions about the transcript, summary, or decisions made in the meeting. Type in **Ask about this meeting…** and send. Tap **Clear chat** to start over.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tasks">
    Shows tasks linked to the meeting.

    - Tap **Suggest with AI** to get suggested tasks from the transcript. Keep the ones you want with **Add** or **Add Selected**, and dismiss the rest.
    - Tap **Add Task** to create a task yourself.

    AI suggestions need a transcript, so they're available once processing finishes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Share, link and manage access

Three icons in the top-right corner of a meeting let you share it, link it to records and choose who can see it.

<Note>
  Only the meeting organizer can create share links, link records and change access. The organizer is the person who recorded the meeting.
</Note>

### Share a link

Tap the share icon to open **Share Meeting**.

1. Turn on **Password protect link** if you want a password, and enter it.
2. Under **Link expires**, choose **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days** or **Never**. The default is 30 days.
3. Tap **Create Link**, then **Copy Link**.

Anyone with the link can view the meeting title, recording and transcript. **Link details** shows when the link expires, whether it has a password, how many views it has had and when it was last viewed. To stop sharing, tap **Revoke Link** and confirm. Anyone with the link loses access straight away.

### Control who can see the meeting

Tap the people icon to open **Manage Access**. Meetings are private to the organizer by default.

- Turn on **Visible to everyone in the org** to let anyone in your organisation view the meeting.
- Under **Add teammates**, search for people and tap **Add** to give them access.
- Remove someone from **Who has access** to take their access away.

For more on privacy, see [Sharing and privacy](/meetings/sharing-and-privacy).

### Link the meeting to records

Tap the link icon to open **Linked Records**. Choose **Candidate**, **Company**, **Contact** or **Job**, search for the record and tap it to link it. Remove a link from the linked list if you added the wrong record.

For more on linking and AI actions, see [Linking and AI actions](/meetings/linking-and-ai-actions).
