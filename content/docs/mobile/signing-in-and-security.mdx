You sign in to the Connekt app with the same account you use on the web. This page covers signing in, two-factor authentication (2FA), passwords, screenshot protection and signing out. For account security on the web, see [Security and 2FA](/account/security-and-2fa).

<Note>
  The app doesn't have a sign-up option. You need an existing Connekt account, either from creating a workspace on the web or from an invitation.
</Note>

## Sign in

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email and password">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Enter your details">
        Open the app and enter your **Email** and **Password**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Login">
        Tap **Login**. If your account has 2FA turned on, the app asks for a code next.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Tap Continue with Google">
        On the login screen, tap **Continue with Google**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose your Google account">
        Pick the Google account that matches your Connekt email. If your account has 2FA turned on, the app asks for a code next.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    Google sign-in only works for an email address that already has a Connekt account. It won't create a new account.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Enter your 2FA code

If you've turned on 2FA, you see a verification screen after you sign in. Open your authenticator app and enter the 6-digit code for Connekt. The app verifies it as soon as you enter all six digits, or you can tap **Verify**.

Signed in to the wrong account? Tap **Back to login**.

## Reset a forgotten password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Forgot password">
    On the login screen, tap **Forgot password?**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a link">
    Enter your email address and tap **Send reset link**. The app shows **Check your inbox**. The email can take a minute to arrive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Open the link in the email on your phone. Enter your new password and confirm it, then tap **Update password**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  New passwords set in the app must be at least 8 characters.
</Tip>

## Change your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security">
    Go to **More → View profile → Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Change Password">
    Enter your **Current Password**, a **New Password** of at least 8 characters and the same password again in **Confirm New Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Update Password**. You might be signed out and asked to sign in again with your new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Turn on two-factor authentication

2FA asks for a code from an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator, Authy or 1Password, each time you sign in. The **Security** screen shows **Enabled** or **Disabled** next to **Two-Factor Authentication**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open 2FA settings">
    Go to **More → View profile → Security** and tap **Two-Factor Authentication**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start setup">
    Tap **Set Up Authenticator App**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Connekt to your authenticator">
    Choose one of these options:

    - Tap **Open in Authenticator App** if the authenticator is on this phone.
    - Copy the key shown under **Or enter this setup key manually** and paste it into your authenticator.
    - Tap **Setting up on another device? Show QR code** and scan the code with the other device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Tap **Next — Enter Code**. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator and tap **Verify & Enable**. You see **Account Protected** when 2FA is on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Turn off two-factor authentication

Go to **More → View profile → Security → Two-Factor Authentication**. Tap **Disable 2FA**, then confirm with **Disable**.

<Warning>
  Turning off 2FA makes your account easier to break into. Only do it if you're setting it up again or your workspace doesn't need it.
</Warning>

## Screenshot protection

Screenshot protection stops people from capturing Connekt data on their phones. When it's on, screenshots and screen recordings of the app come out blank. This is expected, and it isn't a fault with your phone.

- It's an organisation-wide setting that applies to all members of your organisation.
- It's **on by default**.
- Only Super Administrators and Administrators can change it.

To turn it on or off in the app, go to **More → View profile → Security**. Use the **Screenshot Protection** switch under **Organisation Security**. Admins can also change it on the web under **Settings → Security**.

<Info>
  If you need a screenshot for a support request and you aren't an admin, ask your admin to turn off protection for a while. You can also describe the problem in the chat instead.
</Info>

## Sign out

Go to **More → View profile** and tap **Sign Out**, then confirm with **Sign out**.

Signing out only affects this device. You stay signed in on the web app and any other devices. This device also stops getting push notifications until you sign in again.

## Error messages

| Message | What it means and what to do |
|---|---|
| Please enter your email and password. | One of the fields is empty. Fill in both and try again. |
| No account found for this Google address. Please contact your administrator. | There's no Connekt account for that Google email. Sign in with the email you were invited with, or ask your admin to invite you. |
| Please enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. | The 2FA code is incomplete. Enter all six digits. |
| No MFA factor found. Please contact support. | Your account is waiting for a 2FA code, but no authenticator is set up. Contact support through [Getting help](/getting-started/getting-help). |
| Sign-in already in progress. | A Google sign-in is already open. Finish or close it, then try again. |
| Password must be at least 8 characters. | Choose a longer password. |
| Passwords do not match. | The new password and the confirmation are different. Type them again. |
| Incorrect password or an error occurred | Your current password is wrong, or the change couldn't be saved. Check your current password and try again. |
| Invalid code. Please check your authenticator app and try again. | The code you entered while setting up 2FA was wrong or expired. Enter the latest code from your authenticator. |

If you still can't sign in, see [Getting help](/getting-started/getting-help).
