Ask AI in the Connekt app is an AI candidate search. You describe who you're looking for in plain language, and it searches your candidate database and ranks the best matches. You can attach a job for context and add the candidates you like to a project. It works the same way as Ask AI on the web. See [Ask AI](/search/ask-ai).

## Start a search

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ask AI">
    Tap the round **Ask AI** button (the sparkles icon) above the tab bar. It's on most screens in the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a mode">
    Tap the mode button in the message box and choose **Fast**, **Auto** or **Deep**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach a job if you like">
    Tap the briefcase icon to open **Attach job context** and pick one or more open jobs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe who you need">
    Type your request in **Ask anything…** and send it. For example, "Senior backend engineers in London with Go and Kubernetes experience".
  </Step>
</Steps>

While Ask AI works, you see its progress, such as **Ranking candidates…**. To stop a response, tap the stop button.

<Info>
  If you see "Still indexing … records — results may be incomplete for a few minutes", Connekt is still processing recently added candidates. Try again shortly to include them.
</Info>

## Choose a mode

| Mode | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Fast** | Quick answers, using a lighter model |
| **Auto** | Balanced speed and depth. This is the default. |
| **Deep** | The most thorough reasoning. It's slower. |

Each chat remembers the last mode you used.

## Attach a job

Attaching a job gives Ask AI the role's details, so you don't have to type them. In **Attach job context**, search your open jobs with **Search open jobs…** and tap each job you want. Attached jobs show as chips above the message box. Tap the **x** on a chip to remove it.

Only jobs with the **Open** status appear in the list.

## Review the results

When the search finishes, the reply includes a card showing the number of **Ranked Candidates**. Tap the card to open the results.

Each ranked candidate shows a match score and, where available, **Match**, **Highlights**, **Experience**, **Education**, **Skills**, **Salary**, **Flags** and **Projects**. Tap **See more** to expand a section. Below the ranked list, you might also see more matches that scored lower.

Tap a candidate to open their profile.

## Add candidates to a project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate menu">
    In the results, tap the three-dot menu on a candidate and choose **Add to Project**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a project">
    Choose a project from **Project**. You can search with **Search projects...**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them">
    Tap **Add to Project**. The app confirms that the candidate was added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Create a project

If the project doesn't exist yet, choose **Create new project** in the project list. In **Create New Project**:

1. Enter a **Project Name**. This is the only required field.
2. Add a **Description** if you like.
3. Optionally pick a **Company** and then a **Job Position** at that company.
4. Choose the **Visibility**:
   - **Private**: only you can see and edit the project.
   - **Team**: all team members can see and edit it.
   - **Specific People**: only you and the users you pick in **Select Users** can access it.
5. Tap **Create Project**.

Connekt creates the project and adds the candidate to it straight away. To browse and manage projects, use the web app. See [Creating a project](/projects/creating-a-project).

## Chat history

Your searches are saved as chats.

- Tap the chat history icon to open **Chats** and go back to an earlier search.
- Tap the new chat icon to start a fresh search.
- In **Chats**, tap the menu next to a chat to **Rename** it or **Delete** it. You can't undo a deletion.
