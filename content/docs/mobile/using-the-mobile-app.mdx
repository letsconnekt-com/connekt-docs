The Connekt mobile app lets you keep working when you're away from your desk. You sign in with your existing Connekt account and see the same workspace data as the web app. The app is built for quick work on the go: looking up records, making edits, recording in-person meetings, signing documents and handling tasks and time off. Workspace setup and admin still happen in the web app.

## Get the app

The iOS app is live on the App Store. The Android app is coming soon.

| Platform | Availability | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| iOS | [Connekt CRM on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/connekt-crm/id6759556691) | iOS 15.1 or later, on iPhone or iPad |
| Android | Coming soon | — |

You can also find the App Store link in the web app under **Settings → Downloads & Guides**.

<Note>
  You can't create an account or a workspace in the app. Sign up on the web first, or ask your admin to invite you. See [Inviting your team](/getting-started/inviting-your-team).
</Note>

## Find your way around

The tab bar at the bottom of the screen has four tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Home** | A greeting, your goal progress and your metrics for the **Week**, **Month**, **Quarter** or **Year**. The bell icon opens your notifications, and your avatar opens your profile. |
| **Candidates** | Your candidate list with search. |
| **Companies** | Your company list with search. |
| **More** | Your profile card and a grid of everything else. |

The round **Ask AI** button (the sparkles icon) floats above the tab bar on most screens. It opens AI candidate search. See [AI candidate search on mobile](/mobile/ai-search).

The **More** grid holds these items:

- **Meetings**
- **Tasks**
- **Contacts**
- **Jobs**
- **eSign**
- **Deals**
- **Placements**
- **Time Off**
- **Help & Support**

<Info>
  What you see depends on your role and your plan. For example, **Candidates**, **Companies**, **Contacts** and **Jobs** only appear if your role can view them. **Time Off** needs the Team plan or higher. **eSign** only appears when your plan includes it.
</Info>

## What you can do in the app

In most areas you can view, create and edit. Deleting records is web only.

| Area | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Candidates** | Search, view profiles (**Overview**, **Submissions**, **Placements**, **Notes**, **Documents**), add candidates and edit them. |
| **Companies** | Search, view companies (**Overview**, **Notes**, **Documents**, **News**), add companies and edit them. |
| **Contacts** | Search, view, add and edit contacts. |
| **Jobs** | Search, view jobs and their submissions, create jobs and edit them. |
| **Notes and documents** | Add and edit notes, reply to comments and upload documents on candidates, companies, contacts and jobs. |
| **Deals** | Browse the pipeline and change a deal's status with **Update Status**. You need permission to manage the pipeline. |
| **Placements** | View placements and their details. Placements are view-only in the app. |
| **Tasks** | Create, edit, complete and delete tasks. Set priority, due date, reminder and assignee, and link tasks to records. |
| **Time Off** | Check your balances, apply for leave and cancel pending requests. Approvers also get an **Approvals** tab. |
| **Meetings** | Record in-person meetings and review summaries, transcripts and tasks. See [Recording meetings on mobile](/mobile/recording-meetings). |
| **eSign** | Create documents from a file, photo or scan, send them for signature and sign. See [eSign on mobile](/mobile/esign). |

### Deals

**Deals** shows your pipeline. Admins and managers can switch between their own pipeline and their team's. Tap the status on a deal card to open **Update Status**. Pick the new status and add an optional note. If you choose **Process Ended**, the app asks you to confirm with **End process**, because it removes the candidate from the active pipeline.

To learn what each status means, see [Submission statuses](/submissions/submission-statuses).

### Tasks

**Tasks** lists your tasks with search and filters for status, priority and assignee. Switch between **My Tasks** and **All Tasks**. Tap a task's checkbox to mark it complete. Tap the task to edit it, or use **Delete Task** to remove it.

For more on tasks, see [Managing tasks](/tasks/managing-tasks).

### Time off

**Time Off** has an **Overview** tab with your available days and upcoming leave, and a **Requests** tab with your requests. Tap **Apply** to request leave. You pick the leave type and dates, and give a reason of at least 10 characters.

If your role is Super Administrator, Administrator, Operations or Manager, you also see an **Approvals** tab. From there you can **Approve** or **Reject** pending requests. When you reject, you can add an optional reason.

For more on time off, see [Requesting time off](/time-off/requesting-time-off) and [Approving leave](/time-off/approving-leave).

## Notifications

After you sign in, your phone asks whether Connekt can send you notifications. Tap **Allow** to get push notifications on this device.

To change what you're notified about, go to **More → View profile → Notifications**:

1. Use the **Push Notifications** switch to turn push on for this device. If you declined the prompt earlier, the switch opens your phone's settings so you can allow notifications there.
2. Use the main switch next to each notification type to turn it on or off.
3. Tap **See details** on a type to choose **Push**, **Email** or both.

Turning a type off stops both push and email for it. It still shows in your notification inbox, which you open with the bell icon on **Home**.

Your choices are the same preferences you see on the web. See [Notification preferences](/integrations/notification-preferences).

## Your profile

Open your profile from **More → View profile**, or tap your avatar on **Home**. From there you can:

- Tap your photo to upload a new one.
- Tap **Edit Profile** to change your full name, mobile, job title, country and currency. You can't change your email in the app.
- Open **Notifications** to manage your notification preferences.
- Open **Security** to change your password, manage two-factor authentication and, for admins, screenshot protection. See [Signing in and security on mobile](/mobile/signing-in-and-security).
- Tap **Sign Out** to sign out of this device.

### Appearance

Under **Appearance**, choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. **System** follows your phone's light or dark setting.

## Get help

Go to **More → Help & Support** to chat with the Connekt team from the app. For other ways to reach us, see [Getting help](/getting-started/getting-help).
