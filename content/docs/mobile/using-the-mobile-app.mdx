The Connekt mobile app brings your full recruitment workspace to your phone or tablet, available for both **iOS** and **Android**. It connects to the same backend as the web app, which means your data is always in sync — anything you do on mobile appears instantly on the web, and vice versa. There's no separate mobile database to manage and nothing falls out of step between devices.

<Note>
  The mobile app is currently rolling out and may not yet be publicly listed on the App Store or Google Play for your organisation. Ask your workspace admin how to get access and install it on your device.
</Note>

## Setting Up Push Notifications

Push notifications keep you updated on the things that matter — new submissions, pipeline updates, approvals, and more.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Download and install the Connekt app on your device following the instructions from your workspace admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in with your Connekt credentials. MFA is supported if your workspace requires it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow notifications">
    When prompted by your device, tap **Allow** to enable push notifications for the Connekt app. If you missed the prompt, go to your device's **Settings → Notifications → Connekt** and enable them manually.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you're not receiving notifications you expect, check that notifications are enabled for the Connekt app in your device's system settings — it's easy to accidentally dismiss the initial permission prompt.
</Tip>
