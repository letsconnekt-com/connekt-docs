The Inbox shows your email threads with candidates and contacts in one place. You can read conversations, reply, and start new emails without leaving Connekt. To open it, click **Inbox** in the sidebar, or press **G** then **I**. The number next to **Inbox** shows how many conversations you haven't read yet.

## Before you start

The Inbox sends and receives through your own email account. Connect your account first. See [Connect your email](/integrations/connecting-email).

If your account isn't connected, the Inbox shows **Connect email** instead of your conversations. If it's connected but sending permissions weren't granted, you see **Sending isn't enabled yet**. Click the reconnect button and grant sending access.

<Warning>
  Sending from the Inbox currently works with Gmail only. You can connect an Outlook account to sync emails to records, but you can't send from Connekt with it yet.
</Warning>

## What you see in the Inbox

The Inbox shows conversations from your own connected mailbox only. Your teammates' conversations don't appear in your Inbox.

Connekt syncs only emails to or from candidates and contacts in your workspace. Emails with people who aren't in Connekt don't appear. See [How email sync works](/integrations/connecting-email#how-email-sync-works).

The conversation list shows each person's name, the latest message, and a dot for unread threads. Opening a conversation marks it as read.

- Use **Search conversations...** to find a thread by the person's name.
- Use the filter chips to narrow the list. **All** shows every conversation. **Replied** and **Opted out** show people in your [email sequences](/outreach/email-sequences) who replied or unsubscribed.

## Read a conversation

Click a conversation to open the thread. Messages you sent appear on the right, and messages you received appear on the left. Click an attachment to preview it.

The header shows the person's name, title and company. Click **View profile** to open their candidate or contact record.

If the person is enrolled in one of your sequences, a bar under the header shows the sequence name and their enrollment status. Click the sequence name to open it.

## Reply to a conversation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the reply box">
    Click **Reply to** at the bottom of the thread.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the subject and recipients">
    Connekt fills in the subject with **Re:** and the original subject. If other people were copied on the last message, Connekt adds them to **Cc** for you. Click **Cc/Bcc** to add more people. Separate several addresses with commas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your reply">
    Type your message, or click **Templates** and choose a template. Click **Attach file** to add attachments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send**. Connekt includes the earlier message as a quote below your reply.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Compose a new email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new email">
    Click **Compose** at the top of the conversation list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the recipient">
    Choose **Candidate** or **Contact**, then search for the person and select them. To pick someone else, click **Change**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the email">
    Enter a **Subject** and your message. Add **Cc/Bcc**, a template or attachments if you need them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For candidates, Connekt sends to the personal email first, then the work email. For contacts, it sends to the work email first, then the personal email. If the record has no email address, you can't send.

You can also email someone from their record. Open the **Conversations** tab on a candidate or contact and click **Compose**.

## Use email templates

Templates save time on emails you send often.

- To use a template, click **Templates** in the reply or compose box and choose one. The template replaces the subject and message.
- To create, edit or delete templates, click **Templates → Manage templates...**. Each template has a **Name**, **Subject** and **Body**.

Templates you create here are shared with everyone in your workspace.

<Note>
  Inbox templates are separate from Outreach templates, and they don't fill in personalization tokens. To create templates for sequences, see [Templates and analytics](/outreach/templates-and-analytics).
</Note>

## Add your signature

If you've set up an email signature, an **Include signature** checkbox appears below the message. It's selected by default, and your signature is added to the end of the email. To set up your signature, see [Profile and email signature](/account/profile-and-email-signature).

## Attachments

Click **Attach file** to add one or more files. To remove a file, click the **x** next to its name.

<Warning>
  All attachments on one email must add up to 20 MB or less.
</Warning>
