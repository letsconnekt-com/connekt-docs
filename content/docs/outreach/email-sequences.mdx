An email sequence is a series of steps that Connekt runs for each candidate you enroll. Steps can send personalized emails from your own mailbox, wait a number of days, check whether someone opened or clicked, or create a task for you to do by hand. When a candidate replies, their sequence stops automatically.

To open Outreach, click **Outreach** in the sidebar, or press **G** then **O**. Sequences are on the **Sequences** tab.

## Before you start

Outreach sends email through your own connected account. You need:

- The **Team** plan or higher. See [Plans compared](/billing/plans-compared).
- A connected Gmail account with sending enabled. See [Connect your email](/integrations/connecting-email).
- Candidates in a [project](/projects/creating-a-project), because you enroll candidates from a project.

If your account isn't connected, Outreach shows **Connect email** instead of your sequences. If it's connected but sending isn't enabled, you see **Sending isn't enabled yet**. Reconnect and grant sending access.

<Warning>
  Outreach currently sends with Gmail only. An Outlook account can sync emails to records, but it can't send sequence emails.
</Warning>

## The Sequences tab

The **Sequences** tab lists the sequences you created. Your teammates can't see or edit your sequences. The cards at the top show **Active sequences**, **Candidates in-flight**, **Total enrolled** and **Avg. reply rate** across your workspace.

For each sequence, the list shows its status, **Owner**, **Enrolled**, **In-flight** and **Reply rate**. Use **Search sequences...** and the status filter to find a sequence.

### Sequence statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | The sequence is new and hasn't been activated. Nothing is sent. |
| **Active** | The sequence is running. Steps run for everyone enrolled. |
| **Paused** | Nothing is sent. When you activate the sequence again, it continues where it left off. |
| **Archived** | The sequence is retired. You can't enroll anyone into it. |

To pause or activate a sequence from the list, click the pause or play button on its row. The **⋯** menu has **Edit**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**.

## Create a sequence

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new sequence">
    On the **Sequences** tab, click **New sequence**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Name** and, optionally, a **Description**. Click **Create**. The sequence builder opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add steps">
    Click a **+** button on the canvas to add a step at that point. Click a step to edit it in the panel on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the delivery schedule">
    Click the schedule button in the header to choose when emails can be sent. See [Set the delivery schedule](#set-the-delivery-schedule).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and activate">
    Click **Save** to keep your changes. When you're ready, click **Activate**. Activating also saves the sequence.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To rename a sequence, hover over its name in the builder and click the pencil icon.

<Note>
  To activate a sequence, it needs at least one email step or manual step. Email steps need a subject and a message. Manual steps need a message.
</Note>

## Step types

| Step | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Email step** | Sends an email from your connected account. |
| **Email from template** | Adds an email step pre-filled from one of your [Outreach templates](/outreach/templates-and-analytics). |
| **Manual LinkedIn message** | Creates a task for you to send a LinkedIn message by hand. |
| **Manual LinkedIn connection request** | Creates a task for you to send a LinkedIn connection request by hand. |
| **Manual phone call** | Creates a task for you to call the candidate. |
| **Manual task** | Creates a task with your own instructions. |
| **Wait** | Pauses the sequence for a number of days. |
| **Condition** | Continues only if the candidate meets a rule. Otherwise, they leave the sequence. |

### Email steps

An email step has these settings:

- **Step name**: a label for the step, such as "Intro email".
- **Delay (days)**: how long to wait after the previous step before sending.
- **Subject** and message: use the token buttons to add personalization. Click **Preview** to see the email with sample details.
- **Attachments**: click **Attach file**. You need to save the sequence once before you can attach files to a new step.
- **Include sender's email signature**: adds your signature to the end of the email. See [Profile and email signature](/account/profile-and-email-signature).
- **Include unsubscribe link**: adds a short footer with an unsubscribe link. It's on by default.

Follow-up emails are sent as replies in the same email thread as the earlier emails in the sequence.

When you add an **Email from template** step, Connekt copies the template's name, subject, message and signature setting into the step. The template's attachments are copied when you save. Changing the step afterwards doesn't change the template.

### Manual steps

Manual steps have a **Step name**, a **Delay (days)** and a **Message**. When a manual step runs, Connekt:

1. Creates a task assigned to you, the sequence owner. The task is linked to the candidate and includes the message, with tokens filled in.
2. Sends you a notification straight away.
3. Moves the candidate on to the next step. It doesn't wait for you to complete the task.

You'll find these tasks on the [Tasks](/tasks/managing-tasks) page.

<Warning>
  Connekt never sends anything on LinkedIn for you. Manual LinkedIn steps only create a task. You send the message or connection request yourself.
</Warning>

### Wait steps

Set **Wait (days)** to pause the sequence before the next step.

### Condition steps

Choose a rule under **Continue only if the candidate…**:

- **opened but no reply**
- **clicked a link**
- **has not opened**

If the candidate meets the rule (**Yes**), they continue to the next step. If not (**No**), they exit the sequence.

The condition checks the most recent email step before it. If there's no email step before the condition, it always continues.

<Tip>
  Connekt tracks opens and link clicks in sequence emails. Some email apps block open tracking, so treat open counts as a guide.
</Tip>

## Personalization tokens

Tokens are replaced with each candidate's details when the email is sent. Click a token button in the step editor to insert it, or type it in double curly braces.

| Button | Token | Filled with |
|---|---|---|
| **First name** | `{{first_name}}` | The candidate's first name |
| **Last name** | `{{last_name}}` | The candidate's last name |
| **Current company** | `{{company}}` | The candidate's current employer |
| **Current title** | `{{title}}` | The candidate's current title |
| **Open role** | `{{role}}` | The job title of the project's job |
| **Location** | `{{location}}` | The candidate's city |
| **Recruiter name** | `{{recruiter_name}}` | Your name |
| **Hiring company** | `{{job_company}}` | The company of the project's job |
| **Job location** | `{{job_location}}` | The city and country of the project's job, or "Remote" |

**Open role**, **Hiring company** and **Job location** come from the job linked to the project you enroll from. If the project has no job, these tokens are empty.

<Warning>
  If a candidate's record is missing a value, the token is left blank. Check that your candidates have the details your emails use before you enroll them.
</Warning>

## Set the delivery schedule

The schedule button in the builder header shows when emails can be sent, such as **5 days, 9:00am–6:00pm**. New sequences use your time zone, Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 6:00pm.

Click the button to change:

- **Timezone**: the time zone the schedule uses. If no time zone is set, the schedule doesn't apply and steps can run at any time.
- **Days**: the days steps can run. No days selected means every day is allowed.
- **From** and **To**: the time window steps can run in.

Click **Save** to apply your changes.

Delays only count allowed days. For example, with Monday to Friday selected, a 2-day delay from Friday runs on Tuesday. Connekt processes steps every 15 minutes, so a step can run up to 15 minutes after its scheduled time.

## Enroll candidates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the enroll dialog">
    In the sequence builder, click **Enroll**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a project">
    Find the project with **Filter projects...** and select it. To pick a different one, click **Change project**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose candidates">
    Every candidate with an email address is selected. Clear the ones you don't want, or use **Select all** and **Deselect all**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enroll">
    Click **Enroll**. Connekt tells you how many candidates were enrolled, and how many were skipped because they were already in the sequence.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- Candidates without an email address can't be selected. Click the enrich icon next to them to look up a personal email. See [Enriching contact details](/candidates/enriching-contact-details).
- For candidates, Connekt sends to the personal email first, then the work email.
- A candidate can only be enrolled in the same sequence once.
- You can enroll candidates while a sequence is a draft or paused. Nothing is sent until you activate it.

<Note>
  You can enroll up to 500 candidates at a time. The enroll dialog shows up to 500 candidates from a project.
</Note>

## Enrollment statuses

Each enrolled candidate has a status. You can see it in the [Inbox](/outreach/using-the-inbox) when you open a conversation with them.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **In flight** | The candidate is moving through the sequence. |
| **Completed** | The candidate reached the end of the sequence. |
| **Replied** | The candidate replied. Their remaining steps were cancelled. |
| **Opted out** | The candidate clicked the unsubscribe link. Their remaining steps were cancelled. |
| **Bounced** | An email couldn't be delivered. Their remaining steps were cancelled. |
| **Exited (condition)** | The candidate left the sequence. This happens when they don't meet a condition, or when an email can't be sent to them, such as when they have no email address. |

## Replies, bounces and opt-outs

- **Replies stop the sequence.** When a candidate replies to a sequence email, Connekt marks them as **Replied** and cancels their remaining steps. Their reply appears in your [Inbox](/outreach/using-the-inbox).
- **Bounces stop the sequence.** If an email bounces, the candidate is marked **Bounced** and gets no more steps.
- **Opt-outs apply to one sequence.** When a candidate clicks the unsubscribe link, they stop getting emails from that sequence. It doesn't remove them from your other sequences.

## Daily sending limit

Each user can send up to 400 sequence emails per day. When you reach the limit, the remaining emails wait and send after the limit resets the next day.

## Edit a running sequence

You can change steps and click **Save** while a sequence is active. The changes apply to steps that haven't run yet.

<Warning>
  You can't remove a step that already has enrollment activity. Connekt shows "Can't remove a step that already has enrollment activity — stop or complete those enrollments first."
</Warning>

## Duplicate or delete a sequence

- To copy a sequence, click **⋯ → Duplicate** on its row. The copy is a **Draft** named with "(copy)" and has the same steps and schedule. It has no enrolled candidates.
- To delete a sequence, click **⋯ → Delete** and confirm.

<Warning>
  Only draft or archived sequences can be deleted. Once you've activated a sequence, pause it instead. Deleting can't be undone.
</Warning>
