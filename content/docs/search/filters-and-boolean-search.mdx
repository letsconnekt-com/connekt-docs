Every main list in Connekt has its own search box and **Filter** button. Use them to narrow Candidates, Companies, Contacts, Jobs or the placement lists under **Deals** down to exactly the records you need. On Candidates, Companies, Contacts and Jobs, your search and filters are also saved in the page URL, so you can bookmark a view or share it with a teammate.

## Search a list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a list">
    Go to **Candidates**, **Companies**, **Contacts** or **Jobs**. For placements, go to **Deals** and open **My Placements** or **All Placements**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the search box">
    The box shows a hint such as **Search candidates… (AND, OR, NOT)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your search and run it">
    Press **Enter** or click **Search**. The list updates to show matching records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To clear the search, click **×** in the search box.

## Use Boolean operators

Combine terms with **AND**, **OR** and **NOT** to build more precise searches.

| Operator | What it does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **AND** | Both terms must match. | `java AND spring` |
| **OR** | Either term can match. | `react OR angular` |
| **NOT** | Excludes records that match the next term. | `developer NOT intern` |

Connekt highlights operators as you type, so you can check your query before you run it.

- Put a space on each side of an operator.
- Text between operators is treated as a phrase. For example, `project manager OR scrum master` searches for "project manager" or "scrum master".
- Operators are applied from left to right.
- Operators aren't case-sensitive, but uppercase is easier to read.

<Warning>
  Connekt won't run a search that starts with **AND** or **OR**, ends with an operator, or has two operators in a row. A message under the box explains what to fix.
</Warning>

## Add filters

Filters narrow a list by specific fields, such as status, owner, location or dates.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Filter">
    Click **Filter** next to the search box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a field">
    Under **Add filter**, pick the field you want to filter on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the condition">
    Choose a condition and enter or select a value. The conditions depend on the field type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply it">
    Click **Apply filter**. The filter appears as a chip, and the **Filter** button shows how many filters are active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Add more filters the same way. To remove one, click **×** on its chip. To remove them all, click **Clear all filters**.

### Conditions by field type

| Field type | Conditions |
|---|---|
| Text | contains, does not contain, is, is not, starts with, ends with, is empty, is not empty |
| Pick lists, such as status or owner | is, is not, is any of, is none of, is empty, is not empty |
| Numbers, such as salary | equals, greater than, less than, between, in list |
| Dates | Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month, Last month, This year, Last year, or a custom date (on, before, after, between) |
| Yes/no, such as **Off Limits** | is true, is false |

You can filter candidates by fields such as **Status**, **Source**, **Level**, **Skills**, **Industries**, **City**, **Country**, **Languages**, **Owner**, **Researcher** and **Last Note Date**. Each list has its own set of filterable fields.

<Tip>
  Combine a Boolean search with filters. For example, search `python OR golang`, then filter **Country** is "Singapore" and **Status** is any of your active statuses.
</Tip>

## Bookmark or share a filtered view

On **Candidates**, **Companies**, **Contacts** and **Jobs**, Connekt saves your search and filters in the page URL as you apply them.

- Bookmark the page in your browser to come back to the same view later.
- Copy the URL and send it to a teammate. They see the same search and filters, limited to the records they have permission to see.

The **My** / **All** toggle at the top of the list, such as **My Candidates** and **All Candidates**, is saved in the URL too.

<Note>
  Connekt doesn't have a saved-filters list inside the app. Use browser bookmarks to keep views you use often. Searches and filters on the placement lists under **Deals** aren't saved in the URL.
</Note>

## Choose which columns you see

You can change the table layout on Candidates, Companies, Contacts, Jobs, Tasks and the placement lists under **Deals**.

Click the arrow next to any column heading to:

- **Sort Ascending** or **Sort Descending**, or **Clear Sort**
- **Pin Column** to the left or right so it stays visible as you scroll
- **Add Column to Left** or **Add Column to Right**, including your [custom fields](/advanced/custom-fields)
- **Hide Column**

You can also drag a column's edge to resize it.

After you change the layout, click **Save Layout** above the table to keep it. Your saved layout is personal and doesn't change what teammates see.
