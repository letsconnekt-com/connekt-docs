Ask AI lets you search your Connekt database in plain language. Describe the candidate you need and Ask AI returns a ranked shortlist with the reasons behind each match. You can also ask questions about your companies, contacts, jobs, submissions and placements.

## Open Ask AI

Click **Ask AI** next to the search bar at the top of any page. You can also open global search (**⌘K** / **Ctrl+K**) and choose **Ask AI** under **Quick Actions**.

Ask AI opens in a full-screen panel. Your searches are listed on the left, the conversation is in the middle, and ranked candidates appear on the right.

## Find candidates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe who you're looking for">
    Type your request in the **Ask anything…** box. For example: "Senior React developers in London with fintech experience."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a mode (optional)">
    Click the mode button below the box to switch between **Fast**, **Auto** and **Deep Thinking**. See [Choose a mode](#choose-a-mode).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your request">
    Press **Enter** or click the send button. Press **Shift+Enter** to add a new line instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the ranked candidates">
    Ask AI writes a short summary and opens **Candidate Results**. Each card shows the candidate's experience, education, salary, skills, projects, highlights, why they match, and any flags to check.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine with follow-up questions">
    Keep the conversation going to narrow the list. For example: "Only people open to contract roles" or "Why isn't Jason Lee in the list?"
  </Step>
</Steps>

Below the top matches, **more matches** lists extra candidates that were ranked but not reviewed individually. Click it to expand the list.

Click a candidate card to open their profile. When you go back, Ask AI reopens where you left off.

<Note>
  Candidate search needs permission to view all candidates. If your role doesn't have it, Ask AI replies "You don't have permission to search candidates."
</Note>

## Choose a mode

| Mode | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Fast** | Quick answers with a short summary. |
| **Auto** | Everyday searches. This is the default. |
| **Deep Thinking** | Harder searches where you want Ask AI to reason through each candidate before it answers. It takes longer. |

## Attach a job for context

Attach an open job so Ask AI ranks candidates against that role.

1. Click **Add context** above the message box.
2. Search for and select a job from **Open Jobs**.
3. Type your request and send it.

You can attach more than one job. Click a job chip to remove it.

<Tip>
  To find the best candidates for one specific job, you can also use the **Matching candidates** card on the job page. See [Matching candidates to jobs](/candidates/matching-candidates-to-jobs).
</Tip>

## Add candidates to a project

1. In **Candidate Results**, click the actions menu on a candidate card.
2. Click **Add to Project**.
3. Select a project, or choose **Create new project** from the dropdown.
4. Click **Add to Project**.

See [Creating a project](/projects/creating-a-project) to learn more about projects.

## Ask questions about your data

Ask AI also answers questions about your companies, contacts, jobs, submissions and placements. For example:

- "How many active companies do we have?"
- "What is our total fee value in Q1?"
- "Which jobs have the most active submissions?"
- "Who's the hiring manager at Acme Corp?"
- "Which companies have open roles but no contact marked as hiring manager?"

Answers only include data you have permission to see.

<Info>
  Ask AI only answers questions about your Connekt data. For anything else, it replies that it can help you search for candidates, companies, job openings or placement data.
</Info>

## Manage your saved searches

Ask AI saves every conversation under **Searches** in the left panel. It names each search automatically from your first message.

- **Start a new search:** click **New Search**.
- **Reopen a search:** click it in the list.
- **Rename a search:** open the **⋯** menu next to it and click **Rename**. Type the new name and press **Enter**.
- **Delete a search:** open the **⋯** menu, click **Delete**, then confirm with **Delete Session**. You can't undo this.

Your searches are private to you.

<Note>
  If Connekt is still indexing records, such as after a large import, a banner says results may be incomplete for a few minutes.
</Note>
