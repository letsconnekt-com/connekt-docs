Global search lets you jump to any candidate, company, contact, job or placement from any page in Connekt. Use it when you know roughly what you're looking for, such as a name, an email address or a job title.

## Open global search

You can open global search in two ways:

- Click **Search everything...** in the bar at the top of the page.
- Press **⌘K** on a Mac or **Ctrl+K** on Windows.

The **Ask AI** button sits next to the search bar. For natural-language questions, see [Ask AI](/search/ask-ai).

## Search for a record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type your search">
    Enter at least two characters, such as a name, email address, company or job title. Results appear as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the results by type">
    Results are grouped under **Candidates**, **Companies**, **Contacts**, **Jobs** and **Placements**. Each heading shows the number of matches. If you're on a list page, such as **Candidates**, that group appears first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a result">
    Click a result, or highlight it with the arrow keys and press **Enter**. Connekt opens the record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If a group has more matches than the preview shows, click **View All** next to its heading. Connekt opens that list page with your search already applied, so you can add filters. See [Filters and Boolean search](/search/filters-and-boolean-search).
</Tip>

To clear what you've typed without closing the search, press **⌘K** or **Ctrl+K** again.

## Reopen a record from Recent Searches

When the search box is empty, **Recent Searches** lists the records you've recently opened from search. Each entry shows the record, the search you typed to find it, and how many times you've opened it.

Click an entry to go straight back to that record.

<Note>
  A search is saved to **Recent Searches** only when you click a result. Typing a search and closing the box doesn't save anything.
</Note>

Recent Searches shows your 10 most recent entries. Your history is private to you, so teammates can't see what you've searched for.
