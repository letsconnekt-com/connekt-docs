Linking a meeting to a candidate, job, company or contact puts it on that record's **Meetings** tab. It also lets Connekt suggest record updates and new records based on what was said in the call.

## Link a meeting to a record

Only the meeting organizer (the person who recorded or uploaded it) can link records.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Linked records">
    Open the meeting, click the **⋯** menu and choose **Linked records**. You can also click **Manage** on the **Linked Records** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the record">
    Choose **Candidate**, **Company**, **Contact** or **Job**, then search by name and click the record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the summary">
    A preview opens with the meeting summary. Choose a template if you want a different summary and edit the text if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link and save">
    Tick **Link this meeting to** the record, then click **Insert to Notes**. Connekt adds the summary as a note on the record and links the meeting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Linked Records** card on the meeting shows every linked record. Click one to open it. To unlink a record, open **Linked records** and click the **×** next to it under **Currently linked**.

<Note>
  You can link a meeting to several records, for example the candidate and the job you discussed.
</Note>

## Review AI suggestions on a meeting

After you link a completed meeting to at least one record, Connekt reads the transcript and may propose actions. They appear in the **Suggested by AI** card on the meeting, each with a quote from the call.

Suggestions can be:

- **Create candidate**, **Create contact**, **Create company** or **Create job**
- **Update record**, which fills fields on a linked record
- **Create task**

Click **Apply** to accept a suggestion or click **×** to dismiss it.

## Create follow-up tasks

The **Tasks** card on a meeting lists tasks linked to it.

- Click **Suggest with AI** to have Connekt read the transcript and propose follow-up tasks. Tick the ones you want and click **Add Selected**, or click **Add** on a single task. Click **×** to discard a suggestion.
- Click **Add Task** to create a task yourself.

Connekt doesn't create tasks automatically from meetings. You decide which ones to add. See [Managing tasks](/tasks/managing-tasks).

## Apply field suggestions on a record

When a linked meeting mentions details that are missing from a record, a suggestion box appears above the record's details. It reads, for example, **AI found 3 empty fields in your meetings**, and lists each field and value.

- Click **Apply all** to fill every suggested field.
- If suggestions come from more than one meeting, you can click **Apply** on each group.
- Click **Dismiss** to hide a group of suggestions.

<Info>
  Only empty fields are filled. Existing values are not overwritten.
</Info>

This works on candidates, companies, contacts and jobs. Questions in your summary templates can be mapped to record fields to guide these suggestions. See [Summary templates](/meetings/summary-templates).

## Pull meeting notes into a record

You can add a meeting summary to a record's notes without opening the meeting.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the record's notes">
    Go to the **Notes** tab on a candidate, company, contact or job. You'll also find this option when you add notes while creating a candidate, company or contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pull from Meeting Notes">
    Click **Pull from Meeting Notes**. Connekt finds up to 10 of your meetings that match the record, by attendee email or by the record's name in the meeting title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Insert the summary">
    Click **View** on a meeting, choose a template, edit the summary if needed and click **Insert to Notes**. To link the meeting to the record at the same time, tick **Link this meeting to** the record first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Pull from Meeting Notes** only finds meetings you recorded or uploaded yourself.
</Note>
