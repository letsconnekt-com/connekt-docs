Connekt Notetaker records your calls, transcribes them and writes an AI summary. Every recording lands on the **Meetings** page in Connekt, where you can review it, link it to candidates, jobs, companies and contacts, and turn it into tasks and record updates.

Connekt Notetaker is a Chrome extension. It's separate from the [Connekt Chrome extension](/integrations/chrome-extension), which captures LinkedIn profiles.

## Two ways to record

You choose a mode each time you start a recording from the extension popup.

| | **Stealth** | **Meeting Bot** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How it records | Captures the meeting tab's audio and video plus your microphone, from your own browser | Sends a bot that joins the call as a separate participant |
| Visible to others | No | Yes, it appears in the participant list |
| Live transcript and checklist | Yes, in the live panel | No, transcript and summary arrive after the call |
| Needs admitting | No | Yes, if the meeting has a waiting room |

## Supported platforms

| Platform | Stealth | Meeting Bot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Meet | Yes | Yes |
| Zoom (web client in Chrome) | Yes | No |
| Microsoft Teams (web, in Chrome) | Yes | No |
| Zoom, Teams or other desktop apps | No | No |

<Note>
  Connekt Notetaker only works with meetings open in a Chrome tab. If you join Zoom or Teams through the desktop app, Connekt Notetaker can't record it. Join from the browser instead.
</Note>

## What to know before you start

- **You start every recording yourself.** Connekt Notetaker doesn't join meetings automatically or on a schedule. Open the popup on the meeting tab and click to start.
- **Recordings use notetaker hours.** Each recording's length counts towards your workspace's monthly notetaker hours, including uploaded recordings. Connekt never stops a recording because you've run out. See [Usage and add-ons](/billing/usage-and-add-ons) to check your balance and top up.
- **In-person meetings.** To record a meeting in the room rather than a call, use the Connekt mobile app. See [Recording meetings on mobile](/mobile/recording-meetings).
- **You can also upload.** Already have a recording? Upload it from the **Meetings** page to get a transcript and summary.

<Warning>
  Recording laws vary by country and region, and some require every participant's consent. You're responsible for telling participants that the meeting is being recorded and getting consent where required. Stealth mode isn't visible to other participants, so announce the recording at the start of the call — or use Meeting Bot, which joins as a visible participant.
</Warning>
