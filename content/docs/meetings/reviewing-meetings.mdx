Every recording you can access appears on the **Meetings** page. Open a meeting to watch the recording, read the AI summary and transcript, check which questions were covered and ask AI about the call.

## Find a meeting

Click **Meetings** in the sidebar. The page is titled **Meeting Notes** and shows each meeting as a card with its thumbnail, duration, date, attendees and a preview of the summary.

- **Search by title**: type in the search box to filter by meeting title.
- **Source**: filter by **All Sources**, **Stealth**, **Meeting Bot** or **Uploaded**.
- **Load more**: the page shows 12 meetings at a time. Click **Load more** to see older ones.

Each card shows a source badge (**Stealth**, **Meeting Bot**, **Uploaded** or **Mobile App**) and a **Shared** badge if the meeting has been shared with teammates.

### Meeting statuses

Meetings that aren't finished yet show a status badge on the card and a banner on the detail page.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Recording** | The recording is still in progress. The transcript and summary appear once it finishes. |
| **Processing** | Connekt is transcribing and summarising the recording. This can take a few minutes. |
| **Completed** | The meeting is ready. No badge is shown. |
| **Failed** | The recording couldn't be processed. The banner explains why, where possible. |
| **Interrupted** | Connekt stopped receiving the recording partway through, for example after a dropped connection or a browser crash. |

A completed meeting can also show **Recording saved with issues**. This means part of the recording had a problem, for example the video has no sound, but the transcript is complete. See [Troubleshooting](/meetings/troubleshooting).

## Open a meeting

Click a meeting card to open it. Click **Back to Notes** to return to the list.

### Recording and title

The video player sits at the top. To rename the meeting, click its title, type a new one and press Enter.

### AI summary

The **AI summary** card shows the summary for the selected template.

- **Switch template**: pick another template from the dropdown to see the meeting summarised a different way. Connekt generates it the first time and remembers it. Click **New template** to create one. See [Summary templates](/meetings/summary-templates).
- **Regenerate**: click **Regenerate** to write a fresh summary with the selected template.
- **Copy**: click the copy icon to copy the summary to your clipboard.

### Attendees

The **Attendees** card lists the people Connekt identified on the call, from your Google Calendar event or from the names shown in the meeting.

### Question coverage

If the template has questions, the **Question coverage** card shows which ones were covered, for example **4/6 covered**. Covered questions show a supporting quote from the call. Questions that weren't answered are listed under **Missed**.

For Meeting Bot and uploaded recordings, coverage is worked out after the call.

### Linked records, suggestions and tasks

Below the summary you'll also find **Linked Records**, **Suggested by AI** and **Tasks**. See [Linking and AI actions](/meetings/linking-and-ai-actions).

## Read the transcript

The **Transcript** tab on the right shows the full conversation with speaker names. For recorded calls, each line also shows a timestamp.

- **Jump to a moment**: click any line to play the video from that point. The line being spoken is highlighted as the video plays.
- **Rename a speaker**: hover over a speaker's name, click the pencil icon, type the correct name and press Enter. Every line from that speaker updates. Only the meeting organizer can rename speakers.

## Ask AI about the meeting

Open the **Ask AI** tab and type a question in **Ask about this meeting...**. For example, ask what the candidate said about notice period, or what the client wants next. Answers are based on the meeting transcript.

## Translate a meeting

When a meeting has a transcript, a language button appears at the top of the page. Pick a language to translate the transcript and summary. The video and audio stay in the original language.

<Note>
  The first language you pick becomes the meeting's translation language. After that, the button switches between the original and that language only.
</Note>

## Download and copy

Click the **⋯** menu at the top of the meeting for these options:

- **Download transcript**: saves the transcript as a `.txt` file.
- **Copy full transcript**: copies the transcript to your clipboard.
- **Download video**: saves the recording as an `.mp4` file.
- **Share meeting**: creates a public link. See [Sharing and privacy](/meetings/sharing-and-privacy).

If you translated the meeting, the transcript you download or copy is in the language you're viewing.

## Meetings on records

Candidates, companies, contacts and jobs each have a **Meetings** tab. It lists the meetings linked to that record that you have access to. Click a meeting to open it.

## Recap email

When a meeting finishes processing, Connekt emails the meeting organizer. The email includes the meeting title, duration and summary, with a link to open the meeting in Connekt.
