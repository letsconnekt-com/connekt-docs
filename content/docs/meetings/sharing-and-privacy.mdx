Meetings are private by default. As the organizer, you decide which teammates can see a meeting, whether to share a public link, and when to delete it.

The organizer is the person who recorded or uploaded the meeting.

## Who can see a meeting

A meeting is visible to:

- The organizer.
- Teammates the organizer adds in **Manage access**.
- Everyone in your organization, if the organizer turns on **Visible to everyone in the org**.

Teammates who were on the call don't get access automatically. There's no admin override either: admins can't see other people's meetings unless they've been given access.

This applies everywhere a meeting appears, including the **Meetings** page and the **Meetings** tab on candidates, companies, contacts and jobs.

<Note>
  Meetings shared with other people show a **Shared** badge. Hover over it to see who has access and who added them.
</Note>

## Give teammates access

Only the organizer can manage access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage access">
    Open the meeting, click the **⋯** menu and choose **Manage access**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a teammate">
    Under **Add a teammate**, search for a team member, select them and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review who has access">
    The **Who has access** list shows everyone who can see the meeting. Click **×** next to a teammate to remove their access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To share the meeting with your whole organization, turn on **Visible to everyone in the org** in the same dialog. This skips the list, so every member of your organization can view the meeting. Turn it off to go back to the list.

## Share a public link

A public link lets anyone view the meeting's video and transcript without signing in to Connekt. People with the link don't see the AI summary, the **Ask AI** chat, attendees or tasks.

Only the organizer can create or revoke a public link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Share meeting">
    Open the meeting, click the **⋯** menu and choose **Share meeting**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a password (optional)">
    Turn on **Require password** and enter a password. Viewers must enter it to open the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an expiry">
    Under **Link expires**, choose **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days** or **Never**. The default is 30 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the link">
    Click **Generate link**. Connekt copies the link to your clipboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once a link exists, **Share meeting** shows the link, whether it's password protected, when it expires and how many times it's been viewed. Anyone who can see the meeting can copy the link from here.

### Revoke a link

Open **Share meeting**, click **Revoke link** and confirm with **Yes, revoke**. The link stops working straight away.

To change a link's password or expiry, revoke it and generate a new one. Each meeting has one public link at a time.

### What viewers see

People who open the link see the meeting title, the video and the transcript. If the link has a password, they see **Password required** first and enter the password to continue. If the link has expired or been revoked, they see **Unable to open this link**.

## Delete a meeting

Only the organizer can delete a meeting.

1. Open the meeting, click the **⋯** menu and choose **Delete meeting**.
2. Click **Delete** to confirm.

<Warning>
  Deleting a meeting permanently removes the recording, transcript, summary, share link and access grants. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

Tasks created from the meeting aren't deleted. They stay on your task list, no longer linked to the meeting.
