Use **Sign** to collect e-signatures on offer letters, contracts and NDAs without leaving Connekt. You upload the file, add your signers, place fields for each signer, and send. Connekt emails signers one at a time, in the order you set.

<Info>
  Sign is included on every plan. You need the **Create Documents** permission to see **Sign** in the sidebar. By default, Super Administrators, Administrators, Operations and Managers have it. An admin can give it to other roles in [Settings → Permissions](/team/customising-permissions).
</Info>

## Send a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the document">
    Go to **Sign** and click **New Document**. Drag your file into the upload area, or click to choose it.

    Connekt accepts **PDF or DOCX files up to 20 MB**. DOCX files are converted to PDF automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and choose the signing type">
    Enter a **Title**, for example "Offer Letter – Jane Smith". Signers see this title in their emails.

    Under **Signing type**, choose **Send for Signature**. To sign a document only yourself, see [Signing a document yourself](/documents/signing-yourself).

    Click **Create & Place Fields**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your signers">
    Click **Add Signers** (or **Signers** in the top bar) to open **Setup Signers**. Signer 1 signs first.

    For each signer, click **Add Signer** and choose a **Type**:

    - **Internal (team member)**: search for a colleague in **Team member**.
    - **External (company / third party)**: enter their **Email**. **Name (optional)** can stay blank if you don't know it.

    Click **Add myself** to include yourself. Click **Save** when the list and order are right.

    See [Internal and external signers](/documents/internal-vs-external-signers) for how each type signs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place fields">
    In the **Field Tools** panel, pick a signer under **Assign to Signer**. Then drag **Signature**, **Name**, **Date** or **Text** fields onto the document.

    Fields save automatically. See [Adding fields to a document](/documents/adding-fields-to-pdf) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Click **Send for Signature**. The confirmation shows each signer in order.

    - Leave **Send Directly** selected and click **Yes, send for signature** to send now.
    - Or choose **Submit for Review** to have a colleague approve it first. See [Reviewing a document before it's sent](/documents/review-before-sending).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Send for Signature** only appears once you've saved at least one signer and placed at least one field.
</Note>

## Who gets emailed, and when

Connekt only emails **the first signer** when you send. Each next signer is emailed after the previous one signs. Nobody can sign out of turn.

- **External signers** get an email with a secure signing link.
- **Internal signers** get an email with a signing link too, unless they're the person who created the document. The creator signs from the document page in Connekt instead.

<Warning>
  Once a document is sent, you can't change its fields or signers. If something is wrong, cancel it and create a new document.
</Warning>

## Track progress

Open the document from the **Sign** list to see:

- **Signers**: each signer's status, and whether they're **Internal** or **External**.
- **Activity**: when the document was sent, viewed and signed.
- The **Created**, **Sent** and **Expires** dates.

For external signers who haven't signed yet, click **Resend** to email them a new link. See [Document statuses](/documents/document-statuses) for reminders and expiry.

## When everyone has signed

The status changes to **Completed**. Connekt emails every signer, the document's creator and any [CC recipients](/documents/managing-documents) with links to the signed PDF and the certificate of completion.

You can also download both from the document's **⋯** menu with **Download Document** and **Download Certificate**.

<Tip>
  You can also create and send documents from the Connekt iOS app. See [eSign on mobile](/mobile/esign).
</Tip>
