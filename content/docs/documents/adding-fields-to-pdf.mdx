Fields tell each signer where to sign and what to fill in. You place them in the field editor, which opens after you create a document in **Sign**. You can also reopen it from a draft with **Edit Fields**.

<Note>
  Set up your signers first. The editor shows **Set up signers first** until you've saved at least one signer.
</Note>

## Field types

| Field | What the signer does |
|---|---|
| **Signature** | Draws, types or uploads their signature. |
| **Name** | Confirms their name. It's pre-filled with the signer's name when Connekt knows it. |
| **Date** | Confirms a date. It's pre-filled with the day they sign, and they can change it. |
| **Text** | Types free text, such as an address or job title. |

Every field is required. A signer can't submit until they've confirmed all of their fields.

## Place and assign fields

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a signer">
    In the **Field Tools** panel, click a signer under **Assign to Signer**. Each signer has their own colour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag a field onto the page">
    Drag a field from **Field Type** onto the document. The new field belongs to the signer you selected and uses their colour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust it">
    Drag the field to move it. Drag its bottom-right corner to resize it. Use the zoom controls at the bottom of the page for precise placement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat for each signer">
    Select the next signer under **Assign to Signer** before placing their fields.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A field stays with the signer it was placed for. To give it to a different signer, delete it, select the right signer and place it again.
</Warning>

## Review your fields

The **Placed Fields** list shows every field, its signer and its page.

- Click a field in the list to highlight it on the document.
- Click the pencil icon on a **Text** field to rename it. The name tells the signer what to enter, for example "Home address".
- Click the trash icon to delete a field. You can also delete it from the field on the page.

Changes save automatically. The top bar shows **Saving…**, then **Saved**. If you see **Save failed**, check your connection before you send.

<Tip>
  Before sending, scroll through every page and check each field's colour. It's easier to fix an assignment now than to cancel and start again.
</Tip>

## Send

When every signer has their fields, click **Send for Signature** (or **Ready to Sign** for documents you're signing yourself). See [Sending a document for e-signature](/documents/uploading-and-sending).
