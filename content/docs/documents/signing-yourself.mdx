Use **Sign Yourself** when you're the only person who needs to sign, for example to countersign a contract that arrived by email. You also use the same signing steps when you're an internal signer on someone else's document.

## Create a Sign Yourself document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the file">
    Go to **Sign** and click **New Document**. Upload a PDF or DOCX file up to 20 MB and enter a **Title**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Sign Yourself">
    Under **Signing type**, choose **Sign Yourself**. Click **Create & Place Fields**.

    Connekt adds you as the only signer automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place your fields">
    Drag **Signature**, **Name**, **Date** or **Text** fields onto the document. See [Adding fields to a document](/documents/adding-fields-to-pdf).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get it ready">
    Click **Ready to Sign**, then **Yes, ready to sign**. The document opens, ready for your signature.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign the document

These steps work for Sign Yourself documents and for any document where you're the next internal signer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start signing">
    Open the document. In the **Your turn to sign** panel, click **Sign Now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your fields">
    In **Sign Document**, check your fields. **Name** and **Date** are pre-filled. You can change them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your signature">
    Pick a signature under **Saved Signatures**, or create one with the **Draw**, **Type** or **Upload** tabs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Click **Save & Review**. Your signature and values appear on the document so you can check them. Click **Edit** to change anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Confirm Signature**. Tick the consent checkbox and click **Complete Signing**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If other signers come after you, Connekt notifies the next one automatically. When the last person signs, the document becomes **Completed**.

## Ways to create a signature

| Tab | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Draw** | Draw with your mouse, trackpad or finger. Expand the pad for more room. |
| **Type** | Type your name and choose a font style. |
| **Upload** | Upload a PNG or JPG image of your signature. |

## Save a signature for later

Tick **Save signature for future use** before you click **Save & Review**. Connekt saves it when you complete signing. Next time you sign in Connekt, it appears under **Saved Signatures**.

- You can save up to **5** signatures. The counter shows how many you've used.
- To remove one, hover over it and click the trash icon.
- Saved signatures are personal. Only you can use them.

<Note>
  Saved signatures are available when you sign inside Connekt. If you sign through an emailed link, you draw, type or upload your signature on the signing page.
</Note>
