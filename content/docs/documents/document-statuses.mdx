Every document in **Sign** has a status that shows where it is in the signing process. Connekt updates it automatically as reviewers approve and signers sign. You can filter the **Sign** list by status and by type (**Sign Yourself** or **Send for Signature**).

## Status reference

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | The document has been created but not sent. You can still change signers and fields. |
| **Pending Review** | The document was submitted for review and is waiting for the reviewer to approve or reject it. |
| **Pending Signature** | The document has been sent and nobody has signed yet. The **Sign** list shows this as **Pending**. |
| **Partially Signed** | At least one signer has signed, and others still need to. The **Sign** list shows this as **Partial**. |
| **Completed** | Everyone has signed. The signed PDF and certificate of completion are ready. |
| **Expired** | An external signer didn't sign within 14 days. Signing links no longer work. |
| **Cancelled** | Someone cancelled the document. Signing links no longer work. |

## Reminders and expiry for external signers

When Connekt emails an external signer, their 14-day window starts.

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| 3 | The signer gets a first reminder. |
| 7 | The signer gets a second reminder. |
| 10 | The signer gets a final reminder, and the document's creator gets an alert. |
| 14 | If they still haven't signed, the document becomes **Expired**. The creator is emailed. |

If you click **Resend** for a signer, they get a new link and the 14 days start again.

<Note>
  Internal signers don't get reminders, and their links don't expire the document. Their links stay valid for 30 days.
</Note>

## What you can do at each status

You need **Can Manage** access to a document to change it. The document's creator always has it. See [Managing documents](/documents/managing-documents).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Draft">
    - Click **Edit Fields** to change signers and fields.
    - Send the document, or submit it for review.
    - Add CC recipients and share it with your team.
    - From the **⋯** menu, choose **Cancel Document** or **Delete**.

    If a reviewer rejected the document, a **Sent back for changes** banner shows their reason. Make your changes and send again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pending Review">
    - The reviewer, or any Administrator, can **Approve** or **Reject** it. See [Reviewing a document before it's sent](/documents/review-before-sending).
    - You can't change signers or fields while it's under review.
    - You can still cancel or delete it from the **⋯** menu.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pending Signature and Partially Signed">
    - Watch progress under **Signers** and **Activity**.
    - Click **Resend** next to an external signer who hasn't signed to email them a new link.
    - If it's your turn as an internal signer, click **Sign Now**.
    - Add or remove CC recipients.
    - From the **⋯** menu, choose **Cancel Document** or **Delete**.

    You can't change fields or signers after sending.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Completed">
    - From the **⋯** menu, choose **Download Document** for the signed PDF, or **Download Certificate** for the certificate of completion.
    - Choose **Link to Company** to attach the document to a company.
    - Choose **Delete** to archive or permanently delete it.

    If the signed PDF isn't ready yet, **Retry Finalization** appears in the **⋯** menu. Use it to generate the signed PDF again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Expired and Cancelled">
    The signing process is over, and signing links no longer work. You can't reopen or resend the document.

    To try again, click **New Document** and upload the file again. Cancelled documents are removed from your **Sign** list.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  Cancelling can't be undone. Signers can no longer use their links, and the document disappears from your **Sign** list.
</Warning>
