---
title: "Reviewing a document before it's sent"
sidebarTitle: "Review before sending"
description: "Submit a document for review in Sign so a colleague approves it before signers are emailed. Reviewers can approve it or send it back with a reason."
---

Use review when someone should check a contract before it goes out, such as a manager approving an offer letter. You submit the document to a reviewer instead of sending it. When they approve it, Connekt sends it to the signers automatically.

Review is available for **Send for Signature** documents.

## Submit a document for review

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the document">
    Create the document, set up signers and place fields as usual. See [Sending a document for e-signature](/documents/uploading-and-sending).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the send options">
    Click **Send for Signature**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a reviewer">
    Select **Submit for Review**. Under **Reviewer**, search for a team member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit for Review**. The document's status changes to **Pending Review**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can't choose yourself or the document's creator as the reviewer.
</Note>

The reviewer gets a notification, and an email if their [notification preferences](/integrations/notification-preferences) allow it. Connekt also gives them view access to the document.

While the document is **Pending Review**, nobody can change its signers or fields.

## Approve or reject a document

If you're the reviewer, open the document from the notification or the **Sign** list. An **Awaiting your review** panel appears on the right.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Approve">
    1. Click **Approve**.
    2. Click **Yes, approve** to confirm.

    The status changes to **Pending Signature**, and Connekt emails the first signer. You can't undo an approval.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Reject">
    1. Click **Reject**.
    2. Explain what needs to change. A reason is required.
    3. Click **Confirm Reject**.

    The document goes back to **Draft**. The sender sees a **Sent back for changes** banner with your reason.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The person who submitted the document is notified either way.

<Info>
  Super Administrators and Administrators can also approve or reject any document that's waiting for review.
</Info>

## After a rejection

Open the document, read the **Sent back for changes** banner and click **Edit Fields** to make your changes. Then send it again, either directly or for another review.

## Track the review

The document's **Activity** log shows each step, for example "Submitted to Alex for review", "Review approved — sent to client" or "Review rejected — back to draft".
