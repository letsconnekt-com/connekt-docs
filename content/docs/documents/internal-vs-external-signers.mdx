A document in **Sign** can have internal signers (people in your Connekt workspace) and external signers (candidates, clients or anyone else). You can mix both on one document. Everyone signs in the order you set.

## Internal signers

In **Setup Signers**, choose **Internal (team member)** and pick the person in **Team member**. The list shows active users in your workspace.

- **How they're told**: when it's their turn, Connekt emails them a signing link. If they created the document, there's no email. They sign from the document page instead.
- **Where they sign**: through the link in the email, or in Connekt. When it's their turn, the document page shows **Your turn to sign** with a **Sign Now** button.
- **Access**: adding someone as an internal signer gives them view access to the document.

When internal signers sign in Connekt, they can reuse a saved signature. See [Signing a document yourself](/documents/signing-yourself).

## External signers

In **Setup Signers**, choose **External (company / third party)** and enter their **Email**. **Name (optional)** can stay blank. If you place a **Name** field for them, the name they enter is used.

- **How they're told**: when it's their turn, they get an email with a secure signing link.
- **Where they sign**: in their browser. They don't need a Connekt account.
- **What they see**: a **Secure Signing** page with their fields highlighted. They click **Next field** to move through them, then **Submit Signature**. They then confirm the **Electronic Signature Consent** and click **I Agree & Submit**.

## Signing order

Signers sign in the order shown in **Setup Signers**. Signer 1 signs first.

- Connekt only emails the first signer when you send.
- Each next signer is emailed after the previous one signs.
- A signer can't sign until everyone before them has signed.

<Tip>
  Order your signers to match the real process. For example, add the candidate first and the hiring manager second if the candidate must sign before the countersignature.
</Tip>

## How signing links work

Each signer gets their own link. Connekt records their name, email, IP address, device and signing time on the certificate of completion.

- **External links** expire after **14 days**. Reminders go out on days 3, 7 and 10. See [Document statuses](/documents/document-statuses).
- **Internal links** stay valid for **30 days**.
- A link stops working once the signer has signed, or if the document is cancelled or expires.

<Warning>
  Signing links aren't protected by a password or login. Anyone who has a signer's link can open it. Double-check email addresses before you send, and ask signers not to forward their emails.
</Warning>

## Resend a link

If an external signer can't find their email, open the document and click **Resend** next to their name. Then click **Resend Email**.

- Connekt emails a new link, and the old link stops working.
- The 14-day window and reminders start again from the resend.

**Resend** appears only for external signers who haven't signed yet, while the document is still waiting for signatures. You can't resend on an expired document.
