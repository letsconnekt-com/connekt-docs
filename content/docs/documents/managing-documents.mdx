Once a document exists in **Sign**, you control who can see it, who gets a copy, and where it's filed. Open any document from the **Sign** list to manage it.

## Who can see a document

Documents are private by default. You see a document in your **Sign** list only if:

- you created it, or
- someone shared it with you, or
- you're an internal signer or the reviewer on it.

Admin roles don't see every document automatically. Someone has to share it with them.

## Share with your team

Use **Team Access** to give colleagues access to a document.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Access">
    Open the document and click **Team Access** in the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add people">
    Search for colleagues in **Add people...**. You can add several at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose their access">
    Pick **Can View** or **Can Manage**, then click **Share**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

People you share with get a notification, and an email if their [notification preferences](/integrations/notification-preferences) allow it.

| Access | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Can View** | Open the document, follow its progress and download the files. The document shows a **View Only** badge. |
| **Can Manage** | Everything in **Can View**, plus edit fields and signers on drafts, send, resend, cancel, delete, add CC recipients, link a company and manage **Team Access**. |

To change someone's access, pick a new level next to their name under **People with access**. To remove them, click the **X**.

<Note>
  **Team Access** is for people in your workspace only. To send someone outside Connekt a copy, add them as a CC recipient.
</Note>

## Add CC recipients

CC recipients get an email with links to the signed PDF and certificate when the document is completed. They don't sign anything.

1. Open the document and find **CC Recipients**.
2. Click **Add**, then enter a **Name** and **Email**.
3. Click **Add CC**.

You can add anyone, inside or outside your workspace. To remove someone, click the **X** next to their name. You can't change CC recipients after the document is completed or cancelled.

## Link a signed document to a company

Link completed documents to a company to keep contracts with the client record.

1. Open a **Completed** document.
2. From the **⋯** menu, choose **Link to Company**.
3. Search for the company and select it.

The company name then shows on the document and in the **Company** column of the **Sign** list. Connekt can also use a linked contract to suggest billing details on the company. To remove the link, choose **Unlink from Company**.

<Note>
  Only completed documents can be linked to a company.
</Note>

## Download the signed PDF and certificate

When a document is **Completed**, open the **⋯** menu:

- **Download Document** downloads the signed PDF with every signature and field applied.
- **Download Certificate** downloads the certificate of completion as a separate PDF.

The certificate lists the document ID, completion time and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the signed PDF. For each signer, it shows their name, email, signature, IP address, device, and when the document was sent, viewed and signed.

<Tip>
  The links in the completion email expire after 24 hours. You can always download fresh copies from the document in Connekt.
</Tip>

## Follow the activity log

The **Activity** panel lists every event with a time, for example:

- Document created
- Sent to a signer
- A signer viewed or signed
- Submitted for review, approved or rejected
- Document completed, cancelled or expired

## Archive or delete a document

From the **⋯** menu, choose **Delete**. Connekt asks whether to archive or permanently delete the document.

| Option | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Archive** | Removes the document from your **Sign** list. Its files are kept for audit purposes. |
| **Delete** | Asks you to confirm with **Delete Permanently?**. This removes the document record and all its files, including the original, the signed PDF, the certificate and signature images. |

<Warning>
  Permanent deletion can't be undone. For **Sign Yourself** documents, archiving also deletes the document and its files permanently.
</Warning>

To stop a document that's still waiting for signatures, use **Cancel Document** instead. See [Document statuses](/documents/document-statuses).
