---
title: "Adding or removing seats in your Connekt subscription"
sidebarTitle: "Manage seats"
description: "Change your Connekt seat count from Settings → Plan. Learn when you're charged, when reductions take effect and how users are deactivated."
---

You can change the number of seats in your subscription at any time from **Settings → Plan**. Adding seats takes effect immediately. Removing seats takes effect at your next renewal.

Only Administrators can change seats.

<Info>
  You're billed for the number of seats you've purchased, not the number of active users. Every active user takes one seat. You can have between 1 and 500 seats.
</Info>

## Add seats

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Plan">
    Go to **Settings → Plan** and find the **Manage plan** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Increase the seat count">
    Under **Seats**, set the new number of seats. The new total for your billing period shows next to the seat picker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Update seats">
    Click **Update seats**, review the charge and click **Update seats** again to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You're charged today for the extra seats, prorated for the rest of your billing period. The new seats are available straight away. Connekt emails a confirmation to the workspace owners and admins.

## Remove seats

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Plan">
    Go to **Settings → Plan** and find the **Manage plan** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decrease the seat count">
    Under **Seats**, set the new, lower number of seats.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Update seats">
    Click **Update seats**, review the details and click **Update seats** again to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Here's what happens when you reduce seats:

- **Your bill goes down at your next renewal.** You've already paid for the current period, so no refund or credit is issued.
- **Your new seat limit applies straight away.** You can't invite or reactivate users beyond the new limit.
- **Existing users stay active until renewal.** Nobody loses access during the current period.

## If you have more users than seats at renewal

If you still have more active users than seats when your subscription renews, Connekt deactivates the extra users automatically:

- It deactivates users who aren't Administrators, starting with the least recently active.
- It never deactivates Administrators.

Connekt emails the workspace owners and admins when you reduce seats below your active user count. It sends a reminder 3 days before your renewal if you're still over the limit.

<Warning>
  To choose who keeps access, deactivate users yourself before your renewal date. Go to **Settings → Manage Users**, open the user's menu and choose **Deactivate User**. See [Deactivating and reactivating users](/team/deactivating-reactivating).
</Warning>

## Rules to know

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Deactivating a user doesn't lower your bill">
    Deactivating a user frees their seat for someone else, but you still pay for the same number of seats. To pay less, reduce your seat count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Inviting someone when all seats are in use">
    If every seat is taken, you see a **Seat limit reached** message when you invite someone. Add a seat or deactivate a user first, then send the invite.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Seats and your monthly allowances">
    Notetaker hours and enrich credits are allocated per seat. Adding seats increases your workspace's monthly allowance. See [Usage and add-ons](/billing/usage-and-add-ons).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
