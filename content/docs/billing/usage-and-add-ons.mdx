Some Connekt features draw from a monthly allowance: notetaker hours for recording meetings, and enrich credits for finding contact details. Administrators can track both on **Settings → Usage** and buy top-ups there when your workspace needs more.

## Check your usage

Go to **Settings → Usage**. The **Usage this month** card shows:

| Item | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Resume parsing** | How many resumes your workspace has parsed this month. Resume parsing is unlimited on every plan. |
| **Notetaker hours** | Hours used out of your monthly allowance, plus any purchased hours you have left. |
| **Enrich credits** | Credits used out of your monthly allowance, plus any purchased credits you have left. |

Your allowance resets on your billing date each month. This also applies to annual subscriptions: the allowance still resets monthly.

When you've used 80% of an allowance, Connekt shows how much is left. The **Buy more hours** or **Buy more credits** button then appears.

## Notetaker hours

Notetaker hours cover meetings recorded with the AI notetaker. Each seat adds hours to your workspace's monthly allowance:

| Plan | Notetaker hours per seat each month |
|---|---|
| Pro | 30 |
| Team | 90 |
| Business | 150 |

Hours are pooled across your workspace. For example, a Team workspace with 5 seats gets 450 hours a month to share. Connekt deducts a meeting's length from the balance after the recording finishes.

Your monthly allowance is used first. When it runs out, Connekt draws from purchased hours. Connekt never stops a recording because you've run out of hours.

## Enrich credits

Enrich credits pay for finding a person's contact details. Every plan includes 300 enrich credits per seat each month, pooled across your workspace.

A lookup only costs credits when Connekt finds a match:

| Result | Cost |
|---|---|
| Work email found | 1 credit |
| Personal email found | 3 credits |
| Phone number found | 10 credits |

A lookup that comes back empty costs nothing. Connekt tries to find a work email first. It only searches for a personal email if it doesn't find one.

<Warning>
  When your workspace runs out of enrich credits, nobody can start new lookups until the allowance resets or an Administrator buys more credits. See [Enriching contact details](/candidates/enriching-contact-details).
</Warning>

## Buy a top-up

Only Administrators can buy top-ups. Purchased hours and credits never expire. Connekt uses them after your monthly allowance runs out.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Usage">
    Go to **Settings → Usage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    In the **Notetaker hours top-up** or **Enrich credits top-up** card, enter how many hours or credits you want. The price appears on the buy button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy and confirm">
    Click the buy button, review the amount and click the **Charge** button to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The top-up is charged to the card on file for your subscription, and your balance updates straight away. If there's no card on file, Connekt takes you to a secure checkout page instead.

<Note>
  You can buy between 1 and 1,000 notetaker hours at a time, and between 1 and 100,000 enrich credits.
</Note>

## Set a per-user monthly cap

Administrators can set a monthly cap on how much each person uses. For enrich credits, a cap stops one person from using up the whole workspace's allowance.

1. Go to **Settings → Usage**.
2. Under the top-up card, find **Per-user monthly cap**. The cap for notetaker hours is set in minutes. The cap for enrich credits is set in credits.
3. Enter a number and click **Save**. To remove a cap, clear the field and click **Save**.

A person who reaches their enrich credits cap can't start new lookups until the allowance resets.

## Email alerts

When your workspace's notetaker hours or enrich credits run out, Connekt emails the workspace owners and admins. This way, someone can top up before your team notices.
