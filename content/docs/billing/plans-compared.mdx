Connekt has three plans: Pro, Team and Business. Each plan builds on the one before it. All plans are priced per seat, and you can pay monthly or annually.

For current prices, see the [Connekt pricing page](https://www.letsconnekt.com/pricing).

## How pricing works

- **You pay per seat.** Your bill is based on the number of seats you've purchased, not the number of active users. Every active user takes one seat. See [Adding or removing seats](/billing/adding-removing-seats).
- **Seats range from 1 to 500.** You can change your seat count at any time from **Settings → Plan**.
- **Monthly or annual billing.** Annual billing is paid upfront for the year and saves about 17% per seat compared with monthly. See [Switching billing interval](/billing/switching-billing-interval).

## Usage allowances

Some features come with a monthly allowance. Allowances are pooled across your workspace, so everyone on your team draws from the same balance.

| | Pro | Team | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notetaker hours per seat each month | 30 | 90 | 150 |
| Enrich credits per seat each month | 300 | 300 | 300 |
| Custom fields | Up to 50 | Up to 50 | Up to 50 |
| Resume parsing | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

If you run low, Administrators can buy top-ups from **Settings → Usage**. See [Usage and add-ons](/billing/usage-and-add-ons).

## Features that need a higher plan

Some features are only included in higher plans. If your plan doesn't include a feature, its settings page or sidebar item doesn't appear until you upgrade.

| Feature | Where to find it | Plan needed |
|---|---|---|
| Custom permissions | **Settings → Permissions** | Team or higher |
| Teams and hierarchy | **Settings → Organization** | Team or higher |
| Audit Log | **Settings → Audit Log** | Team or higher |
| API and Zapier/Make | **Settings → API Key** | Team or higher |
| Time Off | **Time Off** in the sidebar | Team or higher |
| Advanced analytics, such as financial, top performer and conversion metrics | **Home** dashboard | Business |

## Change your plan

Administrators can change the plan from **Settings → Plan**. Open the **⋯** menu on the **Manage plan** card and choose **Upgrade to** or **Downgrade to** a plan.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Upgrading">
    Upgrades take effect immediately. You're charged the prorated difference for the rest of your current billing period straight away.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Downgrading">
    Downgrades take effect at your next renewal. You keep your current plan's features until the end of the billing period, and no refund is issued for the current period. From your renewal date, you're billed at the lower plan's price.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
