---
title: "What happens when a Connekt payment fails"
sidebarTitle: "Payment failures"
description: "Learn what happens when a Connekt payment fails, who gets notified, how the 7-day grace period works and how to update your payment method."
---

If a payment fails, your team keeps working for 7 days while you fix it. Connekt emails the workspace owners and admins so the right people know straight away.

## Emails you'll receive

Connekt emails every workspace owner and admin:

- **Each time a payment attempt fails.** The email shows the amount due and includes an **Update payment method** button. It may also include an **Open Stripe invoice** link to pay that invoice directly.
- **3 days after the first failure**, as a reminder, if the payment still hasn't gone through.
- **7 days after the first failure**, as a final reminder.

<Info>
  If you don't see these emails, check your spam folder. Emails go to the workspace owners and admins, so ask one of them if you're not sure.
</Info>

## Grace period

You have a **7-day grace period** from the first failed payment. During this time:

- Your workspace stays fully accessible, and your team keeps working as normal.
- A banner shows how many days are left before your account is suspended.
- Administrators see a **Past due** status on **Settings → Plan**, along with the number of days left before access is suspended.

## Fix a failed payment

Only Administrators can update payment details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Plan">
    Go to **Settings → Plan**. You can also click **Update payment** in the banner or in the payment email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Update payment">
    Click **Update payment** on your plan card. This opens the secure billing portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your payment method">
    Add a new card or update your existing payment details. You can also pay the outstanding invoice from the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Connekt">
    Once the payment goes through, your subscription becomes active again. The banner disappears, and your team keeps full access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also fix a failed payment without signing in. Use the **Open Stripe invoice** link in the payment email to pay that invoice directly.
</Tip>

## If the grace period ends

If the payment isn't resolved within 7 days, your workspace access is suspended and your team can't use Connekt.

<Warning>
  Your data isn't deleted when access is suspended. An Administrator can restore access at any time by paying the outstanding balance through **Update payment** or the invoice link in the email.
</Warning>

If you can't resolve a payment issue, contact the Connekt team. See [Getting help](/getting-started/getting-help).
