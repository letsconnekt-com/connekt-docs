---
title: "Switching between monthly and annual billing"
sidebarTitle: "Billing interval"
description: "Switch your Connekt subscription between monthly and annual billing from Settings → Plan, and learn when each change takes effect."
---

You can pay for Connekt monthly or annually. Annual billing is paid upfront for the year and saves about 17% per seat compared with monthly. Administrators can switch between the two from **Settings → Plan**.

Switching your billing interval doesn't change your plan, your seats or your data.

## Switch to annual billing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Plan">
    Go to **Settings → Plan** and find the **Manage plan** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Annually">
    Next to **Billing interval**, select **Annually**. The annual saving per seat appears below the toggle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Switch to annual">
    Click **Switch to annual**. Review the amount, then click **Switch to annual** again to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You're charged for the full year straight away. Any unused time on your current monthly period is taken off that charge. Your billing cycle restarts from today, so your next renewal is one year from the switch.

## Switch to monthly billing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Plan">
    Go to **Settings → Plan** and find the **Manage plan** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Monthly">
    Next to **Billing interval**, select **Monthly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Switch to monthly">
    Click **Switch to monthly**. Review the details, then click **Switch to monthly** again to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your annual plan continues until the end of the current annual period. After that, you're billed monthly. No refund is issued for the rest of the annual period.

<Warning>
  If you have a discount on your annual plan, switching to monthly removes it.
</Warning>

## What changes and what stays the same

| What changes | What stays the same |
|---|---|
| How often you're billed | Your plan |
| The amount on each invoice | Your seat count |
| Your renewal date, when you switch to annual | Your data and records |

<Note>
  The **Manage plan** card only appears while you have an active subscription. If your subscription is set to cancel, undo the cancellation first. See [Cancelling your subscription](/billing/cancelling-subscription).
</Note>
