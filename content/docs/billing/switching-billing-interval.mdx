Switching to annual billing is one of the simplest ways to reduce your Connekt costs. You can make the switch at any time — you won't lose any data, features, or active users in the process, and you'll receive a prorated credit for any unused time on your current monthly cycle.

## Switching to Annual Billing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Billing & Usage">
    Navigate to **Settings → Billing & Usage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Change Billing Interval">
    Select **Change Billing Interval** to open the billing interval settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Annual">
    Choose **Annual** as your billing interval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Review the updated pricing before confirming. A prorated credit for the remaining days in your current monthly period is automatically applied to your first annual payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you want to understand exactly how much you'll save before committing, contact Connekt support — we can walk you through the numbers for your specific plan and seat count.
</Tip>

## Switching Back to Monthly

If you ever want to return to monthly billing, go to **Settings → Billing & Usage → Change Billing Interval** and select **Monthly**.

<Note>
  If you don't see the option to switch back to monthly, or if you have questions about how proration works for your account, contact Connekt support for help.
</Note>

## What Stays the Same

Switching your billing interval doesn't change anything about how you use Connekt:

| What changes | What stays the same |
|---|---|
| Your billing date (updates to the new cycle start) | Your plan tier |
| Your billing interval (monthly → annual) | Your seat count |
| The total amount billed | All your data and records |
| | Active add-ons (continue without interruption) |

Your team won't notice a thing — everything keeps running exactly as before.
