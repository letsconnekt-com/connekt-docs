---
title: "Cancelling or resuming your Connekt subscription"
sidebarTitle: "Cancel subscription"
description: "Cancel your Connekt subscription from the billing portal, undo a cancellation before it takes effect, or reactivate after your access ends."
---

Administrators can cancel your Connekt subscription from the billing portal. Your team keeps access until the end of the current billing period. You can undo the cancellation at any time before then.

<Tip>
  If you want to pay less rather than leave, you have other options. You can reduce your seats or move to a lower plan from **Settings → Plan**. See [Adding or removing seats](/billing/adding-removing-seats) and [Plans compared](/billing/plans-compared).
</Tip>

## Cancel your subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Plan">
    Go to **Settings → Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Manage billing">
    Click **Manage billing** on your plan card. The secure billing portal opens. You manage your payment method, invoices and cancellation there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel in the portal">
    Choose to cancel your subscription and follow the prompts to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Connekt">
    Go back to **Settings → Plan**. Your plan card now shows **Access until** and the date your access ends.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While the cancellation is pending, the **Manage plan** card is hidden. You can't change seats, plan or billing interval until you undo the cancellation.

## Undo a cancellation

You can undo the cancellation any time before your access ends.

1. Go to **Settings → Plan**.
2. Click **Undo cancellation** on your plan card.

Your subscription renews as scheduled, and nothing else changes.

## When your access ends

At the end of your billing period, your subscription ends and your workspace becomes inactive. Your team can no longer use Connekt. Connekt emails the workspace owners and admins to confirm the subscription has been cancelled.

<Info>
  Cancelling doesn't delete your data. Your workspace data is kept, so you can pick up where you left off if you subscribe again.
</Info>

## Reactivate after your access ends

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Plan">
    Sign in as an Administrator and go to **Settings → Plan**. You'll see the **Reactivate subscription** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your billing interval and seats">
    Select **Monthly** or **Annually**, then set the number of seats you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Click **Subscribe to** followed by the plan name. Review the total, then click **Continue to payment** and complete checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once payment goes through, your team's access is restored with all your data in place.

<Note>
  Need help before you cancel? Chat with the Connekt team. See [Getting help](/getting-started/getting-help).
</Note>
