Each placement has two separate statuses. The placement status tracks the candidate's start. The payment status tracks invoicing and collection of your fee.

## Placement statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Starting Soon** | The candidate hasn't started yet. New placements start here by default. |
| **Already started** | The candidate has started the role. |
| **Backed out before the start date** | The candidate or client pulled out before the start date. |
| **Backed out during probationary period** | The placement ended during probation. |

In the placements list, the **Status** column shows short labels: **Starting Soon**, **Started** and **Backed Out**.

### Change a placement's status

You change the placement status on the placement's edit page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the placement">
    Click **Deals** in the sidebar, open **My Placements** or **All Placements**, and click **View** on the placement's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Edit">
    Click **Edit** at the top of the placement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the status">
    In **Placement Details**, choose the new **Status**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update Placement**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To edit a placement, you need the **Edit Deals** permission. You also need to be the placement's main recruiter, an Admin or Operations. For anyone else, the **Edit** button is disabled.

### What a backed-out status changes

- **Jobs:** only **Starting Soon** and **Already started** placements count as filled positions. When a placement backs out, the position opens up again and the job's status can change back to **Open** or **Partially Filled**.
- **Revenue and goals:** backed-out placements are left out of dashboard fee totals and goal progress.

## Payment statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending Invoice** | You haven't sent the invoice yet. |
| **Invoice Sent** | You've sent the invoice to the client. |
| **Payment Received** | The client has paid. |
| **Overdue** | The payment is late. |
| **Cancelled** | The invoice was cancelled. |

A placement with no payment status shows **Not Set**.

<Note>
  Only Super Admins, Admins and Operations can change the payment status, invoice number, invoice date and payment due date. Everyone else sees these values as read-only.
</Note>

### Change a payment status

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the placements list">
    Click **Deals** in the sidebar, then open **My Placements** or **All Placements**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show the payment columns">
    The **Payment Status**, **Invoice Number**, **Invoice Date** and **Payment Due Date** columns are hidden by default. Add them using the table's column settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the new status">
    Click the placement's **Payment Status** and choose a status. It saves right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you choose **Invoice Sent** and the placement has no invoice date, Connekt sets the invoice date to today. To set dates yourself, click the calendar icon next to the payment status. Then pick the **Invoice Date** and **Payment Date**. You can also edit **Invoice Number**, **Invoice Date** and **Payment Due Date** directly in their columns.

<Tip>
  The invoice date can lock the exchange rate used for reporting, depending on your company's policy in **Settings → Revenue & FX**. See [Offices and currency](/advanced/offices-and-currency).
</Tip>

## Filter placements

On **My Placements** and **All Placements**, use the search box or open the filters. You can filter by:

- **Status** and **Type**
- **Country**
- **Start Date**, **Created Date** and **Updated Date**
- **Year** and **Quarter**
- **Main Recruiter**, **Candidate**, **Company** and **Job**
- **Placement ID**

<Note>
  You can't filter by payment status. To review unpaid invoices, show the **Payment Status** column and sort by it.
</Note>
