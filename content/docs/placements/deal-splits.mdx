Deal splits record who shares a placement's fee and how much each person receives. You set splits by hand when you create or edit a placement. Connekt doesn't create them for you, and it won't save splits that don't add up to the full fee.

## Where you set splits

Splits live in the **Splits** section of these placement pages:

- **Create New Placement**, or the **Create Placement** dialog when you convert a submission. See [Create a placement](/placements/creating-a-placement).
- **Edit Placement**. Open the placement and click **Edit**.

Every placement needs at least one split. Once saved, splits show on the placement's **Overview** tab under **Commission Splits & Team Details**.

## Roles you can add

| Role | Who it's for | How you choose the person |
|---|---|---|
| **Recruiter** | The credited recruiter on the deal. | Pick an active user. |
| **Researcher** | Someone who sourced or researched the candidate. | Pick an active user with the Researcher role. |
| **Referral** | Someone outside your team who referred the deal. | Type their name. |

<Note>
  The people you can pick depend on your access. Admins can pick anyone. If you manage an office or lead a team, you can pick people in the offices and teams you manage. Other users with the Operations or Manager role can pick anyone. Everyone else can only credit themselves.
</Note>

## Add or change splits

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the fee first">
    Enter the **Fee Value** in **Compensation & Fees**. The **Splits** section shows it as **Fee to split**. Until you set a fee, Connekt asks you to set one before you configure splits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add people">
    Click **Add person** and choose **Recruiter**, **Researcher** or **Referral**. Then pick the user, or type the referral's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set each person's share">
    For each row, choose **%** or **Flat**. Enter a percentage of the fee, or a flat amount. The **Receives** column shows what each person gets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Split evenly (optional)">
    Click **Split evenly** to share the fee equally across all percentage rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add region, office and team (optional)">
    Open a row's details to set its **Billing Region**, **Office** and **Team**. Office and team come from the person's own offices and teams. Referral rows don't have an office or team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the splits">
    Click **Save Splits**. Then click **Create Placement** or **Update Placement** to save the placement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Connekt only saves splits that add up to 100% of the fee. The bar above the rows shows how much is assigned and how much is left, or how far over you are. **Save Splits** stays disabled until the footer shows **Adds up to 100%**.
</Warning>

<Note>
  You must click **Save Splits** before you save the placement. While you have unsaved split changes, the placement's save button is disabled. Click **Reset** to discard your changes.
</Note>

<Info>
  The **Create Placement** dialog you get when converting from a **Submissions** tab uses a slightly different layout. Instead of **Add person**, it has **Add Recruiter**, **Add Referral** and **Add Researcher** buttons. You choose **Percentage (%)** or **Flat Fee** for each row, and quick buttons set 0%, 25%, 50%, 75% or 100%. **Split evenly** isn't available there. The same 100% rule and **Save Splits** button apply.
</Info>

If you change the fee later, percentage splits update their amounts to match the new fee.

## Why office and team matter

A split's office and team decide which office and team goals it counts towards. Office and team goals add up the split amounts for that office or team. Referral splits aren't counted. See [Goals and metric targets](/dashboard/goal-progress).

## The split estimate on the Deals page

Before a deal becomes a placement, the **Deal Details** panel on the **Deals** page shows an estimated split. Open it from **⋯ → View Details**, then look under **Commission Splits** on the **Commission** tab. The estimate uses who owns the candidate and the company, and who made the submission. Common cases:

| Situation | Estimated split |
|---|---|
| One person owns both the candidate and the company | 100% to that person |
| Different people own the candidate and the company | 80% company owner, 20% candidate owner |
| A third person made the submission (first placement with the company) | 20% company owner, 20% candidate owner, 60% to the person who made the submission |
| The company has no owner, and someone else made the submission | 20% candidate owner, 80% to the person who made the submission |

<Info>
  The estimate is only a guide for forecasting. It isn't copied to the placement. You still enter the actual splits yourself when you create the placement.
</Info>
