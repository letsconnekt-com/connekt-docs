A placement records a deal you've closed, with the fee, splits, dates and invoicing details. You can convert a submission into a placement, or add a placement directly from the **Deals** page. Both create the same record.

<Tip>
  If the candidate has a submission at **Offer Accepted** or **Hired**, convert it instead. Connekt fills in the candidate, job and company for you. See [Convert a submission to a placement](/submissions/converting-to-placement).
</Tip>

## Add a placement

You need the **Create Deals** permission.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the placements list">
    Click **Deals** in the sidebar, then click the **My Placements** or **All Placements** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Placement">
    Click **Add Placement**. The **Create New Placement** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the placement type">
    Pick a **Placement Type**. The type decides whether you need a candidate. See the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the candidate, company and job">
    Select the **Company** and **Job Position**. For types that need a candidate, also pick one in **Select Candidate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the placement details and fee">
    Complete the required fields listed below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the splits">
    In **Splits**, add each person who shares the fee and click **Save Splits**. See [Deal splits](/placements/deal-splits).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach documents (optional)">
    Upload files such as the offer letter or signed terms in **Placement Documents**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the placement">
    Click **Create Placement**. Connekt opens the new placement when it's saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Create Placement** stays disabled until the required fields are complete and your splits are saved. If you haven't saved your splits, the page shows "Save your split changes first".
</Note>

## Placement types

| Type | What it's for | Candidate |
|---|---|---|
| **Candidate** | A regular contingency placement. | Required |
| **Incentive Fee** | A reward or bonus for achieving specific milestones. | Required |
| **Success Fee** | A fee paid when a retainer completes. | Required |
| **Other** | Any placement not covered by the other types. | Required |
| **Upfront Fee** | A fee paid in advance, before the candidate starts. | Not used |
| **Cancellation Fee** | A fee charged when a placement is cancelled or ends early. | Not used |
| **Shortlist Fee** | A fee for providing a shortlist of qualified candidates. | Not used |

For types that don't use a candidate, the candidate compensation fields are disabled.

## Required fields

| Section | Required fields |
|---|---|
| **Placement Type** | **Placement Type** |
| **Candidate & Job Details** | **Company**, **Job Position**, and **Select Candidate** for types that need one |
| **Placement Details** | **Position Title**, **Status**, **Contract Type**, **Country** (where the candidate is placed), **Billing Country**, **Billing Currency** |
| **Compensation & Fees** | **Fee Value** and **Start Date**. For types with a candidate, also **Base Salary** and **Candidate Compensation Currency**. |
| **Splits** | At least one split, adding up to 100% of the fee |
| **Fiscal Information** | **Year**, **Quarter** and **Month** for reporting. These default to the current period. |

**Status** defaults to **Starting Soon**. **Contract Type** can be **Permanent**, **Contract**, **Temporary**, **Contingent**, **Project** or **Retained**.

<Note>
  Your admin can rename sections, hide sections or make more fields required in **Settings → Field Configuration**. See [Custom fields](/advanced/custom-fields).
</Note>

## Optional fields

- **Process Entry Date:** the date the candidate started interviewing.
- **Bonus** and **Other Compensation:** extra pay for the candidate.
- **Fee Terms:** how you calculated the fee, for example "30% of annual salary".
- **Invoice Date:** when you invoice the client. **Invoice on start date** is on by default. Turn it off to pick another date.
- **Payment Terms:** 15, 30, 45, 60 or 90 days. Connekt calculates the **Payment Due Date** from the invoice date.
- **Date of Birth** and **Home Address:** the candidate's details, shown when you've selected a candidate.

## After you create the placement

- The placement appears on the **My Placements** and **All Placements** tabs.
- If the candidate already had a submission for the job with no placement, Connekt links the two and sets the submission to **Hired**.
- The job's filled positions update. Its status changes to **Partially Filled** or **Filled** as positions fill.

To make changes later, open the placement and click **Edit**. See [Placement and payment statuses](/placements/placement-statuses).
