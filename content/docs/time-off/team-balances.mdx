The **Team Management** tab in **Time Off** is where admins look after everyone's leave. You can see each person's balances, correct them, check the history of changes, and record leave for someone else.

<Info>
  Time Off needs the **Team** plan or higher. The **Team Management** tab appears for Super Administrators, Administrators and Operations users.
</Info>

Go to **Time Off** and open **Team Management**. It has three views: **Team balances**, **Adjustment history** and **Submit on behalf**.

## View team balances

**Team balances** lists active team members with one row per leave type. Use **Search all team members** to find someone.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Employee** and **Region** | Who the balance belongs to, and the region their policies come from. |
| **Leave type** | The policy name. |
| **Granted** | Days granted this period. |
| **Carried forward** | Days carried over from the previous period. |
| **Available** | Days available this period. |
| **Taken** | Days already used. |
| **Remaining** | Days left. |

## Adjust a balance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the adjustment">
    On **Team balances**, click **Adjust** next to the person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the policy">
    In **Adjust Leave Balance**, pick the **Leave Policy** to change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the adjustment type">
    Pick **Credit days** to add days, **Debit days** to remove days, or **Correction** to fix a mistake. For a correction, use a negative number to lower the balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Enter the number of days. Decimals are allowed for part days. Connekt shows the **New remaining total**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a reason">
    Enter a **Reason for Adjustment** of at least 10 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Adjust Balance**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review adjustment history

**Adjustment history** records every change to a balance. It shows the **Date**, **Employee**, **Leave type**, **Change**, **Reason** and who it was **Recorded by**.

- Use **Search by user, policy, reason, or admin** to find entries.
- Filter by type: **Manual Addition**, **Manual Deduction**, **Correction**, **Rollover Adjustment** or **Migration**.

## Submit leave on someone's behalf

Use **Submit on behalf** to record leave for someone else, such as leave agreed by phone or a past absence.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the person and policy">
    Pick the **Team Member**, then their **Leave Policy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the dates">
    Pick the **First day** and **Last day**. Past dates are allowed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reason">
    Enter **Reason (internal)**. You can add an **Admin Note (optional)**, which is added to the reason for the audit trail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on approval">
    **Auto-approve** is on by default and approves the request straight away. Turn it off to leave the request **Pending**.

    If the person doesn't have enough balance, turn on **Skip balance check** to submit anyway.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit & Approve**, or **Submit Request** if auto-approve is off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Submissions on someone's behalf are logged and attributed to you.
</Note>

## View the approval hierarchy

Click **View Hierarchy** at the top of the Time Off page. The **Leave Approval Hierarchy** dialog shows your own approval rights and which roles can approve which. Team membership also matters. See [Approving leave requests](/time-off/approving-leave).
