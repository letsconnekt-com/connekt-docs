You request leave from **Time Off** in the sidebar. Connekt shows how many working days a request uses before you submit it, and your approver reviews it from their Leave Requests list.

<Info>
  Time Off needs the **Team** plan or higher. If you don't see **Time Off** in the sidebar, ask your admin.
</Info>

## Apply for leave

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Go to **Time Off** and click **Apply for Leave**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a leave policy">
    Pick a **Leave Policy**. The list shows the policies you've been given, with the days you have left for each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your dates">
    Pick a **Start Date** and **End Date**. You can choose dates from today up to one year ahead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the working days">
    Connekt shows **Working days deducted** and how much you'll have left. Weekends aren't counted. Public holidays in your region are excluded automatically, and the form lists any that fall in your dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reason">
    Enter a **Reason for Leave** of at least 10 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add people (optional)">
    Use **Calendar Invites (Optional)** to pick team members connected to your leave.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit Application**. Your request is **Pending** until someone approves or rejects it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  You can't submit a request if you don't have enough balance, or if it overlaps another pending or approved request.
</Warning>

<Note>
  If your policy list is empty or shows **No entitlement set up**, your leave hasn't been set up yet. Ask your admin to set up your leave policies.
</Note>

## Who approves your request

Your request goes to whoever can approve it under your workspace's team structure. Usually that's a manager of your team in **Settings → Organization**, or an Administrator. See [Approving leave requests](/time-off/approving-leave) for the full rules.

Click **View Hierarchy** on the Time Off page to see which roles can approve which requests.

## Track your requests

Your **Overview** tab shows:

- **Total available days** across all your leave types.
- Your balance for each leave type: **Granted this year**, **Carried forward**, **Available this period**, **Taken so far** and **Remaining**.
- **Upcoming leave** with the status of each request.
- **Public holidays** for your region.

The **Leave History** tab lists all your past requests and holidays.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting for approval. |
| **Approved** | Approved. The days have been taken from your balance. |
| **Rejected** | Not approved. **Leave History** shows the approver's reason next to the request. |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled the request. |

You get a notification when your request is approved or rejected.

## Cancel a request

You can cancel a request that's **Pending** or **Approved**.

1. Go to **Time Off → Overview**.
2. Under **Upcoming leave**, click the cancel icon next to the request.

The request is cancelled straight away. There's no confirmation step. If the request was already approved, the days go back into your balance.

## Contractors

If your user profile's employment type is **Contractor**, the form is called **Request Time Off**. Contractor requests are approved automatically.
