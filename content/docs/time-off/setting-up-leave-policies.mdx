Leave policies decide how much leave people get and how it builds up and carries over. Each policy covers one leave type in one region, or one person. Set up your policies before your team starts requesting leave, so balances are right from the start.

<Info>
  Time Off needs the **Team** plan or higher. To manage leave policies, you need the **System Administration** permission. By default, only Super Administrators and Administrators have it.
</Info>

## Where to find leave policies

Go to **Settings → Leave Policies**. There are three tabs:

- **Policy Configurations**: create and edit policies.
- **Delegation**: record who covers your approvals. See [Delegating leave approvals](/time-off/delegating-approvals).
- **Admin**: give people their balances and run grant, rollover and expiry jobs.

## Create a policy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new policy">
    On **Policy Configurations**, click **Create Policy Configuration**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the basic information">
    - **Policy Name**, for example "UK Annual Leave". This is what people see when they request leave.
    - **Leave Type**: **Annual Leave**, **Birthday Leave** or **Personal Day**.
    - **Region**: the country the policy applies to. People get the policies for the **Region** on their user profile.
    - **Override For (User-Specific)**: leave blank for a region-wide policy. Pick one person to create a policy that replaces the region-wide one for them.
    - **Effective From** and **Effective To**: the dates the policy applies. Leave **Effective To** blank for no end date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the initial entitlement">
    - **Initial Days Granted**: the days granted each period.
    - **Eligibility Wait Days**: how long new starters wait before they can take leave, for example a 90-day probation.
    - **Accrual Period Type**: **Anniversary** (granted on each person's hire date anniversary), **Calendar Year** (granted on 1 January) or **Fiscal Year**.
    - **Pro-Rate First Year**: give mid-year starters a proportional amount. This applies to **Calendar Year** policies.
    - **Bonus Days** and **Bonus Eligibility Days**: extra days after someone reaches a length of service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set annual increases (optional)">
    Turn on **Has Annual Increase** to add days for each year of service. Set **Days Added Per Year** and a **Max Days Cap**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set carryover (optional)">
    Turn on **Can Carry Over** to let unused leave roll into the next period. Choose a **Carryover Expiry Type** and set **Max Carryover Days**. Leave the maximum blank for no limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Policy**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The form also has a **Cash Out & Special Features** section with optional advanced settings.

<Tip>
  Create one policy per leave type for each region you employ people in. For someone with a special arrangement, create a user-specific override instead of a new region-wide policy.
</Tip>

## Give people their balances

Connekt sets up leave balances automatically for people you invite. For everyone else, and after you add a policy, use the **Admin** tab.

| Button | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **Set Up All Users Without Complete Data** | Some people have missing or zero balances, for example after you first switch on Time Off. People who are already set up are skipped. |
| **Create Missing Entitlements for New Policies** | You've added a new policy and existing users need balances for it. Existing balances aren't changed. |

Both buttons ask you to confirm first.

<Warning>
  The **Admin** tab's operations affect everyone in your workspace. Read each confirmation carefully before you continue.
</Warning>

## Other Admin tab tools

- **Grant & Expiry Management** shows **Upcoming Grants** (anniversaries in the next 30 days) and **Upcoming Expirations** (grants expiring in the next 90 days). Grants and expirations normally run automatically. Use **Process Anniversary Grants**, **Process Calendar Year Grants** or **Process Expirations** to run them now.
- **Year-End Rollover Processing** carries unused leave into the next year based on your carryover rules. Enter the **Year to Process** and click **Process Rollover**.
- **Expire Carried Forward Balances** removes carried-over days that are past their expiry date. Click **Expire Balances**.

## Edit or deactivate a policy

On **Policy Configurations**, use the icons in the **Actions** column:

- **Preview calculation** shows how the policy works out.
- **Edit policy** opens the form. Click **Update Policy** to save.
- **Deactivate policy** stops the policy being used. The **Status** column shows **Inactive**.

To change one person's balance, use the **Team Management** tab in Time Off. See [Managing team leave balances](/time-off/team-balances).
