If you're going to be away, you can create a delegation that names a manager to cover your leave approvals for a set period. Connekt keeps the delegation with its dates and reason, and shows it to both of you.

<Info>
  Delegation is in **Settings → Leave Policies → Delegation**. It needs the **Team** plan or higher and the **System Administration** permission, which Super Administrators and Administrators have by default.
</Info>

## Before you delegate

Connekt checks every approval against roles and teams. See [Approving leave requests](/time-off/approving-leave). A delegation doesn't change those checks. It records who is covering for you.

Your delegate can only approve requests they already have the rights to approve. To make sure they can cover your team, make them a manager of that team in **Settings → Organization**. See [Teams and hierarchy](/team/teams-and-hierarchy).

## Create a delegation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Delegation">
    Go to **Settings → Leave Policies** and open the **Delegation** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a delegation">
    Click **Create Delegation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your delegate">
    In **Delegate To**, select a manager. If no one is listed, you'll see **No eligible managers found**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the dates">
    Pick a **Start Date** and **End Date**. The start date can't be in the past, and the end date must be after the start date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reason (optional)">
    Use **Reason (Optional)** to add context, such as "Annual vacation".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Delegation** in the dialog. The delegation appears under **My Delegations**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Your delegations can't overlap. If you already have an active delegation for some of those dates, edit it instead.
</Warning>

## See your delegations

The **Delegation** tab has two lists:

- **My Delegations**: cover you've set up, with the delegate, dates, reason and an **Active** badge.
- **Delegated to Me**: cover other people have set up with you as the delegate.

Both lists show active delegations only.

## Edit, pause or delete a delegation

1. Under **My Delegations**, click the edit icon next to the delegation.
2. In **Edit Delegation**, change the dates or reason, then click **Update Delegation**.

To pause it, turn off **Active** and click **Update Delegation**. To remove it, click **Delete**, then confirm with **Delete**.

<Note>
  Paused and deleted delegations no longer appear in either list.
</Note>
