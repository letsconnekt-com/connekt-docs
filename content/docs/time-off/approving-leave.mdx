Leave approvals follow your role and your team structure. Admins can approve most requests. Managers and Operations users approve requests from the teams they manage in **Settings → Organization**.

<Info>
  Time Off needs the **Team** plan or higher. Teams are set up in [Teams and hierarchy](/team/teams-and-hierarchy).
</Info>

## Who can approve whose leave

| Your role | Whose requests you can approve |
|---|---|
| **Super Administrator** | Anyone's. |
| **Administrator** | Anyone's, except a Super Administrator's. |
| **Operations** | People on teams where you're a manager. You can't approve Administrators, Super Administrators or other Operations users. |
| **Manager** | People on teams where you're a manager. |
| Any other role | No one. |

Nobody can approve their own request.

<Note>
  Being a team manager in **Settings → Organization** is different from having the **Manager** role. Operations and Manager users need both: the role, and a manager spot on the requester's team.
</Note>

## Find requests to review

Go to **Time Off** and open the **Leave Requests** tab. The tab appears for Super Administrators, Administrators, Operations users and Managers who have someone to approve.

Use the controls at the top to narrow the list:

- Status filters: **All**, **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected** and **Cancelled**, each with a count.
- **Search by type, ID or approver**.
- A year filter.
- **All requesters**, to show one person's requests.

Each row shows the dates, the requester, the leave type, the number of days and the status. Click the arrow at the end of a row to see the **Request ID**, **Submitted** date, **Approver**, days **Deducted** and **Note**.

## Approve a request

On a **Pending** row, click **Approve**.

The days are taken from the person's balance, and they get a notification.

<Warning>
  Connekt checks the balance again when you approve. If the person no longer has enough days, the approval fails.
</Warning>

## Reject a request

1. On a **Pending** row, click **Reject**.
2. Enter a reason in the box. The reason is shown to the employee.
3. Click **Confirm reject**.

The person gets a notification, and nothing is taken from their balance.

## View a team member's time off

On the **Overview** tab, use the **My Time Off** dropdown to see a team member's balances and upcoming leave. On **Leave History**, pick someone under **View team member history**.

Administrators and Operations users can pick anyone. Managers can pick members of the teams they manage.

<Tip>
  You can also review requests in the Connekt iOS app. See [Using the mobile app](/mobile/using-the-mobile-app).
</Tip>
