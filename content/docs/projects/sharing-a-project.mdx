You can share a project with a client through a secure link. Connekt emails the link to your client, and they open the shortlist in their browser without a Connekt account. Clients can review candidates, give a decision on each one, leave feedback, view CVs and export the list as a CSV.

## Before you share

- **Only the project's creator can share it.** Teammates with **Can Edit** access can open the share dialog, but sharing fails for them.
- **The project must be linked to a company.** If it isn't, you pick one in the share dialog.

## Share a project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share dialog">
    Open the project, click the **⋯** menu in the header and click **Share**. You can also go to the **Settings** tab and click **Share with Company**. The **Share with Company** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the company">
    Under **Select company**, check the **Company**. Click **Change** to pick a different one. You can also pick a **Job (optional)**. Your choice is saved to the project when you send the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what the client can see">
    Under **What the client can see**, turn each option on or off. See the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the client's details">
    Under **Generate access link**, enter a **Client name (optional)** and the client's **Company email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the expiry">
    Under **Link expires**, pick **7 days**, **14 days**, **30 days**, **60 days**, **90 days** or **Never**. The default is **30 days**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate & Send">
    Connekt emails the link to your client and copies it to your clipboard. The link also appears in the dialog so you can copy it again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The email includes the project name, the link, the expiry date and your name. Once you share a project, it shows a **Shared with Client** badge in the **Projects** list and on the project.

## Choose what the client can see

All seven options are on by default. They save as soon as you switch them, and they apply to every client link for the project.

| Option | What the client sees |
|---|---|
| **Contact info** | Email, mobile and LinkedIn URL |
| **Salary** | Current salary and currency |
| **Resume** | A button to view the candidate's CV |
| **Client Feedback** | The feedback thread on each candidate, where the client can post comments |
| **Status** | Each candidate's stage in the project |
| **Status history** | A timeline of status changes and the client's past decisions |
| **Last Update** | Your update log for each candidate |

<Warning>
  Contact details and salary are shared by default. Turn off **Contact info** and **Salary** before you click **Generate & Send** if the client shouldn't see them.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  Your team's candidate **Notes** in the project are always visible to the client as **Recruiter Notes**. There's no option to hide them. Only add notes you're happy for the client to read.
</Warning>

## One link per email address

Each email address gets its own link. To share with several people at the client, generate a link for each email address.

If you share again with an email address that already has a link, Connekt sends a new link and the old one stops working. The expiry resets to your new choice. This also restores access if you'd revoked it.

## Manage active links

Every link that hasn't been revoked appears under **Active access** in the share dialog. Each entry shows:

- The client's name and email
- **Shared by** and the date it was shared
- The expiry date, or **Never expires**
- How many times the client has viewed it, or **Not yet viewed**
- An **Expired** badge, or a badge showing the days left when fewer than 7 remain

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Change the expiry or client name">
    Click the calendar icon to open **Edit access**. Update the **Client name** or pick a new **Expires** option, then click **Save changes**. The client keeps the same link. The new expiry counts from today.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Copy the link again">
    Click the copy icon. You can't copy an expired link. Update its expiry first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Revoke access">
    Click the bin icon, then click **Yes**. The link stops working straight away.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Only the project's creator can change, revoke or create links.

## What your client sees

When your client opens the link, they see the project name, company and description, plus the date their access expires. Below that, the **Candidate Shortlist** lists each candidate with their current role and company. Other columns depend on the options you turned on.

Clients see client-friendly stage names. For example, **Rejected** shows as **Not Selected**, **No Reply** shows as **Did Not Reply**, and **Candidate Declined** shows as **Candidate Not Interested**.

Your client can:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Give a decision" icon="circle-check">
    Under **Next Steps**, choose **Move Forward**, **Put on Hold** or **End Process**, add an optional note and click **Submit**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Leave feedback" icon="pen-nib">
    Post comments on a candidate in the **Client Feedback** thread. Needs the **Client Feedback** option.
  </Card>
  <Card title="View CVs" icon="file">
    Click **View** in the **CV** column. Needs the **Resume** option.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Export a CSV" icon="file-lines">
    Click **Export CSV** to download the shortlist.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Clicking a candidate opens a details panel with their contact details, compensation, feedback, update log, recruiter notes, status history and the client's decision history, depending on your settings.

<Note>
  The **View** button opens the most recently uploaded file on the candidate's record. If you upload other documents after the CV, the client sees the newest one instead.
</Note>

<Info>
  The CSV includes each candidate's name, current title and company. It includes contact details, salary and stage only when those options are on. A **Remarks** column holds the latest recruiter note.
</Info>

A client's decision applies to the candidate's current stage. When you change the candidate's status in the project, the decision resets so the client can decide again.

## See your client's feedback

Client activity shows up in the project's **Candidates** tab:

- **Client Status** shows the latest decision: **Moved Forward**, **On Hold**, **Process Ended** or **Pending**.
- **Client Feedback** opens the client's comments. You can read them but not reply there.
- **History** lists each decision, labelled **By Client**.

The project's creator also gets a notification:

- **Client feedback: …** when the client submits a decision. This also arrives by email.
- **New client feedback** when the client posts a comment.

Client decisions don't change the candidate's status or any submission. Update the status yourself when you're ready. See [Notification preferences](/integrations/notification-preferences).

## When a link stops working

A link stops working when it expires, when you revoke it, or when you send a new link to the same email address.

When your client opens a link that no longer works, they see **Unable to Load Project** with the reason, such as "Access token has expired" or "Access has been revoked". To give them access again, update the link's expiry under **Active access**, or share the project with their email again.
