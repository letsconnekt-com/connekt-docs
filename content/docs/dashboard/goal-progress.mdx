Connekt has two ways to measure performance against expectations. **Goals** are revenue targets for the whole company, an office or a team over a date range. **Metric targets** set benchmarks for individual metrics, such as CV send-outs per week, and appear on each person's dashboard cards.

You manage both in **Settings**, under **System**. You need the **System Administration** permission, which Admins have by default.

## Goals

### Create a goal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Goals">
    Go to **Settings → Goals**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Goal">
    Click **Create Goal**. The **Create New Goal** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the goal and set the target">
    Enter a **Goal Name**, the **Target Amount** and the **Currency**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the dates">
    Choose the **Start Date** and **End Date**. The end date must be after the start date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the scope">
    Under **Scope**, choose **Organization-wide**, **Specific office(s)** or **Specific team(s)**. For an office or team goal, select one or more offices or teams. For example, select several offices to model a region like APAC.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the goal">
    Click **Create Goal**. The new goal is active straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How active goals work

You can have many goals, but only one active goal for any overlapping scope:

- **Organization-wide:** creating or activating one deactivates the other active organization-wide goal.
- **Office goals:** creating or activating one deactivates other active office goals that share an office.
- **Team goals:** creating or activating one deactivates other active team goals that share a team.

Goals that don't overlap, such as goals for two different offices, can be active at the same time.

<Note>
  You can only activate a goal while its end date hasn't passed and its progress is under 100%. Only Admins can change an organization-wide goal.
</Note>

### Manage goals

The **Goals** page groups goals into **Organization**, **Offices** and **Teams**, with active goals listed first. On each goal you can:

- Use the switch to activate or deactivate it.
- Click the edit icon to change its name, target, currency, dates or scope.
- Click the delete icon to delete it permanently.

### What counts towards a goal

- Placements reported between the goal's start and end dates count towards it.
- Backed-out placements don't count.
- An organization-wide goal adds up the full fee of each placement.
- An office or team goal adds up only the split amounts credited to that office or team. Referral splits don't count. See [Deal splits](/placements/deal-splits).
- Amounts are converted into the goal's currency.

## Track goal progress on the dashboard

The **Goal Progress** block on **Home** shows your active goals. The header shows the number of active goals and how much of the combined target has been achieved. If there are goals at more than one level, tabs let you switch between **Company**, **Offices** and **Teams**.

Each goal shows its **Progress**, **Target Amount**, **Achieved** and **Remaining** amounts, plus the days left. A goal shows **Achieved** once it reaches its target, or how many days overdue it is once its end date passes.

Which goals you see:

- By default, you see the organization-wide goal plus the active office and team goals within your view. For most people, that's the goals for their own office and teams. Admins see every goal. Office managers and team leads also see goals for the offices and teams they manage.
- If you pick an office, team or person in the scope menu, the block shows the goals for that scope.

<Note>
  The block needs the **Dashboard: Goal Progress** permission. If no goals are active, the block doesn't appear.
</Note>

## Metric targets

Metric targets set the benchmark shown on metric cards, such as **Revenue (QTD)** or **CV Send Outs**. Each card shows the **Target** and a rating of **GOOD**, **OKAY** or **POOR**.

Targets work in two layers:

- **Org-Level Defaults** apply to everyone. Revenue defaults are matched by office, currency and employee level. Other metrics apply company-wide unless overridden.
- **Per-user overrides** replace the defaults for one person. Only add exceptions here. Everyone else inherits the organization defaults.

### Metrics you can target

| Metric | Periods |
|---|---|
| **Candidate Meetings** | Per week, month, quarter or year |
| **CV Send-Outs** | Per week, month, quarter or year |
| **CV to Interview Ratio (%)** | Per week, month, quarter or year |
| **BD Activity:** Reach Outs, Meetings or Signed | Per week, month, quarter or year |
| **Revenue QTD** | Per quarter only |
| **Revenue YTD** | Per year only |

### Add an organization default

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Metric Targets">
    Go to **Settings → Metric Targets**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add target">
    In **Org-Level Defaults**, click **Add target**. The **Add org default** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the metric and period">
    Pick the **Metric**. For **BD Activity**, also pick the **Detail**. Then choose the **Period**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the scope for revenue">
    For **Revenue QTD** or **Revenue YTD**, also choose the **Office / Region**, **Currency** and **Employee level**. All three are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Enter the **Target value** and click **Save target**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Add a per-user override

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the person">
    In **Per-user overrides**, search for the team member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add target">
    Click **Add target** on their row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the target">
    Choose the **Metric** and **Period**, enter the **Target value** and click **Save target**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

People without an override show "No overrides set. Using org defaults or role-based thresholds." To remove a target, click its delete icon.
