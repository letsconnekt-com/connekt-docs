**Home** is your dashboard in Connekt. It shows your targets, activity, pipeline and revenue as a set of blocks you can arrange yourself. Click **Home** in the sidebar to open it.

To open **Home**, you need at least one of these permissions: **View Personal Dashboard**, **View Team Dashboard** or **View Company Dashboard**.

## Choose whose numbers you see

If you're an admin, or you manage an office or lead a team, a scope menu appears at the top right. It starts on **My Dashboard**. Open it to pick:

- **Office:** everyone in an office.
- **Teams:** everyone in a team.
- **Users:** one person, for example before a one-to-one.

The menu shows your choice, such as **London › Sales**. Clear it to return to **My Dashboard**. If you don't manage anyone, you only see your own numbers and there's no scope menu.

## Choose the currency for figures

Click the currency button next to the scope menu. The **Show figures in** menu offers:

- **Company currency:** official company figures. This is the default.
- **My currency:** the currency on your profile.
- The selected office's currency, when you've picked an office.

The currency only changes how amounts display. It doesn't change which deals count. An admin sets the company currency in **Settings → Revenue & FX**. See [Offices and currency](/advanced/offices-and-currency).

## Change a card's period

Many cards and charts have their own period selector, so you can view each one over a different timeframe. Choose **Week**, **Month**, **Quarter** or **Year**. For month, quarter and year, you can also pick which one, for example a past quarter.

## What's on the dashboard

The default layout includes these blocks:

| Block | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Goal Progress** | Progress towards your active revenue goals. See [Goals and metric targets](/dashboard/goal-progress). |
| **Revenue (QTD)** and **Revenue (YTD)** | Quarter-to-date and year-to-date revenue, against your target. |
| **Candidate Meetings** | Candidate meetings, against your target. |
| **CV Send Outs** | CVs sent to clients, against your target. |
| **CV to Interview Ratio** | How many CVs turn into interviews, against your target. |
| **BD Activity** | Reach outs, meetings and signed companies. |
| **My Metrics** | Your candidates, companies, jobs and interviews. |
| **Recruiter Revenue Billboard** | Top recruiters ranked by individual revenue. |
| **Candidate Activity** | Candidate activity over time. |
| **Recruitment Funnel** | Submissions, interviews and placements. |
| **Revenue by Month** | Monthly revenue breakdown. |
| **Revenue by Region** | Regional revenue leaderboard. |
| **Top Recruiters by Candidate Introductions** | A leaderboard of recruiters by candidate introductions. |

You can add these extra blocks:

| Block | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Top Performers** | Top sourcers, owners and recruiters. |
| **Conversion Metrics** | Submission-to-placement conversion rates. |
| **Upcoming Interviews** | Interviews scheduled this week. |
| **Billing Health** | Billed, paid, outstanding and overdue invoices by period. |
| **Hiring Funnel** | Stage-by-stage conversion from submission to placement. |
| **Pipeline Forecast** | Active deals by stage and offers in progress. |
| **Org Trends** | 12-month company-wide trends for candidates, companies and placements. |
| **Company Overview** | Company-wide candidate, company, placement and conversion metrics. |

Revenue figures leave out backed-out placements.

## Who sees which blocks

Each block has its own permission, such as **Dashboard: Goal Progress** or **Dashboard: Revenue (YTD)**. If your role doesn't have a block's permission, the block doesn't appear on your dashboard or in **Customize**. On the **Team** plan or higher, admins can change these permissions in **Settings → Permissions**. See [Customising permissions](/team/customising-permissions).

<Note>
  **Billing Health**, **Top Performers** and **Conversion Metrics** use advanced analytics, which needs the **Business** plan. See [Plans compared](/billing/plans-compared).
</Note>

## Customise your dashboard

Your layout is personal. Changes you make don't affect anyone else.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Customize">
    Click **Customize** at the top right. The **Customize Dashboard** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder blocks">
    Under **Visible blocks (drag to reorder)**, drag blocks into the order you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hide or show blocks">
    Use the switch on a block to hide it. Hidden blocks move to **Hidden**, where you can switch them back on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add blocks">
    Under **Add blocks**, click **Add** next to any block you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save layout**. To go back to the default layout, click **Reset** before you save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you have the **System Administration** permission, **Customize** also shows **Targets & goals**, with shortcuts to **Goals** and **Metric targets**.
</Tip>
