Connekt gives you four ways to find records. Global search and list filters find records that match what you type. Ask AI and AI job matching understand meaning, so they can surface good candidates even when the wording doesn't match exactly. This page helps you pick the right tool.

## At a glance

| | Global search | Filters and Boolean search | Ask AI | AI job matching |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Where** | **Search everything...** bar at the top of every page, or **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** | Search box and **Filter** button on each list page | **Ask AI** button next to the search bar | **Matching candidates** card on a job, **Matching open jobs** card on a candidate |
| **What you enter** | A name, email, company or title | Keywords with AND / OR / NOT, plus field conditions | A plain-language request or question | Nothing. Click **Find matches** |
| **What it searches** | Candidates, companies, contacts, jobs and placements | One list at a time | Candidates, plus questions about companies, contacts, jobs, submissions and placements | Candidates for one job, or open jobs for one candidate |
| **Results** | Records grouped by type | A filtered table you can sort and bookmark | Ranked candidates with reasons and flags, or a written answer | A ranked list with a match score and reasoning |
| **Best for** | Jumping straight to a record you know | Building a precise, repeatable list | Open-ended sourcing and quick data questions | Shortlisting for a specific role |

## Global search

Use global search when you already know which record you want. Type a name, email or job title and open the result.

When the box is empty, **Recent Searches** shows the records you opened from search recently.

[Learn more about global search](/search/search-history)

## Filters and Boolean search

Use list filters when you need an exact, repeatable list. For example: candidates with `python OR golang` in their profile, **Country** is "Singapore", and **Status** is any of your active statuses.

On Candidates, Companies, Contacts and Jobs, your search and filters stay in the page URL, so you can bookmark the view. You can also choose and save which columns the table shows.

[Learn more about filters and Boolean search](/search/filters-and-boolean-search)

## Ask AI

Use Ask AI when you'd rather describe what you need than build a query. For example: "Senior React developers in London with fintech experience." Ask AI returns a ranked shortlist with the reasons behind each match. You can refine it with follow-up questions, attach a job for context, and add candidates straight to a project.

Ask AI also answers questions about your data, such as "Which jobs have the most active submissions?"

[Learn more about Ask AI](/search/ask-ai)

## AI job matching

Use AI job matching when you're working on one job or one candidate.

- On a job, the **Matching candidates** card ranks candidates against the job's title, skills and description.
- On a candidate, the **Matching open jobs** card ranks open jobs against the candidate's profile and CV.

Choose how many results you want, such as **Top 5**, then click **Find matches**. Click **Regenerate** to run it again after the job or profile changes.

[Learn more about AI job matching](/candidates/matching-candidates-to-jobs)
