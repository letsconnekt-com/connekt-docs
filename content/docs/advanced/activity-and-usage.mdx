Connekt keeps two kinds of activity record. The **History** tab on a candidate or job shows what happened to that record. The workspace **Audit Log** shows actions taken by users across the whole workspace, such as sign-ins, record changes and document events.

## See a record's history

Candidates and jobs have a **History** tab. Projects have a similar **Activity** tab.

Each entry shows what happened, who did it, and when. Entries include:

- The record being created or updated
- Status changes
- Submissions to jobs and submission status changes
- Placements
- Notes, comments and logged activities
- Files added or deleted
- Off-limits changes

<Tip>
  Go to the History tab first when you need to answer questions like "When did this candidate's status change?" or "Who moved this job to a new status?"
</Tip>

To see how a deal moved through its stages, go to **Deals**, open the deal's **⋯** menu, click **View Details**, then open the **Stage History** tab. See [Deals pipeline](/submissions/pipeline-board).

## Review the audit log

The Audit Log lists user actions across your workspace in the **Activity Feed**.

<Info>
  The Audit Log is available on the **Team** and **Business** plans. See [Plans compared](/billing/plans-compared).
</Info>

<Note>
  Only Super Admins and Admins can open the Audit Log by default. It needs the **System Administration** permission.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Audit Log">
    Go to **Settings → Audit Log**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the feed">
    Search by user or event with **Search user or event…**. Pick an event type from **All events**, and a time range from **All time**, **Last hour**, **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 3 days** or **Last 7 days**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the entries">
    Each row shows the **Time**, the **User**, the **Event**, and **Details**. Hover over a detail to see its full value, or over **+N more** to see all the details for that event.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click **Refresh** to load the latest events. Click **Clear** to remove your filters.

### What the audit log records

The feed includes events such as:

- Sign-ins and sign-outs
- Candidates, companies, contacts, jobs and placements being created, updated or deleted
- Ownership transfers and merges
- Submissions and submission status changes
- Notes, and attachments being uploaded, previewed, downloaded or deleted
- Documents being created, submitted for review, signed, cancelled or deleted
- Leave requests being submitted, approved, rejected or cancelled

Details can include the IP address, approximate location, and browser or device used.
