You can export a copy of your workspace data from **Settings → Data Import/Export**. Connekt builds the export in the background and emails you a ZIP file when it's ready.

<Note>
  Data export needs the **System Administration** permission. Super Admins and Admins have it by default.
</Note>

## Export your data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data Import/Export">
    Go to **Settings → Data Import/Export**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the export">
    Under **Export data**, click **Export**. A message confirms that the export has started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    When the export is ready, Connekt emails the address on your account. The subject is "Your Connekt data export completed successfully", and the ZIP file is attached to the email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can keep working while the export runs. If you stay on the page, Connekt also shows a message when the email has been sent.

<Tip>
  Large workspaces with many attachments take longer to export. You don't need to keep the page open.
</Tip>

## What's included

The ZIP file contains one CSV file per record type, plus a folder of attachments for each:

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `users.csv` | Your team members, with role, teams, employment details and currency |
| `companies.csv` | Your companies, including website, industries, BD status, owner and billing details |
| `candidates.csv` | Your candidates, including contact details, employment, salary, skills, status and owner |
| `jobs.csv` | Your jobs, including company, status, positions, location, skills and salary range |
| `contacts.csv` | Your contacts, including company, job title, stage and owner |

Each CSV has a `documents` column that lists the file names of that record's attachments. The files themselves are in the matching folder, such as `candidates/`.

<Info>
  The export doesn't include placements, deals, notes or custom field values. To import data instead, see [Bulk import](/candidates/bulk-import).
</Info>

## If something goes wrong

- If the export finishes but the email can't be sent, Connekt shows an error. Start the export again.
- If the export fails, Connekt shows an error message. Try again, and contact support if it keeps failing.

For help, see [Getting help](/getting-started/getting-help).
