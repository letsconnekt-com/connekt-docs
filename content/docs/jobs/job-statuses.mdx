Every job has one status that shows where the role stands. New jobs start as **Open**. You can change the status yourself, and Connekt also updates it automatically when placements are added, changed or removed.

## The 8 statuses

| Status | Typical use |
|---|---|
| **Open** | The role is live and you're recruiting. This is the default for new jobs. |
| **In Progress** | Recruiting is underway, with candidates moving through the process. |
| **Partially Filled** | Some positions on a multi-hire job are filled. Recruiting continues for the rest. |
| **Filled** | All positions are filled. |
| **On Hold** | Recruiting is paused, for example during a client hiring freeze. |
| **Closed** | The role is no longer active. |
| **Cancelled** | The client called off the role. |
| **Inactive** | The job isn't being worked right now. |

## Change a job's status

You can change status in three places.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="On the job">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the job">
        Click **Jobs** in the sidebar and open the job.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click the status badge">
        Click the status badge in the job header. The **Change Job Status** menu opens.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pick a status">
        Click the new status.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm">
        Click **Change Status** in the confirmation dialog.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="In the Jobs list">
    In the **Jobs** list, use the dropdown in the **Status** column. The change saves as soon as you pick a status.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="For several jobs">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select jobs">
        In the **Jobs** list, tick the checkbox next to each job.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Change the status">
        Click **Change Status**, then pick the new status. It applies to every selected job.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Status changes you make are recorded in the job's **History** tab.

## Automatic status changes

Connekt counts a job's active placements and compares them with its **Number of Positions**. A placement is active when its status is **Already started** or **Starting Soon**.

| Active placements | Status becomes |
|---|---|
| None | **Open** |
| Some, but fewer than the number of positions | **Partially Filled** |
| Equal to the number of positions | **Filled** |

This check runs when you create a placement for the job, change a placement's status or delete a placement. For jobs with more than one position, you can also run it yourself with the refresh button next to the status badge.

<Warning>
  The automatic update replaces whatever status the job had, including one you set by hand. For example, if you set a job to **Closed** and then delete its only placement, the job goes back to **Open**. Check the status after you change placements.
</Warning>

## Which jobs accept new submissions

On a job's **Submissions** tab, **Submit Candidate** only works when the job is **Open**, **In Progress** or **Partially Filled**. For any other status the button is disabled and shows "Cannot submit candidates to a closed job". You also can't change submission statuses on that tab until the job is back in one of those three statuses.

<Note>
  This check only applies on the job's own page. When you submit from a candidate's **Submissions** tab with **Submit to Job**, you can pick jobs in any status. Check the job's status before you submit from the candidate side. See [Submitting a candidate](/submissions/submitting-a-candidate).
</Note>

AI matching also uses status. **Matching open jobs** on a candidate record only suggests jobs that are **Open** or **Partially Filled**. See [Matching candidates to jobs](/candidates/matching-candidates-to-jobs).

## Finding jobs by status

Changing a status never deletes a job. Closed, cancelled and inactive jobs keep their submissions, placements, notes and history. To find them, filter the **Jobs** list by **Status**.

<Info>
  Users with permission to delete jobs can delete them from the job's **⋯** menu or with the **Actions** bulk menu. Deleted jobs no longer appear in Connekt. If you only want to stop working a role, change its status instead.
</Info>
