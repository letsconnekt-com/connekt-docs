Every job in Connekt belongs to a company. You can start a job from a job posting link or an uploaded job description, and Connekt reads it and fills in the form for you. You can also start from a blank form. Either way, you review every field before you save.

<Note>
  You need permission to create jobs. If you don't see **Add Job**, ask an admin to check your role. See [User roles](/team/user-roles).
</Note>

## Choose how to start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Jobs">
    Click **Jobs** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Job">
    Click **Add Job**. The **Create New Job** page asks how you want to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a method">
    Choose one of the three options described below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Paste a job link" icon="link">
    Paste the URL of a job posting. Works with LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor and most company career pages.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Upload a job description" icon="file-lines">
    Upload a PDF, DOC or DOCX file, such as the client's job spec.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Start from scratch" icon="pencil">
    Open a blank form and fill in the details yourself.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  To import many jobs at once, click the arrow next to **Add Job** and choose **Import CSV**. See [Bulk import](/candidates/bulk-import).
</Tip>

## Paste a job link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Paste a job link">
    Click **Paste a job link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the URL">
    Paste the posting's address into **Job posting URL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Extract">
    Click **Extract**. Connekt shows "Reading the posting…" while it works. Pages that need extra rendering take a little longer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the form">
    When extraction finishes, the job form opens with the details filled in. Check each field, pick a **Company**, then click **Create Job**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Upload a job description

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Upload a job description">
    Click **Upload a job description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the file">
    Drag and drop a PDF or Word document, or click to browse. You can upload one file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Extract">
    Click **Extract**. Connekt reads the file and opens the job form with the details filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the form">
    Check each field, pick a **Company**, then click **Create Job**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What AI fills in

When you use a link or a file, Connekt fills in these fields when it finds them in the posting:

- **Job Title**
- **Job Description**
- **Skills**
- **Salary Currency**, **Minimum Salary** and **Maximum Salary**
- **Number of Positions**
- **Job Type**
- **Job Level**
- **Country**, **City** and **Remote Job**

<Warning>
  AI never picks the **Company**. You always choose it yourself. Always check the filled-in fields before you save, because postings vary and some details may be missing or misread.
</Warning>

## When a link or file can't be read

If extraction fails, you see a **Failed to extract job details** message with the reason. Common reasons include:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| Could not find a readable job posting at this URL. The page may require a login, use a format we can't render, or the link may not point directly to a job posting. | Check that the link opens the job posting itself, not a search results page. If the page needs a login, copy the job description into a file and upload it instead. |
| No text could be extracted from the job posting URL. The page may be inaccessible or protected. | The site blocked Connekt from reading the page. Upload the job description as a file instead. |
| This document doesn't appear to contain a readable job posting. It may be corrupted, a scanned image, or unrelated content. | Upload a text-based PDF or Word file. Scanned images can't be read. |
| No text could be extracted from the document. It may be a scanned image or corrupted. | Save the document again as a text-based PDF or DOCX and retry. |
| The job extraction service is temporarily unavailable. Please try again in a moment. | Wait a moment and try again, or use **Start from scratch**. |

To try a different method, click **Start over** on the extraction screen.

## Job form fields

The form has the same fields whichever method you choose. Only **Job Title** and **Company** are required.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Basic Information">
    - **Job Title** (required)
    - **Company** (required). Search for an existing company. To add a new one first, see [Adding a company](/companies/adding-a-company).
    - **Job Type**: Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Temporary, Internship or Freelance
    - **Status**: defaults to **Open**. See [Job statuses](/jobs/job-statuses).
    - **Date Opened**: defaults to today
    - **Target Start Date**
    - **Number of Positions**: defaults to 1
    - **Job Level**
    - **Salary Currency**, **Minimum Salary** and **Maximum Salary**
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Job Description">
    The full description candidates will read. Click **Extract from URL** to replace the description with text pulled from a job posting link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Location & Requirements">
    - **Country**: defaults to your own country
    - **City**
    - **Remote Job**
    - **Industries**
    - **Skills**
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Languages">
    Add each required **Language** and its **Proficiency**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Job Documents">
    Upload the client's job spec or other supporting files. They're saved to the job's **Attachments** tab.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Your admin may have added custom fields, hidden some fields or made others required. See [Custom fields](/advanced/custom-fields).

<Note>
  **Maximum Salary** must be greater than or equal to **Minimum Salary**.
</Note>

## Create a job from a company

You can also add a job from a company record. Open the company, go to the **Jobs** tab and click **Add Job**. The company is filled in for you.
