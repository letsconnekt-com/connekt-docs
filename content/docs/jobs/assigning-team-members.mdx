---
title: "Assigning researchers to a job"
sidebarTitle: "Assign researchers"

---

Researchers only see the jobs they're assigned to. You give a Researcher access to a job by assigning them to it, either from the job itself or in bulk from the Jobs list. The assignment list only shows users with the **Researcher** role.

<Note>
  You need permission to edit jobs to assign or remove researchers.
</Note>

## Assign researchers to a job

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the job">
    Click **Jobs** in the sidebar and open the job.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Assign Researchers">
    Click the **⋯** menu in the job header, then click **Assign Researchers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick researchers">
    Under **Add researchers**, search for and select one or more Researchers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Assign**. The button shows how many researchers you've selected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Currently assigned** list at the top of the dialog shows everyone already assigned to the job.

## Assign researchers to several jobs at once

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select jobs">
    In the **Jobs** list, tick the checkbox next to each job.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the bulk action">
    Click **Actions**, then click **Assign Researcher**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick researchers">
    Search for and select one or more Researchers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Assign to X Jobs**. Each selected Researcher is added to every selected job. Researchers who are already assigned to a job stay assigned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remove a researcher

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Assign Researchers">
    Open the job, click the **⋯** menu and click **Assign Researchers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove them">
    In **Currently assigned**, click the **X** next to the researcher's name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Researcher loses access to the job straight away.

## What assignment does

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Controls what Researchers can see">
    A Researcher's Jobs list only shows jobs they're assigned to. If a Researcher can't find a job, check that they're assigned to it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Logged in the job's history">
    Each new assignment is recorded in the job's **History** tab with who made it. Removals aren't logged.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  Connekt doesn't notify Researchers when you assign or remove them. Let them know directly, especially for urgent roles.
</Warning>
