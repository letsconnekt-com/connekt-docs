Enrich looks up missing contact details for candidates and contacts. It can find a work email, a personal email or a phone number, using the Full Enrich data provider. You can enrich one record, up to 50 records from a list, or a profile in the Chrome extension.

## What Connekt needs to run a lookup

Each record needs one of these:

- A LinkedIn URL
- A first name, a last name and a current employer (for contacts, their company)

Records without either are skipped.

## Enrich one record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the record">
    Open the candidate or contact. Find the **Contact Information** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Enrich">
    Click the **Enrich** badge next to **Work Email**, **Personal Email** or **Phone**. Each badge looks up only that field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the result">
    The lookup can take a couple of minutes. The field fills in automatically when a value is found.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The badge runs even if the field already has a value. If Enrich finds a different value, it replaces the one on the record.
</Note>

## Enrich several records from a list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the records">
    On **Candidates** or **Contacts**, tick the boxes next to up to 50 records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Action menu">
    Click **Action**, then point to **Enrich**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to find">
    - Point to **Email**, then choose **Personal email**, **Work email** or **Both**. **Both** finds a work email and a phone number.
    - Or choose **Phone only**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it run">
    Connekt confirms how many records it's enriching. The lookup can take a couple of minutes, and the values fill in automatically. You can keep working while it runs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From a list, Connekt skips records that already have the value you asked for, so they don't use credits.

## Enrich in the Chrome extension

When you open a saved candidate or contact in the Connekt Chrome extension, an **Enrich** badge appears next to the email and phone fields. Click it to look up that field. The lookup can take up to 2 minutes.

See [Chrome extension](/integrations/chrome-extension) to install and use the extension.

## Credit costs

Enrich uses enrich credits. You're only charged when Enrich finds a value. If nothing is found, no credits are used.

| Detail found | Credits |
|---|---|
| Work email | 1 |
| Personal email | 3 |
| Phone number | 10 |

Your workspace gets 300 enrich credits per seat each month, shared by everyone in the workspace. Admins can also set a monthly cap for each user.

## If you run out of credits

When your workspace has no credits left, or you've reached your personal monthly cap, the Chrome extension shows an **Out of enrich credits** message and doesn't run the lookup.

An admin can check the balance and buy more credits in **Settings → Usage**. See [Usage and add-ons](/billing/usage-and-add-ons).

<Tip>
  Work emails cost the least. Start with **Work email** and look up personal emails or phone numbers only for the candidates you're actively pursuing.
</Tip>
