---
title: "Mark candidates as off-limits"
sidebarTitle: "Off-limits candidates"
description: "Flag candidates you shouldn't approach, with a reason and duration. Learn where the warning shows and what off-limits does and doesn't block."
---

Mark a candidate as off-limits when your team shouldn't approach them, for example because they were recently placed or work at a client. Connekt shows a warning wherever the candidate appears and leaves them out of AI matching.

You need permission to edit candidates to change off-limits status.

## Mark one candidate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate">
    Go to **Candidates** and open the candidate's profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Click the **⋯** menu in the header, then click **Mark as Off-Limits**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reason and duration">
    Enter a **Reason** so your team knows why. Then choose a **Duration**: 1 Week, 2 Weeks, 1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, 1 Year, 2 Years or Permanent. The default is 3 Months.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Mark as Off-Limits**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mark several candidates at once

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the candidates">
    On **Candidates**, tick the boxes next to the candidates you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Action menu">
    Click **Action**, then click **Mark as Off-Limits**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reason and duration">
    Enter a **Reason**, choose a **Duration**, then click **Mark as Off-Limits**. The same reason and duration apply to every selected candidate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can only remove off-limits status one candidate at a time, from each candidate's record.
</Note>

## Set it on the candidate form

You can also turn on the **Off Limits** switch when you create or edit a candidate, then choose an **Off Limits Duration** and add an **Off Limits Reason**. The form offers 6 Months, 1 Year, Until Job Change and Forever.

<Info>
  Durations set on the form are saved as a label only. They don't set an end date or a countdown. To get a countdown, use **Mark as Off-Limits** from the **⋯** menu.
</Info>

## Where the warning shows

- **Candidate profile:** a red banner reads **This candidate is marked as off-limits**, with the reason and the time remaining (or **Permanent**). The header also shows an off-limits badge.
- **Candidates list:** a red icon appears next to the name. Hover over it to see the reason.
- **Chrome extension:** when you open the candidate's LinkedIn profile, the extension shows **This candidate is off-limits** with the reason and duration. See [Chrome extension](/integrations/chrome-extension).

## What off-limits does

- **AI matching:** off-limits candidates are left out of **Matching candidates** results on jobs. See [AI matching](/candidates/matching-candidates-to-jobs).
- **AI search:** off-limits candidates are flagged and pushed down the results.
- **History:** the change is recorded on the candidate's **History** tab, with the reason.

<Warning>
  Off-limits is a warning, not a lock. Connekt doesn't stop you submitting an off-limits candidate to a job, adding them to a project or emailing them. Check the banner before you reach out.
</Warning>

## When the duration ends

Off-limits status doesn't lift itself when the duration ends. The banner on the profile changes to **Expired**, but the candidate stays off-limits in Connekt and is still left out of matching until you remove the status.

The Chrome extension stops showing its off-limits banner once the end date has passed.

## Remove off-limits status

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate">
    Open the candidate's profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the status">
    Click the **⋯** menu, then click **Remove Off-Limits**. In the **Remove Off-Limits Status** dialog, click **Remove Off-Limits**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
