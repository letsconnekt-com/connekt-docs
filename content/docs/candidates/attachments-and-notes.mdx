Every candidate record has tabs for files, notes and activity. Companies and contacts have the same **Notes**, **Activity** and **Attachments** tabs. Use **Attachments** for files, **Notes** for context your team needs, **Activity** for calls, emails and meetings, and **History** to see what changed on the record.

## Upload attachments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Attachments tab">
    Open the record and click the **Attachments** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add files">
    In **Upload New Attachments**, drop your files or click to browse. You can add up to 5 files at a time, 25 MB each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload">
    Click **Upload Selected Files**. The files appear in the list with **File Name**, **Uploaded By** and **Uploaded At**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From the list, you can preview, download or delete a file. Uploading and deleting depend on your role's permissions.

### Supported file types

| Type | Formats |
|---|---|
| Documents | PDF, DOC, DOCX |
| Spreadsheets | XLS, XLSX, CSV |
| Presentations | PPT, PPTX |
| Text | TXT |
| Images | JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP, SVG |
| Audio and video | MP3, WAV, MP4 |

<Note>
  Files over 25 MB can't be uploaded. For larger files, add a link to your cloud storage in a note.
</Note>

<Tip>
  AI matching reads the text of the candidate's most recent attachment. If you upload other files after the CV, upload the CV again so matching uses it. See [AI matching](/candidates/matching-candidates-to-jobs).
</Tip>

## Add a note

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Notes tab">
    Open the record and click the **Notes** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your note">
    Type in the editor under **Add Note**. You can use formatting such as bold, lists and links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mention a teammate (optional)">
    Type **@** and a teammate's name to mention them. They get a notification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add Note**. The note shows your name and the time you added it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To reply to a note, click **Reply** on it. Replies stay in a thread under the original note.

### Edit or delete a note

Open the menu on the note and click **Edit Note**. Make your changes, then click **Update Note**. To remove a note, click **Delete Note**.

Your role's permissions decide which notes you can edit and delete. Roles can allow editing your own notes, other people's notes, or both, and the same for deleting.

### Pull from meeting notes

If you record meetings with Connekt, you can copy a meeting summary into a note.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the meetings">
    On the **Notes** tab, click **Pull from Meeting Notes**. Connekt finds up to 10 recent meeting notes that match the record's name or email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview a meeting">
    Click **View** on a meeting to open its summary. You can edit the text before you insert it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Insert it">
    Click **Insert to Notes**. The summary is added to your note.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Linking and AI actions](/meetings/linking-and-ai-actions) for more ways to use meetings on records.

## Log an activity

Use the **Activity** tab to record calls, emails, meetings and other touchpoints.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Activity tab">
    Open the record, click the **Activity** tab, then click **Log activity**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    - **Type:** Call, Meeting, Email, LinkedIn or Other
    - **When did it happen?:** the date and time
    - **Subject** and **Notes:** optional
    - **Duration (minutes):** optional
    - **Outcome:** optional. Choose Completed, No Answer, Left Message, Rescheduled, Cancelled, Replied or No Response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Log activity**. The entry appears on the timeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Activities Connekt logs for you

Some activities are added automatically. Each entry has a badge that shows where it came from:

- **Notetaker:** meetings recorded with Connekt Notetaker
- **Sequence:** steps from email sequences
- **Email Sync:** emails synced from your connected mailbox
- **Manual:** activities someone logged by hand

You can edit or delete manual activities you logged yourself. Super Administrators, Administrators and Operations users can edit or delete anyone's manual activities. Automatic activities can't be edited or deleted.

## Check the History tab

The **History** tab on a candidate is a read-only log that Connekt keeps for you. It records changes to the record, such as updates, off-limits changes and merges, with who made each change and when. You can't edit or delete History entries.
