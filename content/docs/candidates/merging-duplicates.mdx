When the same person or company is in Connekt twice, you can merge the duplicate into the record you want to keep. You start from the record you're keeping, pick the duplicate, and choose which values to keep where they differ. The duplicate is then removed.

<Warning>
  You can't undo a merge in Connekt. Some data on the duplicate isn't moved across (see below). Check both records before you merge.
</Warning>

## Merge two candidates

You need permission to edit candidates.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the record you want to keep">
    Go to **Candidates** and open the candidate you want to keep. This record keeps its ID and URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Merge Duplicate">
    Click the **⋯** menu in the record header, then click **Merge Duplicate**. The **Merge Duplicate Candidate** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the duplicate">
    Connekt lists possible duplicates that share the candidate's name, email or mobile number. If the duplicate isn't listed, search by name, email, phone or company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the duplicate">
    Click **Select** on the duplicate. A comparison dialog opens with every field where the two records differ.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the values to keep">
    For each field, choose **Current Profile Data** (the record you're keeping) or **From New Resume** (the duplicate you selected). The dialog reuses the labels from resume upload, so "From New Resume" means the duplicate here.

    Click **Keep All Existing** or **Accept All New** to set every field at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Merge">
    Click **Update Candidate**. Connekt merges the duplicate into the record you're keeping.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Click a choice for every field, or use **Keep All Existing** or **Accept All New**, before you click **Update Candidate**. Then open the merged record and check the key fields.
</Tip>

### What moves to the record you keep

- Submissions and placements
- Work experience and education entries. Entries from both records are kept, so you may see repeats to tidy up.
- Notes and attachments
- Languages and social profiles, except where both records have the same language or the same platform. In that case, the kept record's entry stays and the duplicate's is dropped.
- Custom field values, where the kept record's field is empty

For skills and industries, you keep one record's list. The two lists aren't combined. Fields that don't appear in the comparison keep the kept record's values.

### What doesn't move

These stay with the duplicate and are no longer visible once it's removed:

- Logged activities on the **Activity** tab
- Tasks and linked meetings
- Email conversations
- Project memberships
- Email sequence enrolments
- **History** entries

The duplicate's owner and off-limits status aren't carried over either. The kept record's **History** tab records the merge.

## Merge two companies

Companies have the same merge flow. You need permission to edit companies.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the company you want to keep">
    Go to **Companies** and open the company you want to keep.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Merge Duplicate">
    Click the **⋯** menu in the header, then click **Merge Duplicate**. The **Merge Duplicate Company** dialog lists possible duplicates that share the company's name, website or LinkedIn URL. You can also search by name, website or industry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select and compare">
    Click **Select** on the duplicate. For each field that differs, choose **Current Company Data** (the company you're keeping) or **From New Submission** (the duplicate). You can also click **Keep All Existing** or **Accept All New**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Merge">
    Click **Merge Company**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What moves:** contacts, jobs, submissions, placements, email conversations, notes and attachments.

**What doesn't move:** projects and signing documents linked to the duplicate, plus its logged activities, tasks and meetings.

## Keep your database clean

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When should I merge?">
    Merge only when you're sure both records are the same person or company. Two people with the same name at different employers are usually different candidates. If you're unsure, leave both records and add a note.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The duplicate has the better profile. Which record should I keep?">
    Keep the record with the most history, such as submissions, activities, tasks and project memberships, because those don't move. Then use the comparison to copy across the duplicate's better field values.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I avoid duplicates in the first place?">
    Resume upload and the Chrome extension check for existing candidates before they create one. Bulk import has duplicate rules you set before each run. See [Adding a candidate](/candidates/adding-a-candidate) and [Bulk import](/candidates/bulk-import).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
