You can add candidates four ways: fill in the form, upload resumes for AI parsing, import a CSV, or save a LinkedIn profile with the Chrome extension. Use the form for a single referral, resume upload when you have CVs in hand, and CSV import when you're moving data from another system.

On the **Candidates** page, the **Add** button has a dropdown arrow next to it. Click **Add** to open the form. Click the arrow to open **Quick Actions**, which has **Import CSV** and **Upload / Parse Resume**.

<Note>
  You need permission to create candidates. If you don't see the **Add** button, ask your admin to check your role.
</Note>

## Fill in the form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Go to **Candidates** and click **Add**. The **Create New Candidate** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the candidate's details">
    **First Name** and **Last Name** are required. Everything else is optional unless your admin has made it required in **Settings → Field Configuration**. Required fields are marked on the form.

    Some countries need ID details:

    - **Singapore:** **ID Type** (NRIC or FIN) and **ID Number** are required.
    - **Hong Kong:** **ID Type** (HK ID Card or Passport) and **ID Number** are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach documents (optional)">
    In **Candidate Documents**, add up to 5 files, 25 MB each. You can add more later on the **Attachments** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Candidate**. The candidate's profile opens, and you're set as the owner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The fields and sections on the form depend on your workspace setup. Admins can add custom fields and reorder sections. See [Custom fields](/advanced/custom-fields).
</Tip>

## Upload resumes

Connekt reads each resume with AI and creates the candidate for you. There's no review step: every resume that isn't a possible duplicate becomes a candidate straight away.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the upload dialog">
    On **Candidates**, click the arrow next to **Add**, then click **Upload / Parse Resume**. The **Bulk Upload Resumes** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your files">
    Drop up to 50 resumes into the dialog, or click to browse. Each file must be PDF, DOCX or HTML, and 10 MB or smaller.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the upload">
    Click **Upload _N_ Resumes**. Connekt parses the files one at a time and shows the status of each one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the results">
    Each file ends in one of these states:

    - **Candidate created**: the new record is ready, with the resume attached. Click **View** to open it.
    - **Possible duplicate candidate**: Connekt found an existing candidate with the same email or name. See [Resolve duplicates](#resolve-duplicates) below.
    - **Error**: the file couldn't be processed. The message tells you why.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Each resume must include the candidate's name and email address. If Connekt can't find either one, that file fails and no candidate is created.
</Warning>

### Resolve duplicates

When a file is flagged as a possible duplicate, you choose what happens:

- Click **Skip** to ignore the file. Nothing is created or changed.
- Click **Resolve** to update the existing candidate. The **Duplicate Candidate Detected** dialog shows each field where the resume differs from the existing record.

In the dialog, pick **Current Profile Data** or **From New Resume** for each field. You can also click **Keep All Existing** or **Accept All New** to set every field at once. Click **Update Candidate** to save. Connekt updates the existing record and attaches the resume to it.

<Note>
  If you close the dialog while resumes are still processing, Connekt asks you to confirm. Processing continues, but you can't see the rest of the batch or resolve its duplicates.
</Note>

<Tip>
  AI parsing works best on clearly laid out resumes. Open a few of the new records to check the parsed details, especially for heavily designed CVs.
</Tip>

## Import from a CSV file

Use CSV import to add many candidates from a spreadsheet or another system. On **Candidates**, click the arrow next to **Add**, then click **Import CSV**. See [Bulk import](/candidates/bulk-import) for the full steps.

## Save from LinkedIn with the Chrome extension

The Connekt Chrome extension saves LinkedIn profiles straight into Connekt. Open a LinkedIn profile, open the extension, and choose a quick action:

- **Save as Candidate**
- **Save as Contact**
- **Save as Contact & Candidate**
- **Review & Save**, to check and edit the details first

See [Chrome extension](/integrations/chrome-extension) to install it and learn how duplicate checks work.
