AI matching ranks the best fits between candidates and jobs. It compares meaning, not just keywords, so "React.js" can match a job that asks for "ReactJS". You can match in both directions: from a candidate to find open jobs, or from a job to find candidates.

## Find jobs for a candidate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate">
    Go to **Candidates** and open the candidate's profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Matching open jobs card">
    The **Matching open jobs** card ranks jobs against the candidate's profile and CV.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how many results">
    Pick a number from the selector, from **Top 5** to **Top 50**. The default is **Top 5**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the match">
    Click **Find matches**. Each result shows a match score, the company, a short reason for the match and key skills.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a job">
    Click a result to open the job. From there, you can review it and submit the candidate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click **Regenerate** to run the match again, for example after you update the profile.

## Find candidates for a job

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the job">
    Go to **Jobs** and open the role you're filling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Matching candidates card">
    The **Matching candidates** card ranks candidates against the job's title, skills and description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how many results and run the match">
    Pick **Top 5** to **Top 50**, then click **Find matches**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a candidate">
    Click a result to open the candidate's profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filters that always apply

Connekt filters results before it ranks them. These filters can't be turned off.

| Filter | Candidate → jobs | Job → candidates |
|---|---|---|
| Job status | Only **Open** and **Partially Filled** jobs | Not applied |
| Location | If the candidate has a country, only jobs in that country or remote jobs | If the job isn't remote and has a country, only candidates in that country |
| Seniority | Leaves out jobs more than one career level below the candidate | Leaves out candidates more than one career level above the job |
| Off-limits | Not applied | Off-limits candidates are left out |

If a candidate or job has no career level, the seniority filter doesn't apply to it. See [Off-limits candidates](/candidates/off-limits).

## What the AI looks at

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="For a candidate">
    Current title, career level, skills, industries and years of experience. Connekt also reads the text of the candidate's most recent attachment, such as their CV, once it has been processed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="For a job">
    Job title, career level, skills, industries and the job description.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How results are ranked">
    Connekt first searches by meaning and keywords. AI then ranks the strongest results and writes the short reason you see on each match.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Get better matches

- Fill in skills, career level and country on both candidates and jobs.
- Attach a CV to each candidate. Matching reads the most recent attachment, so upload the CV again if you add other files after it.
- Write full job descriptions with clear requirements.
- Rate results with the thumbs up (**Helpful**) and thumbs down (**Not helpful**) buttons. Your feedback helps improve matching.

<Note>
  Matching and search work differently. To learn when to use each one, see [Search vs AI matching](/advanced/search-vs-ai-matching).
</Note>
