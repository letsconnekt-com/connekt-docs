You manage your account security from **Settings → Security**. There you can change your password, turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) and see every device signed in to your account. Administrators can also turn Screenshot Protection on or off for the mobile app.

## Change your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Security">
    Go to **Settings → Security** and find the **Password & Security** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Change Password">
    Click **Change Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your passwords">
    Enter your **Current Password**, then your **New Password** and **Confirm New Password**. Your new password must be at least 8 characters and different from your current one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and sign in again">
    Click **Change Password**, then click **Continue to Sign In**. Sign in with your new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you change your password, Connekt signs you out of all your devices. Connekt also emails you to confirm the change.

<Tip>
  Forgot your password? On the sign-in page, click **Forgot your password?**, enter your email and click **Send reset email**. Follow the link in the email to set a new password.
</Tip>

## Turn on two-factor authentication

With 2FA turned on, you need a 6-digit code from an authenticator app as well as your password to sign in. A stolen password alone isn't enough to get into your account. You can use any authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator, Authy or 1Password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Security">
    Go to **Settings → Security** and find **Two-Factor Authentication** in the **Password & Security** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Enable 2FA">
    Click **Enable 2FA**, then click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Scan the QR code with your authenticator app. If you can't scan it, click **Can't scan? Enter code manually** and copy the secret key into your app. Then click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a code to confirm">
    Enter the 6-digit **Verification Code** from your authenticator app and click **Verify 2FA**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once 2FA is on, the **Password & Security** card shows an **Enabled** badge. Next time you sign in, enter your password, then the 6-digit code from your app, and click **Verify**.

<Warning>
  Connekt doesn't provide backup codes. If you lose access to your authenticator app, you can't complete sign-in yourself. Contact the Connekt team for help. See [Getting help](/getting-started/getting-help).
</Warning>

## Turn off two-factor authentication

1. Go to **Settings → Security** and click **Manage 2FA**.
2. Click **Disable Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Click **Disable 2FA** to confirm.

After you turn off 2FA, you only need your password to sign in. We recommend keeping 2FA turned on.

## Review your active devices

The **Active devices** card lists every device signed in to your account. For each device, you can see:

- The device name and browser
- The approximate location and IP address
- When the device was last active

Your current device is marked **This device**. If you have more than three devices, click the **Show all** button below the list to see every session.

## Sign out other devices

If you don't recognise a device, or you've lost one, sign it out straight away.

- **Sign out one device.** Click the **⋯** button next to the device, then click **Sign out device**.
- **Sign out every other device.** Click **Sign out all others**, then click **Sign out all others** again to confirm.

Signed-out devices must sign in again before they can use Connekt.

<Tip>
  If you see a device you don't recognise, sign it out and then change your password. Changing your password also signs out every device.
</Tip>

## Screenshot Protection (Administrators)

Administrators can block screenshots and screen recordings in the Connekt mobile app for everyone in the workspace. Screenshot Protection is on by default.

1. Go to **Settings → Security** and find the **Organisation Security** card.
2. Turn the **Screenshot Protection** switch on or off.

The setting applies to every member of your workspace on the mobile app. Only Administrators see the **Organisation Security** card. See [Signing in and security on mobile](/mobile/signing-in-and-security).
