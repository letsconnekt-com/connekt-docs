Your profile holds your personal details, such as your name, photo and job title. Your email signature is added to the emails you send from Connekt. Both are personal, so changes only affect your own account.

## Update your profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Profile">
    Go to **Settings → Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Edit Profile">
    Click **Edit Profile** at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your details">
    Update any of these fields: **Full Name**, **Mobile Number**, **Job Title**, **Currency** and **Region**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save Changes**. To discard your edits, click **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Full Name** can't be empty.
</Note>

## Change your profile picture

1. Go to **Settings → Profile** and click **Edit Profile**.
2. Under **Profile Picture**, drop your photo into the box or click **browse files**. You can also click the camera icon on your avatar.
3. The photo uploads straight away.

Photos can be PNG, JPG or GIF files up to 5 MB.

## Details you can't change yourself

Some details on your profile are managed by your workspace:

| Detail | Who can change it |
|---|---|
| **Email Address** | An admin, from **Settings → Manage Users** |
| **Account Role** | An admin, from **Settings → Manage Users** |
| **Offices** and **Teams** | An admin, from **Settings → Manage Users** or **Settings → Organization** |

If one of these is wrong, ask an Administrator in your workspace to update it.

## Set up your email signature

Your signature can include formatted text, links and images, such as your company logo.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Email Signature">
    Go to **Settings → Email Signature**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your signature">
    Type your signature in the editor. Use the toolbar to format text and add links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an image (optional)">
    Upload an image, such as a logo, from the editor toolbar. You can resize it in the editor. Images can be PNG, JPEG, GIF or WEBP files up to 5 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save signature**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where your signature is used

Connekt adds your signature below the message when you send email from Connekt. You need a connected email account to send email. See [Connecting your email](/integrations/connecting-email).

| Where you send from | How the signature works |
|---|---|
| **Inbox**: new emails and replies | The **Include signature** checkbox is ticked by default. Untick it to leave the signature off that email. |
| **Emails on a record**, such as replying from a candidate's email conversation | The **Include signature** checkbox is ticked by default. |
| **Outreach sequences** | Each email step has an **Include sender's email signature** checkbox. The signature used is the one belonging to the person who enrolled the recipient. |
| **Outreach templates** | Tick **Include sender's email signature by default** so steps that use the template include it. |

<Tip>
  The **Include signature** checkbox only appears after you've saved a signature.
</Tip>

See [Using the inbox](/outreach/using-the-inbox) and [Email sequences](/outreach/email-sequences) to learn more about sending email from Connekt.
