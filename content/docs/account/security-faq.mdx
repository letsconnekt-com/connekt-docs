Your Connekt database holds your relationships, conversations and years of work. This page answers common questions about how Connekt protects it.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Connekt support two-factor authentication?">
    Yes. Every plan includes two-factor authentication (2FA). With 2FA turned on, you need a code from an authenticator app as well as your password to sign in. A leaked password on its own isn't enough to get into your workspace.

    Each person turns on 2FA for their own account from **Settings → Security**. See [Security and two-factor authentication](/account/security-and-2fa).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I control what each person can see and do?">
    Yes. Every user has a role, and each role comes with a set of permissions. That way, each person sees what they need and nothing more.

    On the Team plan or higher, Administrators can fine-tune what each role can do from **Settings → Permissions**. See [User roles](/team/user-roles) and [Customising permissions](/team/customising-permissions).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I see who did what in my workspace?">
    Yes. The **Audit Log** shows an **Activity Feed** of user actions across your workspace. These include sign-ins and sign-outs, changes to records, document activity, leave requests and attachments. Each event shows who did it and when, along with the IP address, location and device.

    The Audit Log is available on the Team and Business plans. You need the System Administration permission to open it from **Settings → Audit Log**. See [Activity and usage](/advanced/activity-and-usage).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I stop people taking screenshots of candidate data?">
    Yes, on the mobile app. Administrators can block screenshots and screen recordings in the Connekt mobile app for everyone in the workspace. Screenshot Protection is on by default.

    To change it, go to **Settings → Security** and use the **Screenshot Protection** switch. See [Security and two-factor authentication](/account/security-and-2fa#screenshot-protection-administrators).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data encrypted?">
    Yes. Your data is encrypted in transit, while it moves between you and Connekt. It's also encrypted at rest, while it's stored.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data backed up?">
    Yes. Your workspace is backed up every day.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What should I do if I think someone else has access to my account?">
    Act straight away:

    1. Go to **Settings → Security** and click **Sign out all others** to sign out every other device.
    2. Change your password. This also signs you out everywhere.
    3. Turn on two-factor authentication if you haven't already.
    4. Tell an Administrator in your workspace.

    See [Security and two-factor authentication](/account/security-and-2fa).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I have a security question that isn't answered here">
    Contact the Connekt team, and we'll walk you through how your team's information is handled. See [Getting help](/getting-started/getting-help).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
