Teams group people under one or more managers. Connekt uses teams to decide who approves whose leave and whose figures a manager sees in dashboards and deals. You set them up in **Settings → Organization**.

<Info>
  Organization needs the **Team** plan or higher. You also need the **System Administration** permission, which Super Administrators and Administrators have by default.
</Info>

## How teams work

Each person on a team is either a **Manager** or a **Member**.

- A team can have several managers.
- A person can be on more than one team.
- A team can belong to an office. See [Offices and currency](/advanced/offices-and-currency).

<Note>
  A team **Manager** is different from the **Manager** role. The role sets someone's permissions. The team position sets whose work they oversee. See [User roles](/team/user-roles).
</Note>

The top of the page shows **Active teams**, **Managers**, **On a team** and **Unassigned** counts.

## Create a team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a team">
    Go to **Settings → Organization** and click **New team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Name**, for example "APAC Delivery". Optionally choose an **Office (optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create**. The team appears in the list, ready for people.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add managers and members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a person">
    On the team, click **Add person**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the person and role">
    Pick the **Person**, then choose **Manager** or **Member** under **Role**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add person**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change someone's position, click **⋯** next to them and choose **Make manager** or **Make member**. To take them off the team, choose **Remove from team**.

Only Super Administrators and Administrators can make someone a manager.

<Tip>
  The **Not on a team** list shows active users who aren't on any team. Add them to a team so their work rolls up to a manager.
</Tip>

## Rename, move or deactivate a team

Click **⋯** on the team and choose:

- **Rename team**: members keep their positions.
- **Assign office**: link the team to an office, or choose **No office**.
- **Deactivate team**: the team moves to **Inactive teams**. It stays on historical deals. Click **Reactivate** to use it again.

## How teams affect leave approvals

Operations and Manager users approve leave for people on teams where they're a manager. Super Administrators and Administrators can approve leave regardless of teams.

For example, to let a Manager approve their team's leave, make them a **Manager** of that team. See [Approving leave requests](/time-off/approving-leave).

## How teams affect dashboards and deals

- Users with the **Manager** role see figures for members of the teams they manage in their dashboard and deal views.
- Administrators and Operations users see everyone. They can narrow their view to an office, a team or a person.

See [Understanding dashboard metrics](/dashboard/understanding-metrics).
