Every user in Connekt has one role. The role sets their default permissions: which records they can see, what they can change, and which settings they can open. This page describes the defaults. On the Team plan or higher, an admin can change them.

## Role reference

| Role | Default access |
|---|---|
| **Super Administrator** | Every permission. Can't be assigned or changed from Settings, and its permissions can't be edited. |
| **Administrator** | Full access to records and every setting, including **Settings → Plan** and billing, **Manage Users**, **Permissions** and **Organization**. The person who creates the workspace is an Administrator. |
| **Operations** | Company-wide visibility for oversight. Sees all candidates, companies, contacts, jobs and deals, plus company and financial dashboards. Can edit records and use **Manage Users**. Can't create candidates, companies, contacts, jobs or deals, and has no access to Plan, Permissions or other admin settings. |
| **Manager** | Everything the consultant roles can do, plus the team dashboard and team deals, changing record ownership, editing and deleting others' notes, and **Sign**. Can't open **Manage Users**. |
| **Principal Consultant** | Same defaults as Consultant. |
| **Senior Consultant** | Same defaults as Consultant. |
| **Consultant** | Sees all candidates, companies, contacts and jobs. Can create and edit them, add notes, and work on their own and assigned deals. Can't delete records or use **Sign**. |
| **Recruiter** | Same defaults as Consultant. |
| **Researcher** | Sees all candidates and can add them, but can't edit candidate profiles or add notes. Sees only the jobs and deals they're assigned to. Doesn't see **Companies** or **Contacts** in the sidebar. |
| **User** | No access to candidates, companies, jobs or deals by default. Can see contacts assigned to them and add notes and attachments to placements. |

<Note>
  Principal Consultant, Senior Consultant, Consultant and Recruiter start with identical permissions. Use the different titles to reflect seniority, or [customise their permissions](/team/customising-permissions) to make them different.
</Note>

## What admin roles can do

- **Billing**: Super Administrators and Administrators can open **Settings → Plan** and **Settings → Usage**. See [Plans compared](/billing/plans-compared).
- **Users**: Super Administrators, Administrators and Operations can invite people, change roles, and deactivate or reactivate users in **Settings → Manage Users**.
- **Permissions**: only Super Administrators and Administrators can edit role permissions. See [Customising permissions](/team/customising-permissions).

## Researchers and jobs

Researchers only see jobs they're assigned to. To give a Researcher access to a job, add them to it. See [Assigning team members to jobs](/jobs/assigning-team-members).

## Leave approvals

Roles also affect who can approve leave. Super Administrators and Administrators can approve most requests. Operations and Manager users approve requests from teams they manage. Other roles can't approve leave. See [Approving leave requests](/time-off/approving-leave).

## Choose a role when you invite someone

When you invite someone from **Settings → Manage Users → Invite User**, pick their **Role** in the form. See [Inviting your team](/getting-started/inviting-your-team).

## Change someone's role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Users">
    Go to **Settings → Manage Users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the person">
    Search by name or email, or filter by role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change their role">
    Click **⋯** next to them and choose **Change Role**. Pick the **New Role** and click **Update Role**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can't change your own role, and a Super Administrator's role can't be changed.

<Tip>
  If a role is close to what someone needs but not quite right, you can grant or deny individual permissions for that person. See [Customising permissions](/team/customising-permissions#override-permissions-for-one-person).
</Tip>
