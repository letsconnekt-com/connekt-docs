Each role comes with default permissions. If they don't match how your team works, you can change them for a whole role, or override them for one person. See [User roles](/team/user-roles) for the defaults.

<Info>
  Editing role permissions needs the **Team** plan or higher. Only Super Administrators and Administrators can open **Settings → Permissions**.
</Info>

## Change a role's permissions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Permissions">
    Go to **Settings → Permissions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a role">
    Choose the role from the dropdown at the top. You can edit every role except Super Administrator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the permissions">
    Open a category to see its permissions, or type in **Search permissions…**. The summary shows how many permissions are enabled for the role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Turn individual permissions on or off. Use **All** or **None** on a category to change the whole category. Use **Enable all** or **Disable all** to change every permission for the role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    A bar appears at the bottom saying **Unsaved changes to** the role. Click **Save changes** to apply them, or **Discard** to undo them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Saved changes apply to everyone with that role.

<Warning>
  If you switch to another role before saving, your unsaved changes are lost.
</Warning>

## Permission categories

| Category | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Personal, team and company dashboards, financial data, and which Home dashboard widgets the role sees. |
| **Candidates** | Viewing, creating, editing and deleting candidates, ownership, notes and attachments. |
| **Companies** | Viewing, creating, editing and deleting companies, ownership, notes and attachments. |
| **Contacts** | Viewing, creating, editing and deleting contacts, primary contacts, notes and attachments. |
| **Jobs** | Viewing all or assigned jobs, creating, editing and deleting jobs, notes, attachments and submissions. |
| **Deals** | Seeing personal, team, company or assigned deals, deal financial data, and creating, editing and deleting deals. |
| **Placements** | Notes and attachments on placements. |
| **System** | **System Administration** (admin settings such as Plan, Permissions and Organization), **Team Management** (**Manage Users**), **User Settings**, **View Reports** and **Advanced Analytics**. |
| **Operations** | **View Operations Dashboard**. |
| **E sign** | **Create Documents**, which shows **Sign** in the sidebar. |
| **Checklist** | Which steps appear on the getting-started checklist on Home. |

## Examples

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Let consultants send documents for signature">
    Consultants, Recruiters and Researchers don't have **Sign** by default. Pick the role, open **E sign**, turn on **Create Documents** and save. **Sign** then appears in their sidebar.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Let Managers delete candidates">
    Only Super Administrators and Administrators can delete candidates by default. Pick **Manager**, open **Candidates**, turn on **Delete Candidate Profiles** and save.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Hide revenue figures from a role's dashboard">
    Pick the role, open **Dashboard**, and turn off widgets such as **Dashboard: Revenue (QTD)** and **Dashboard: Revenue (YTD)**. Save, and those widgets no longer appear on the role's Home dashboard.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Override permissions for one person

Use permission overrides when one person needs different access from the rest of their role. Overrides don't change the person's role.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Users">
    Go to **Settings → Manage Users** and find the person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open their overrides">
    Click **⋯** next to them and choose **Manage Permission Overrides**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set each permission">
    For each permission, choose **Default** (follow the role), **Grant** or **Deny**. Use **Search permissions…** to find one quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The footer shows how many permissions are overridden. Click **Save changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An override always wins over the role's setting. To undo one, set it back to **Default**.

<Note>
  Super Administrators, Administrators and Operations users can open **Manage Permission Overrides**. Only Super Administrators and Administrators can **Grant** permissions. Operations users can only deny permissions or reset them to default.
</Note>
