When someone leaves or takes an extended break, deactivate their account. They can't log in, but everything they created stays in Connekt. You can reactivate them at any time if you have a free seat.

<Info>
  Super Administrators, Administrators and Operations users can deactivate and reactivate users.
</Info>

## Deactivate a user

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Users">
    Go to **Settings → Manage Users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the person">
    Search by name or email, or filter by role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deactivate them">
    Click **⋯** next to them and choose **Deactivate User**. Click **Deactivate User** again to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What deactivation does

- **Login is blocked.** The person can't sign in to Connekt.
- **Their data stays.** Candidates, notes, deals, documents and activity they created remain visible to the rest of the team.
- **A seat is freed.** Deactivated users don't count against your seat limit, so you can invite or reactivate someone else.

You can't deactivate yourself. The **⋯** menu doesn't appear on your own row.

## Deactivating doesn't lower your bill

Your bill is based on the number of seats you've bought, not the number of active users. Deactivating someone frees a seat, but you keep paying for it.

To pay for fewer seats, lower your seat count in **Settings → Plan**. See [Adding and removing seats](/billing/adding-removing-seats).

<Warning>
  If you lower your seat count below your number of active users, deactivate people yourself before the change takes effect. Otherwise, Connekt deactivates the least recently active non-admin users when the new seat count starts.
</Warning>

## Reactivate a user

<Steps>
  <Step title="Show inactive users">
    In **Settings → Manage Users**, change the status filter from **Active Only** to **Inactive Only**. Deactivated users have an **Inactive** badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reactivate them">
    Click **⋯** next to the person and choose **Reactivate User**. Click **Reactivate User** again to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The person can sign in again with their existing account. They keep their role and data, and don't need a new invitation.

<Note>
  Reactivating someone needs a free seat. If every seat is in use, you'll see "Seat limit reached". Deactivate someone else or add seats first.
</Note>
