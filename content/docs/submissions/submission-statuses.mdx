Every submission has a status that shows where the candidate is in the hiring process. Connekt has 19 statuses, grouped into four sets in the status menu: **Initial Stages**, **Interview Stages**, **Final Stages** and **Other**.

## Initial stages

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | You've put the candidate forward. New submissions start here by default. |
| **Under Review** | The client is reviewing the candidate's profile. |
| **Shortlisted** | The client has shortlisted the candidate. |

## Interview stages

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Phone Screening** | A screening call is scheduled or done. |
| **1st Interview** | The candidate is at the first interview. |
| **2nd Interview** | The candidate is at the second interview. |
| **3rd Interview** | The candidate is at the third interview. |
| **Final Interview** | The candidate is in the final round. |
| **Technical Assessment** | A skills test or take-home task is in progress. |
| **Presentation** | The candidate is preparing or giving a presentation. |

## Final stages

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Reference Check** | References or background checks are in progress. |
| **Offer Extended** | The client has made an offer. |
| **Offer Accepted** | The candidate has accepted the offer. |
| **Offer Declined** | The candidate turned down the offer. |
| **Hired** | The candidate is hired. Connekt sets this automatically when you create a placement from the submission. |

<Tip>
  When a submission reaches **Offer Accepted** or **Hired**, you can create a placement from it. See [Convert a submission to a placement](/submissions/converting-to-placement).
</Tip>

## Other

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Rejected** | The client didn't select the candidate. |
| **Withdrawn** | The candidate withdrew from the process. |
| **On Hold** | The process is paused. The submission stays active on the **Deals** page. |
| **Process Ended** | The process closed for any other reason. The submission leaves the **Deals** page. |

## Change a submission's status

You can change the status in three places. In each one, the change saves as soon as you pick a status. There's no **Save** button and no note field.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Job Submissions tab">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the job">
        Go to **Jobs**, open the job and click the **Submissions** tab.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click the status">
        In the **Interview Stage** column, click the candidate's status badge.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pick the new status">
        Choose a status from the **Update Status** menu.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
    You need the **Edit Jobs** permission. The menu is disabled if the job isn't **Open**, **In Progress** or **Partially Filled**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Candidate Submissions tab">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the candidate">
        Go to **Candidates**, open the profile and click the **Submissions** tab.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click the status">
        In the **Status** column, click the status badge for the job.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pick the new status">
        Choose a status from the **Update Status** menu.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
    You need the **Edit Candidate Submissions** permission.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Deals page">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Deals">
        Click **Deals** in the sidebar. The **Pipeline** tab opens.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Change the status">
        In **Table** view, click the badge in the **Status** column and pick a status. In **Kanban** view, drag the card to another column.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
    You need the **Manage Deal Pipeline** permission. If you choose **Process Ended**, Connekt asks you to confirm, because the candidate leaves the active pipeline.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

When a status changes to **Offer Accepted** or **Hired**, Connekt shows a message that the submission is ready for placement conversion. On the **Submissions** tabs, that message has a **Create Placement** button.

<Note>
  Connekt records each status change in the candidate's **History** tab.
</Note>

## How status affects the Deals page

- The **Deals** page shows the 14 active statuses by default: every Initial and Interview stage, plus **Reference Check**, **Offer Extended**, **Offer Accepted** and **On Hold**.
- **Offer Declined**, **Rejected**, **Withdrawn**, **Process Ended** and **Hired** don't appear unless you filter by that status.
- Each deal has a probability that Connekt sets from its status. See [Deals board](/submissions/pipeline-board) for how probability works and how to override it.
