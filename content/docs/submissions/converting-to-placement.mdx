title: "Convert a submission to a placement"
sidebarTitle: "Convert to a placement"

When a candidate accepts an offer, you record the deal as a placement. The placement holds the fee, the splits and the invoicing details. Creating it from the submission saves time, because Connekt fills in the candidate, job and company for you.

## Before you start

- The submission must be at **Offer Accepted** or **Hired**. The **Create Placement** option doesn't appear at any other status. To change the status, see [Submission statuses](/submissions/submission-statuses).
- The submission can't already have a placement. Each submission can have only one placement.
- You need the **Create Deals** permission.

## Where to start

You can create the placement from four places.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Job Submissions tab">
    Go to **Jobs**, open the job and click the **Submissions** tab. On the submission's row, click **Create Placement**. The **Create Placement** dialog opens. This button also needs the **Edit Jobs** permission.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Candidate Submissions tab">
    Open the candidate's profile and click the **Submissions** tab. On the submission's row, click **Create Placement**. The **Create Placement** dialog opens.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Deals page">
    Click **Deals** in the sidebar. On the deal's row, click **⋯** and choose **Create Placement**. On a Kanban card, use the card's menu. The **Create New Placement** page opens.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Status change message">
    When you change a status to **Offer Accepted** or **Hired** on a **Submissions** tab, Connekt shows a message that the submission is ready for placement conversion. Click **Create Placement** in that message.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Create the placement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the placement type">
    Pick the **Placement Type**. For a standard contingency placement, choose **Candidate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the pre-filled details">
    Connekt fills in the candidate, company and job from the submission. The **Status** starts as **Starting Soon** and the **Start Date** starts as today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the placement details">
    Complete the required fields, such as **Contract Type**, **Country**, **Billing Country**, **Billing Currency**, **Fee Value** and **Start Date**. For the full list, see [Create a placement](/placements/creating-a-placement).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the splits">
    In the **Splits** section, add each person who shares the fee, then click **Save Splits**. See [Deal splits](/placements/deal-splits).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the placement">
    Click **Create Placement**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The **Create Placement** button stays disabled until the required fields are complete and you've saved your splits.
</Note>

## What happens next

- Connekt creates the placement and links it to the submission.
- The submission's status changes to **Hired**.
- The submission leaves the **Pipeline** tab on the **Deals** page and its **Submissions** tab lists.
- The placement appears on the **My Placements** and **All Placements** tabs on the **Deals** page.
- The job's filled positions update. A job's status changes to **Partially Filled** or **Filled** as its positions fill.

<Tip>
  If you add a placement from **Deals → My Placements → Add Placement** instead, and the candidate already has a submission for that job, Connekt links the placement to that submission automatically.
</Tip>

To change the fee, splits or status later, open the placement and click **Edit**. See [Placement and payment statuses](/placements/placement-statuses).
