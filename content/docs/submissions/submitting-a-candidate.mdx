A submission links a candidate to a job and puts them on the **Deals** page, where you track them through each interview stage. You can submit from the job or from the candidate. Both routes create the same submission record.

## Submit from a job

Use this route when you're working a job and want to put a candidate forward for it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the job">
    Go to **Jobs** and open the job.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Submissions tab">
    Click the **Submissions** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Submit Candidate">
    Click **Submit Candidate**. The **Submit Candidate** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the candidate">
    In **Select Candidate**, search for and pick the candidate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the submission details">
    Enter the **Estimated Fee Value** and choose the **Fee Currency**. Both are required. Fill in any optional fields you need (see the table below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit Candidate**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can only submit to a job whose status is **Open**, **In Progress** or **Partially Filled**. For any other status, the **Submit Candidate** button is disabled. You also need the **Edit Jobs** permission to see the button.
</Note>

## Submit from a candidate

Use this route when you're reviewing a candidate and want to put them forward for a role.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate">
    Go to **Candidates** and open the candidate's profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Submissions tab">
    Click the **Submissions** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Submit to Job">
    Click **Submit to Job**. The dialog opens with the candidate already selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the company and job">
    Select the **Company** first, then the **Job Position**. Both are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the submission details">
    Enter the **Estimated Fee Value** and choose the **Fee Currency**. Add any optional details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit Candidate**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You need the **Edit Candidate Submissions** permission to see **Submit to Job**.
</Note>

## Submission fields

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Estimated Fee Value** | Yes | The fee you expect to earn. It becomes the deal value on the **Deals** page. |
| **Fee Currency** | Yes | The currency of the fee. It can differ from the candidate's salary currency. |
| **Submitted To** | No | Filled in with the job's company when you submit from a job. |
| **Submission Method** | No | **Email** (default), **Company Portal**, **Phone**, **In-Person** or **Other**. |
| **Rating (1-5)** | No | How well the candidate matches, from **1 - Very Poor Match** to **5 - Excellent Match**. |
| **Notes (Optional)** | No | Free text for the hiring team, such as why the candidate fits. |
| **Initial Status** | No | Job route only. Defaults to **Submitted**. |
| **Deal Type** | No | Job route only. Defaults to **Candidate Submission**. |
| **Submission Date (Optional)** | No | Candidate route only. Leave it blank to use today's date. You can't pick a future date. |

## What happens after you submit

- The submission appears on the job's **Submissions** tab and the candidate's **Submissions** tab.
- It also appears on the **Deals** page, with its status and deal value. See [Deals board](/submissions/pipeline-board).
- If the candidate belongs to another recruiter, Connekt notifies the candidate's owner.

<Warning>
  Connekt doesn't stop you from submitting the same candidate to the same job twice. Check the candidate's **Submissions** tab before you submit.
</Warning>

## Delete a submission

Click the delete icon on the submission's row in either **Submissions** tab, then confirm. You can't delete a submission that already has a placement.
