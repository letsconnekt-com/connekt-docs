The **Deals** page shows every active submission as a deal, with its stage, probability and value. Use it to review your pipeline, update deals without opening each record, and forecast revenue. Click **Deals** in the sidebar to open it.

## Tabs on the Deals page

| Tab | What it shows | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Pipeline** | Active submissions (deals). This page covers this tab. | Anyone with a deals view permission |
| **My Placements** | Placements you're the main recruiter on. | **View Personal Deals** or **View Assigned Deals** |
| **All Placements** | Every placement in the company. | **View Company Deals** |

For the placement tabs, see [Create a placement](/placements/creating-a-placement) and [Placement and payment statuses](/placements/placement-statuses).

## Which deals appear

The **Pipeline** tab shows submissions in the 14 active statuses:

- **Initial stages:** Submitted, Under Review, Shortlisted
- **Interview stages:** Phone Screening, 1st Interview, 2nd Interview, 3rd Interview, Final Interview, Technical Assessment, Presentation
- **Final stages:** Reference Check, Offer Extended, Offer Accepted
- **Other:** On Hold

Submissions that are **Offer Declined**, **Rejected**, **Withdrawn**, **Process Ended** or **Hired** only appear if you pick that status in the status filter. A submission leaves the pipeline as soon as it has a placement.

## Stats cards

The cards at the top summarise the deals that match your current filters.

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Active Interviews** | The number of deals between **1st Interview** and **Offer Extended**. |
| **Pipeline Value** | The total estimated value of the deals, converted into the currency you've chosen. |
| **Avg Probability** | The average probability across the deals. |
| **Split Deals** | The number of split deals. |
| **Forecast Deals** | The number of late-stage deals with a probability of 70% or more. |
| **Forecasted Billings** | The total value of the forecast deals, converted into the currency you've chosen. |

Use the currency button at the top right to choose how amounts display. The **Show figures in** menu offers **Company currency**, **My currency**, and the selected office's currency when you've picked an office. It defaults to the company currency, which an admin sets in **Settings → Revenue & FX**.

## Filter your pipeline

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Search deals...** | Finds deals by candidate name, job name, company name or **Submitted To**. |
| Team member picker | Chooses whose pipeline you see. Managers get **My Pipeline**, **All Team Members** and each team member. Users with **View Company Deals** get **All Pipeline**, **My Pipeline Only** and any user. It only appears if you can see other people's deals. |
| Status filter | Shows **All Statuses** or a single status, including closed ones. |
| **Full view** / **Company View** / **Candidate View** | Chooses which ownership counts. See below. |
| Office and team menu | At the top right, admins and managers can narrow the pipeline to an office or team. |

The ownership views work like this for the person you've selected:

- **Full view:** every deal related to the person, as deal owner, candidate owner or company owner.
- **Company View:** anyone's candidates in process with the person's companies.
- **Candidate View:** the person's candidates in process with anyone's companies.

<Note>
  **Company View** and **Candidate View** have no effect while you're viewing **All Pipeline**. Choose a person first.
</Note>

## Switch between Table and Kanban

Click **Table** or **Kanban** next to the filters. **Table** is the default. Connekt remembers your choice on this browser.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Table">
    Each row is a deal, with these columns: **Candidate**, **Position**, **Company**, **Deal Type**, **Status**, **Probability**, **Deal Value**, **Recruiter**, **Comments**, **Last Activity** and **Actions**.

    Rows at **Offer Accepted** or **Hired** are highlighted green. Rows **On Hold** are highlighted orange.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Kanban">
    Each status is a column, and each deal is a card. Drag a card to another column to change its status.

    To hide columns you don't use, click **Columns** and clear the statuses under **Visible Columns**. Connekt saves your column choice to your account. If deals are hidden in collapsed columns, a message shows how many, with a **show all** link.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Edit deals inline

If you have the **Manage Deal Pipeline** permission, you can edit these fields directly in the table or on a Kanban card. Without it, they're read-only.

| Field | How to edit |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Click the badge and pick a status. Choosing **Process Ended** asks you to confirm. |
| **Deal Type** | Choose **Solo Deal** or **Split Deal**. |
| **Probability** | Click the percentage, enter a value from 0 to 100 and click **Save**. |
| **Deal Value** | Click the value, choose the **Currency** and **Amount**, then press Enter. Press Escape to cancel. |
| **Comments** | Click the comment to open **Edit Comments**. |

### How probability works

Connekt sets each deal's probability from its status, with later stages getting a higher value. You can override it in **Edit Probability**. To go back to the status-based value, click **Reset**. Moving a deal to **Offer Accepted**, **Hired**, **Offer Declined**, **Rejected**, **Withdrawn** or **Process Ended** always recalculates the probability.

## View deal details

In the table, click **⋯** in the **Actions** column and choose **View Details**. On a Kanban card, use the card's menu. The **Deal Details** panel opens on the **Commission** tab. The panel also has a **Stage History** tab.

The **Commission** tab is a read-only summary with **Deal Overview**, **Value Breakdown**, **Commission Splits**, **Comments** and **Summary**. **Commission Splits** shows an estimated split based on who owns the candidate and company. The actual splits are set on the placement. See [Deal splits](/placements/deal-splits).

<Note>
  For a deal that's already **Hired**, **View Details** opens the candidate's profile instead.
</Note>

## Create a placement from a deal

When a deal reaches **Offer Accepted** or **Hired**, click **⋯** and choose **Create Placement**. You need the **Create Deals** permission. See [Convert a submission to a placement](/submissions/converting-to-placement).
