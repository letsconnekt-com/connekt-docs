You add teammates one at a time from **Settings → Manage Users**. Each person gets an email with the login link, their email address and a temporary password you set.

By default, Administrators and Operations users can invite people. You need a free seat for each person you invite. During the free trial your workspace has only one seat, so subscribe first to invite teammates. See [Free trial](/getting-started/free-trial).

## Invite a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Users">
    Go to **Settings → Manage Users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite User">
    Click **Invite User** to open the invite form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Fill in the required fields: **Email Address**, **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Temporary Password**, **Role**, **Currency** and **Employment Type**. You can also add a **Mobile Number**, **Job Title**, **Region**, **Office** and **Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Click **Send Invite**. The new user receives their invitation email straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The invitation email asks new users to change their password after they first sign in. They can do this from **Settings → Security**. See [Security and two-factor authentication](/account/security-and-2fa).
</Tip>

## Choose a role

Pick the role that best fits each person's work. You can change it later from **Settings → Manage Users**. These are the default permissions for each role.

| Role | Default access |
|---|---|
| **Administrator** | Full access to every feature and setting, including billing. The person who creates the workspace is an Administrator. |
| **Operations** | Company-wide visibility for reporting and oversight. Can invite and manage users, and edit records, but can't create or delete records. No access to workspace configuration or billing. |
| **Manager** | Creates and manages candidates, companies, contacts, jobs and deals. Can see team dashboards. |
| **Principal Consultant**, **Senior Consultant**, **Consultant** | Create and manage candidates, companies, contacts, jobs and deals. These three roles start with the same permissions. |
| **Recruiter** | Create and manage candidates, companies, contacts, jobs and deals. |
| **Researcher** | Views and adds candidates. No access to companies, contacts or jobs by default. |
| **User** | Basic access to view and update assigned tasks. Can't see candidates, companies or jobs by default. |

<Note>
  **Super Administrator** is a reserved role. You can't assign it from **Settings → Manage Users**.
</Note>

On the Team plan or higher, Administrators can change what each role can do from **Settings → Permissions**. See [User roles](/team/user-roles) and [Customising permissions](/team/customising-permissions).

## Seats

Your plan includes a number of purchased seats. Every active user takes one seat. When all your seats are in use, you see a **Seat limit reached** message and can't invite anyone else until you free up or add a seat.

- **Add seats.** Administrators can increase the seat count from **Settings → Plan**. See [Adding or removing seats](/billing/adding-removing-seats).
- **Deactivate someone who no longer needs access.** Deactivating a user frees their seat for someone new. See [Deactivating and reactivating users](/team/deactivating-reactivating).

<Warning>
  Deactivating a user frees a seat, but it doesn't lower your bill. You pay for the number of seats you've purchased, not the number of active users. To pay for fewer seats, reduce your seat count on **Settings → Plan**.
</Warning>
