Getting your teammates into Connekt takes less than two minutes per person. You send each invite individually, and everyone receives an email with their login link and a temporary password they'll be prompted to change on first login.

## How to Invite a Team Member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Users">
    Go to **Settings → Manage Users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite User">
    Select **Invite User** to open the invite form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Type the person's email address and set a temporary password for them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a role">
    Choose the role that matches their responsibilities. See the role descriptions below if you're not sure which to pick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Click **Send Invite**. The team member will receive an email with their login link and temporary password right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Invites are sent one person at a time. Repeat the steps above for each additional teammate you want to add.
</Note>

## Available Roles

Choose the role that best fits each person's responsibilities. You can update roles later from **Settings → Manage Users**.

| Role | What They Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Super Administrator** | Full access to everything, including billing and workspace-level settings |
| **Administrator** | Full feature access across the entire workspace |
| **Operations** | Manages users, leave administration, and system configuration |
| **Manager** | Manages their team, approves leave requests, and has full CRM access within their scope |
| **Principal Consultant** | Senior CRM access with broad visibility across the business |
| **Senior Consultant** | Full access to create and edit candidates, jobs, and placements |
| **Consultant** | Core CRM access focused on their own candidates, jobs, and placements |
| **Recruiter** | Core CRM access focused on sourcing and submissions |
| **Researcher** | Candidate sourcing access with limited placement rights |
| **User** | Basic, largely read-only access across the CRM |

## Seat Limits

The number of users you can invite depends on your plan. If you've reached your seat limit, you have two options before you can send more invites:

- **Add more seats** — upgrade your seat count from **Settings → Billing & Usage**
- **Remove inactive users** — deactivate users who no longer need access to free up seats

<Tip>
  Only active users count toward your seat limit. Deactivating a user immediately frees up their seat, so you can invite someone new without increasing your plan cost.
</Tip>
