Connekt is a cloud-based recruitment CRM that keeps the whole recruitment lifecycle in one place, from sourcing candidates to recording placements. Your team works in one shared workspace, so pipelines, people and paperwork stay in sync.

## Who Connekt is for

- **Recruitment agencies** managing several clients and active pipelines
- **In-house talent teams** handling internal hiring
- **Independent recruiters and consultants** who want a complete CRM without the setup

## Where you can use Connekt

Connekt runs in any modern web browser, so there's nothing to install to get started. Everyone on your team shares the same live workspace, and you always have the latest version.

You can also add these optional apps:

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Connekt mobile app** | Look up records, record in-person meetings, sign documents and search with AI on the go. Available on the App Store for iOS. The Android app is coming soon. |
| **Connekt Chrome extension** | Save LinkedIn profiles to Connekt as candidates or contacts in one click. |
| **Connekt Notetaker** | A separate Chrome extension that records and transcribes your online meetings. |

<Tip>
  You'll find download links for both Chrome extensions in **Settings → Downloads & Guides**. See [Chrome extension](/integrations/chrome-extension), [Recording meetings](/meetings/recording-meetings) and [Using the mobile app](/mobile/using-the-mobile-app) for setup steps.
</Tip>
