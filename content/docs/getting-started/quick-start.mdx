title: "Connekt quick start: your first placement in 5 steps"
sidebarTitle: "Quick start"
description: "Follow this five-step walkthrough to record your first hire in Connekt, from adding a company to creating a placement."
---

The fastest way to learn Connekt is to follow one real workflow from start to finish. This guide takes you from an empty workspace to a recorded placement in five steps. You can start simple and add more detail later.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a company">
    A company is the business that's hiring. Go to **Companies**, click **Add Company**, enter the company name and click **Create Company**.

    <Tip>
      A company name is enough to get started. You can fill in the rest of the record later.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a job">
    A job is the open role you're recruiting for. Go to **Jobs** and click **Add Job**. Add the job title and link it to the company, then save.

    See [Creating a job](/jobs/creating-a-job) for other ways to create a job.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a candidate">
    A candidate is the person you're placing. Go to **Candidates**, click **Add Candidate** and enter their details.

    If you have their resume, open the add menu and choose **Upload / Parse Resume** instead. Connekt creates the candidate record from the resume for you. See [Adding a candidate](/candidates/adding-a-candidate).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit the candidate to the job">
    Open the job, go to the **Submissions** tab and click **Submit Candidate**. Choose the candidate you just added and fill in the submission details.

    You can now track the candidate's progress through each stage, from submission to offer. See [Submitting a candidate](/submissions/submitting-a-candidate).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a placement">
    When the submission reaches **Offer Accepted** or **Hired**, click **Create Placement** on the submission in the job's **Submissions** tab. Enter the placement details, such as the start date, fee and splits, and save.

    Your first hire is now recorded in Connekt. See [Converting to a placement](/submissions/converting-to-placement).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  You don't have to finish all five steps in one go. Everything saves as you work, so you can add a company today and create jobs tomorrow.
</Info>
