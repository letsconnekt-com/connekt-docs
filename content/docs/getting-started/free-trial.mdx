Every new Connekt workspace starts with a free trial. The standard trial lasts 14 days, and you don't need a credit card to start.

## What's included

Your trial includes the features of the plan you choose during onboarding: Pro, Team or Business. For example, a Team trial includes Time Off, custom permissions and the Audit Log, but a Pro trial doesn't. See [Plans compared](/billing/plans-compared) for what each plan includes.

During your trial, you can:

- Add candidates, companies, contacts, jobs and placements
- Try every feature in your chosen plan

<Note>
  A trial workspace has one seat, for you. To invite teammates, subscribe and add a seat for each person. See [Subscribe before your trial ends](#subscribe-before-your-trial-ends).
</Note>

<Tip>
  Choose the plan you expect to buy. That way you try the same features your team will use after you subscribe.
</Tip>

## Check how long is left

Administrators can see the time remaining on **Settings → Plan**. The plan card shows **Trial ends in** followed by the number of days left.

In the last 3 days of your trial, Connekt emails the workspace owners and admins a daily reminder.

## Subscribe before your trial ends

You can subscribe at any time during your trial. Only Administrators can subscribe.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Plan">
    Go to **Settings → Plan** and find the **Choose your plan** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a billing interval">
    Select **Monthly** or **Annually**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your seats">
    Set the number of seats. You need one seat for every person who should have access, including yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your plan">
    Click **Start Pro**, **Start Team** or **Start Business**. Review the total, then click **Continue to payment** and complete checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your workspace and everything in it carry straight over to your paid plan. Once you've subscribed, you can invite teammates. See [Inviting your team](/getting-started/inviting-your-team).

## When your trial ends

When the trial ends, your workspace must subscribe to a paid plan to keep using Connekt. If you haven't subscribed, the workspace becomes inactive and you lose access. Connekt emails the workspace owners and admins when this happens.

To restore access, an Administrator goes to **Settings → Plan**. They choose a plan and seats in the **Restore access** card, then click **Subscribe to** followed by the plan name.

<Info>
  Your data isn't deleted when your trial ends. Everything you created during the trial is preserved and ready when you subscribe.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Subscribe before your trial ends to avoid interruption. Once the workspace becomes inactive, you can't sign in and work until an Administrator subscribes.
</Warning>
