If you're stuck, you can chat with the Connekt team in the app, email us, or ask the docs a question with Ask AI. This page explains each option and what to include in your request.

## Chat with us in the app

In-app chat is the fastest way to reach the Connekt team.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Web app">
    Click the chat bubble in the bottom corner of any page in Connekt. Type your question and send it. You're already signed in, so we can see who you are and which workspace you're in.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mobile app">
    Tap **More**, then tap **Help & Support**. The chat opens inside the app.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Email us

You can also email the Connekt team at [support@letsconnekt.com](mailto:support@letsconnekt.com). Email works well for requests that need attachments or longer explanations, or when you can't sign in.

## Ask AI in the docs

For quick "how do I…" questions, click **Ask AI** at the bottom of any page in this help centre. Ask AI answers from these docs and links you to the pages it used.

<Note>
  Ask AI can only see the help centre. It can't see your workspace, your data or your account. For anything specific to your account, contact us through chat or email.
</Note>

## What to include in your request

The more detail you give us, the faster we can help. Include:

- **What you were trying to do.** For example, "Send a document for signature".
- **What happened instead.** Copy the exact error message if you saw one.
- **Where it happened.** Tell us the page or record, and whether you were on the web app, the mobile app or a Chrome extension.
- **Steps to reproduce it.** List what you clicked, in order.
- **When it started.** Tell us whether it happens every time or only sometimes.
- **A screenshot or screen recording**, if you can. Email is best for attachments.

<Warning>
  Never send your password or two-factor authentication codes to anyone, including the Connekt team. We'll never ask for them.
</Warning>
