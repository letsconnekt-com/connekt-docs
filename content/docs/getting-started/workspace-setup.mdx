Connekt is invite-only. You get a personal signup link, usually after a demo, instead of signing up from a public page. Setup takes a few minutes, and you don't need a credit card.

## Create your workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invite link">
    Click the signup link you received. The **Create your workspace** form opens with your email address already filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Fill in **Company name**, **First name**, **Last name** and **Password**. Your password must be at least 8 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the workspace">
    Complete the human verification check and click **Create workspace**. Your free trial starts now.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Invite links work once. If you see **This link is no longer valid**, the link is missing, expired or already used. Ask the person who sent it for a new one, or see [Getting help](/getting-started/getting-help).
</Note>

## Complete onboarding

After you create your workspace, a short onboarding flow walks you through three steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your plan">
    Answer **How large is your team?**, choose **Monthly** or **Annual**, and pick a plan. Click **Start free trial**. Your trial includes the features of the plan you choose. See [Plans compared](/billing/plans-compared) and [Free trial](/getting-started/free-trial).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your workspace">
    Select your **Industry**, then click **Next →**. Your company name comes from the signup form. You can click **Skip** to move on without choosing an industry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Enter your teammates' email addresses. If your workspace already has teams, you can add them to a **Team** and set their **Team role**. Click **Done — go to dashboard**, or click **Skip** to invite people later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you finish, you land on **Home**, your dashboard.

<Note>
  The invite step only appears when your workspace has seats available for teammates. During the free trial your workspace has one seat, for you, so this step is skipped. Subscribe and add seats to invite people from **Settings → Manage Users**. See [Inviting your team](/getting-started/inviting-your-team).
</Note>

## Things to know

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="You're the workspace Administrator">
    The person who creates the workspace gets the **Administrator** role. Administrators have full access to every feature and setting, including billing on **Settings → Plan**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Changing your workspace name or industry">
    You can't edit the company name or industry from **Settings** after setup. To change them, contact the Connekt team. See [Getting help](/getting-started/getting-help).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Teammates you invite during onboarding">
    Teammates you invite during onboarding join with the **User** role. You can change their role at any time from **Settings → Manage Users**. See [User roles](/team/user-roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
