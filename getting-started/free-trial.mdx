Every new Connekt workspace starts with a free trial — no credit card required. Your exact trial length is confirmed at signup, but you can expect 14 days of full access to explore everything Connekt has to offer before choosing a paid plan.

## What's Included in Your Trial

Your trial gives you access to the complete Connekt feature set — there are no locked features or watered-down functionality during this period:

## What Happens When Your Trial Ends

When your 14-day trial is coming to a close, Connekt sends you reminder emails in the days leading up to expiry so you're not caught off guard.

At expiry, you'll be prompted to choose a paid plan. If you don't upgrade, your workspace becomes inactive and your team will lose access until you subscribe.

<Warning>
  Don't wait until the last day to choose a plan — if your workspace becomes inactive, your team immediately loses access. Pick a plan before your trial expiry date to keep things running without interruption. See [Plans Compared](/billing/plans-compared) for your options.
</Warning>

## Your Data Is Always Safe

All the candidates, jobs, placements, and documents you created during your trial are **fully preserved** when you upgrade to a paid plan. Nothing is deleted when your trial ends — your data simply waits for you until you're ready to continue.

<Info>
  Even if your workspace becomes inactive after trial expiry, your data remains intact. You can reactivate at any time by choosing a plan, and everything will be exactly as you left it.
</Info>
