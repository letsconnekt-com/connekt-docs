Connekt is currently invite-only, which means you'll receive a personalised signup link — for example, after booking a demo — rather than signing up from a public page. Once you follow that link, getting your workspace ready takes just a few minutes.

## Setting Up Your Workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your signup link">
    Click the invite link you received. Connekt pre-fills your workspace name based on your company name — you can edit it before moving on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the signup form">
    Fill in the remaining details to create your account. No credit card is required at this stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your free trial starts automatically">
    As soon as you complete signup, a 14-day free trial begins. You don't need to do anything to activate it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on your dashboard">
    You'll arrive on the Connekt dashboard, ready to start adding data. A short onboarding flow — including setting your industry — walks you through the basics. You can skip it at any point and go straight to the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Things to Know Before You Start

<Accordion title="Your workspace URL is permanent">
  Your workspace URL is set at signup and **cannot be changed later**. If you ever need it updated, contact Connekt support directly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="You're the workspace administrator">
  The person who completes signup is automatically granted full administrator access. This gives you control over users, settings, and billing from day one.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Updating workspace details after signup">
  To change your workspace name, industry, or other workspace-level settings after you've signed up, contact Connekt support. These settings aren't editable directly from the dashboard at this time.
</Accordion>

<Note>
  If you're setting up Connekt on behalf of your team, take a moment to confirm your workspace name and URL look right before you complete signup — the URL in particular can't be edited once it's set.
</Note>
