Connekt is a cloud-based recruitment CRM that gives you one place to manage the full recruitment lifecycle — from sourcing candidates all the way through to placed hires. Whether you're running a busy staffing agency or leading an in-house talent team, Connekt keeps your pipelines, people, and paperwork in one shared workspace.

## Who Is Connekt For?

Connekt is designed to fit a range of recruitment contexts:

- **Recruitment agencies** managing multiple clients and active pipelines
- **In-house HR and talent acquisition teams** handling internal hiring
- **Individual recruiters, consultants, and hiring managers** working independently

## How Connekt Is Delivered

Connekt is fully cloud-based — there's no software to download or install. You can log in from any modern browser, on any device, and every member of your team shares the same live workspace the moment they sign in.

A companion mobile app is available for both iOS and Android, so you can keep working on the go. Ask your workspace admin for access details if you need to get set up on mobile.

<Tip>
  Because Connekt is cloud-based, you'll always have the latest version automatically. No updates to manage, no version mismatches between teammates.
</Tip>
