The Connekt Chrome extension lets you add and enrich records in your workspace without leaving LinkedIn. As you browse profiles and company pages, a side panel appears so you can capture data with a single click — complete with automatic duplicate detection to keep your database clean. It works for candidates, companies, and contacts all in one place.

<Note>
  The extension is currently distributed as an **unpacked installation** rather than through the Chrome Web Store. Ask your workspace admin for the install package and setup instructions.
</Note>

## What the extension captures

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="LinkedIn Profiles" icon="user">
    Creates new or enriches existing **Candidate** records from individual LinkedIn profiles.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Company Pages" icon="building">
    Creates new or enriches existing **Company** records from LinkedIn company pages.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Team Members" icon="users">
    Captures individuals listed on a company page as **Contact** records.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to capture a record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install and sign in">
    Install the extension using the package from your admin, then sign in with your Connekt credentials — the same email and password you use on the web app, including MFA if you have it enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse to a LinkedIn profile or company page">
    Navigate to any LinkedIn candidate profile, company page, or team member listing you want to capture.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the extension panel">
    A floating indicator appears on the page. Click it to open the Connekt side panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the pre-filled details">
    The panel automatically detects whether you're viewing a candidate, contact, or company and pre-fills a form with data scraped from the page. Review the details and make any corrections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check for duplicates">
    Connekt checks your existing records for likely matches and flags any duplicates before you save — helping you avoid creating the same person or company twice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the record">
    Click **Save** to create a new record or update an existing one in your Connekt workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Other things the extension can do

Beyond capturing LinkedIn data, the extension's side panel gives you quick access to several other actions:

<Accordion title="Upload and parse a resume">
  Upload a resume file directly from the side panel and Connekt will automatically parse the candidate's information using AI, populating the candidate record for you.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Attach a Notetaker meeting summary">
  If you've recently run a Connekt Notetaker meeting with or about this person, you can attach the meeting summary to the record directly from the extension.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Search your Connekt workspace">
  Use the **Status** tab in the extension to search across your Candidates, Contacts, and Companies without navigating away from LinkedIn. Useful for quickly checking if someone is already in your database.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Always let the duplicate detection run before saving. If Connekt flags a match, review it — you may want to update the existing record rather than creating a second one.
</Tip>
