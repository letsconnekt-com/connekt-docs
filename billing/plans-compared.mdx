Connekt offers four plans so you can choose the right level of access as your team and your needs grow. All plans use per-seat pricing, which means you only pay for the active users on your workspace.

## Pricing

Connekt's pricing is based on the number of active users (seats) in your workspace:

- **Per-seat pricing** — you pay only for active users. Deactivated users don't count toward your seat total.
- **Monthly billing** — pay month to month with full flexibility.
- **Annual billing** — pay upfront for a full year and save compared to monthly. The exact discount varies by plan.

<Tip>
  If you're planning to use Connekt long-term, annual billing is the more cost-effective choice. You can switch from monthly to annual at any time — see [Switching Billing Interval](/billing/switching-billing-interval) for steps.
</Tip>
